## Return

The back of the chair reached Xuyuan first.

Interface's fingertip was still resting against the centre of his forehead. It withdrew.

He opened his eyes.

She stood in front of him with her hand lowered between them. For a moment, Xuyuan looked at her shoulders instead of her face.

He pressed both feet against the floor. The permission screen remained lit beyond her. Its expiry time had barely moved.

Xuyuan read it twice, then touched the corner of the reader. The orbital diagram returned. The field handoff was still blank.

Interface waited.
