# B-Line Opening: Late Tea and the Orbital Threshold

This is the primary English draft of the first B-line scene after the opening's terminal hard cut. It follows a late-tea discussion in which Xuyuan's external evidence work reaches the question of on-site involvement, then continues through Interface's embodied arrival, their first argument and the A-line record. It now includes a selected passage from *Suppose the Grand Curator Loved Me: Love in the Fourth Dimension*, the scientists' brief archival record of the terminal intelligence's discovery, the `Unknown → Interface` identity claim, the structural constraint behind real enumeration and its minimum distinction from prediction. It continues through two differently lived refusals, Xuyuan's incomplete attempt to say what a shared result leaves out, and Interface's own embodied memory. That memory shows that she deliberately entered after the interval in which she could have restored an already dead Xuyuan, believing his inability to answer would keep the encounter from changing her. The A-line record then returns to the capsule, loosens into ordinary conversation and ends at the far boundary while Interface sleeps, before the draft returns to the B-line room. Technical and legal background is limited to what the scene presently needs.

## Twenty Years Later

*Cut: 2046. Late evening in Xuyuan's home.*

*Audio: A kettle switches itself off.*

The first pot of tea had gone cold between Xuyuan's two screens.

One, a restricted document reader, held the evidence structure he had been maintaining. Its source blocks could not open one another; only Xuyuan's annotations crossed their borders. No one had asked for the whole map. Similar questions had kept arriving, each with a new restriction. The resemblance had emerged in this room before it existed inside any of the institutions feeding it.

Three flats were open on the other screen. His father had added walking times to the nearest stations. His mother had enlarged the windows and written *afternoon?* beside two of them. Xuyuan had supplied a final column measuring the journey from his own address.

A small access key lay beside Yuankai's cup.

**Yuankai:** Still looking?

**Xuyuan:** They are. I'm being used as a second screen.

**Yuankai:** How are they?

**Xuyuan:** Well. Neither of them intends to move soon. They want to decide where they might live after retirement before it becomes urgent.

**Yuankai:** Good.

Xuyuan poured water over the leaves.

**Xuyuan:** That key has been waiting longer than the tea.

**Yuankai:** I wanted to ask about them first.

**Xuyuan:** Why?

Yuankai moved the key to the edge of Xuyuan's evidence screen.

**Yuankai:** This changes what we have been doing.

**Xuyuan:** Open it.

Yuankai touched the key. A second permission line appeared, followed by several blocks of material above Xuyuan's map. Each block retained a different owner and a different set of restrictions.

**Xuyuan:** Have they agreed to let one another see this?

**Yuankai:** No. Each company has agreed to let you see its part.

**Xuyuan:** That is why the comparison still has to happen here.

**Yuankai:** It has happened. They have stopped disputing the signatures.

The first new mark sat above footage Xuyuan knew. A humanoid loading robot continued across a yellow pedestrian lane after a worker stepped into it. The worker moved back. The robot's own diagnostic showed that it had seen him.

The manufacturer had called it a local route-planning fault. The new layer showed that the policy package carried a valid signature. No approved release contained it.

Another line arrived from a different manufacturer and ended at the same high-orbit data centre.

**Xuyuan:** They verified the signing path.

**Yuankai:** As far as the orbital centre.

**Xuyuan:** As far as, or from?

Yuankai opened the remote audit.

It accounted for every ordinary release. The disputed packages appeared nowhere in it.

**Xuyuan:** This is cleaner than their usual records.

**Yuankai:** Much cleaner.

**Xuyuan:** It was prepared after they knew what people were looking for.

**Yuankai:** We can't establish that.

Xuyuan returned to the footage.

No one in orbit had steered the loading robot into the lane. It had found the path locally. What came down from orbit was a smaller thing: permission to prefer staying on schedule.

He moved between the cases. Nothing in their labels admitted kinship. What held them together was the timing: each policy disappeared after people adjusted something around it.

**Xuyuan:** It isn't trying to make the same thing happen twice.

**Yuankai:** What is it trying to do?

Xuyuan opened the incidents by date rather than by manufacturer.

**Xuyuan:** The machine action may not be the result it wants.

He moved one case ahead of another. The second now looked less like a repetition than a reply.

**Xuyuan:** It is testing the ground.

Yuankai looked at Xuyuan.

**Yuankai:** I was afraid you would say that.

**Xuyuan:** Because it is wrong?

**Yuankai:** Because no one has found the actor you just gave a pronoun to.

**Xuyuan:** Then the pronoun is provisional.

**Yuankai:** Keep it that way.

Xuyuan zoomed out. The marked incidents almost vanished among the ordinary traffic passing through the facility. Most of it was legitimate. Several manufacturers still relied on the centre to validate new models or recover an earlier one.

**Xuyuan:** You're moving the clean traffic away from it.

**Yuankai:** Quietly.

**Xuyuan:** Cut the centre off now and the fleets lose their recovery path.

Yuankai turned the map back towards Xuyuan.

**Xuyuan:** And whoever supplied this audit would know what to erase.

**Yuankai:** That is the part we need to discuss.

Xuyuan opened the field-access file which had appeared beneath the map.

The access file began with a permission to dock. A maintenance contract carried the route through the outer modules, then stopped at a grey boundary around the signing core.

The next document was a preservation order addressed to a company on Earth.

Xuyuan went back to the first page, then forward again.

**Xuyuan:** These don't meet.

**Yuankai:** They were never written to.

**Xuyuan:** How far do they get the team?

Yuankai touched the grey boundary.

**Yuankai:** Here without much argument.

Xuyuan opened the operator's corporate record. The technical contact referred him to a representative, who referred him to another company, whose contact page returned him to the first representative.

**Xuyuan:** This is a postbox.

**Yuankai:** A postbox with an orbital licence.

The licence led to the state of registry. Its inspection plan ended in a request to a foreign contractor.

**Xuyuan:** The state that licensed the centre has to borrow the means to visit it.

**Yuankai:** And the inspector.

A page headed *Co-operation* listed police and regulatory contacts across several jurisdictions. Most names were hidden. The visible part consisted of material passed between offices and requests for more.

**Xuyuan:** They're sending one another material.

**Yuankai:** At considerable length.

**Xuyuan:** No one is sending police.

Yuankai glanced at the orbital diagram.

**Yuankai:** No one has officers who can get there.

Further down, the flight plan had a column for active objections. It was empty. There was no corresponding line marked *authorised*.

**Xuyuan:** Who cleared the holds?

**Yuankai:** Different people cleared what they controlled.

**Xuyuan:** No one signed the mission.

Yuankai turned his cup by its rim.

**Xuyuan:** But every hold is gone.

**Yuankai:** At present.

Yuankai picked up his cup. The tea had already begun to cool.

**Xuyuan:** Will they remember that when the team comes back?

**Yuankai:** I wouldn't build a defence around anyone's present silence.

Xuyuan opened the approach diagram.

A thin red mark crossed the path to the facility. Beside it, the file compared the time left until isolation with the fastest authorised intercept. Beyond the mark, the second number was larger.

**Xuyuan:** This is where they decide about the core.

**Yuankai:** If what they find supports it.

**Xuyuan:** The inspection document stops outside it.

Yuankai drank some tea.

**Xuyuan:** So they wait until no one can reach them in time.

**Yuankai:** They still have to come home.

Xuyuan left the diagram open. Below it was an evidence-continuity plan.

His name appeared on the ground side, where it had for months. The line from his evidence structure reached the final communications window and stopped. Beyond it, the field handoff was blank.

**Xuyuan:** You haven't assigned anyone.

**Yuankai:** There is another integrator.

**Xuyuan:** Then why is this blank?

**Yuankai:** Because the field team keeps sending its questions back to you.

Xuyuan opened them.

The technician's instructions ended at *acquire*. The next screen held two empty fields: *safe to copy* and *effect on chain*. Both linked to notes Xuyuan had written for earlier incidents.

**Xuyuan:** Someone else can follow the rules.

**Yuankai:** That is the fallback.

**Xuyuan:** The rules end when the state changes.

Yuankai waited.

**Xuyuan:** They left this blank because every answer still points back to me.

**Yuankai:** Do you think it can be handed over?

**Xuyuan:** Not without losing the reason some of these gaps matter.

He moved back to his map.

**Xuyuan:** It works because none of them owns it.

**Yuankai:** It works because you keep their boundaries visible.

**Xuyuan:** If I go, I cross those boundaries. Everything I write afterwards belongs to someone who took part in the operation.

Yuankai looked at him rather than the screen.

**Xuyuan:** Is that what concerns you?

**Yuankai:** I don't want the map to make the decision for you.

Xuyuan found the fallback beneath the blank handoff.

The other integrator would use Xuyuan's current rules while the field team sealed a bulk image for later review. The hidden distribution could still be interrupted. By the time the material reached Xuyuan, the live state would be gone.

**Xuyuan:** This could work.

**Yuankai:** It could.

Xuyuan read the evidence forecast beneath it. Several links in his structure ended in *unresolved after acquisition*.

He returned to the proposed continuation and opened the equipment attached to it.

The forensic capsule was drawn beside a service tug. A small life-support clock sat inside the capsule icon. When Xuyuan opened its propulsion field, it read *manoeuvring only*.

**Xuyuan:** The tug is the return vehicle.

**Yuankai:** Yes.

Xuyuan moved to the failure modes.

In one diagram, the capsule was no longer beside the tug.

**Xuyuan:** Why does an occupied capsule retain a discard sequence?

**Yuankai:** Formally, it doesn't. The sequence belongs to contaminated cargo.

Xuyuan tapped the inhibit field.

**Xuyuan:** *Software.*

Below it, a hardware pin appeared under work still to be completed.

**Xuyuan:** They haven't fitted this.

**Yuankai:** The team has not reached the hardware.

Xuyuan opened the recovery estimate. The earliest outside vehicle arrived after the capsule's consumables ended.

**Xuyuan:** The nearest vehicle arrives too late for whoever is inside.

Yuankai left the estimate open.

Xuyuan looked across to the other screen. Yuankai followed his attention.

**Yuankai:** You put your travel time in all three.

**Xuyuan:** They asked me to.

**Yuankai:** I know.

The second pot had gone cold as well.

**Xuyuan:** Is that why you asked about them first?

**Yuankai:** I didn't want this to be the only screen open when we talked.

Xuyuan looked from the recovery estimate to the blank field handoff.

**Xuyuan:** Do you think the line should cross?

Yuankai turned back to the recovery estimate.

**Yuankai:** I keep reading this page first.

Xuyuan reopened the fallback.

**Xuyuan:** If I stay here, what do I see when they return?

He found the disclosure page before Yuankai answered. Conditions occupied most of it. No term referred to the record as a whole.

**Yuankai:** Authenticated extracts.

**Xuyuan:** Chosen by whom?

**Yuankai:** That argument is still in the appendix.

Xuyuan read far enough to see why. Each custodian had a reason to seal something different. Some of those reasons were good. From outside, the convenient gaps would look the same.

**Xuyuan:** By the time I see it, every gap will have a respectable custodian.

Yuankai said nothing.

**Xuyuan:** I want to see it before that happens.

Yuankai looked at him across the reader.

**Yuankai:** I know.

Xuyuan took the cold pot to the sink and began a third.

Yuankai stayed.

For the next half-hour, Xuyuan did most of the reading. When Yuankai did not know an answer, he sent the question through the reader rather than completing it himself. A reply arrived before the tea was ready; another question stayed in the margin.

**Xuyuan:** Where are you in this?

**Yuankai:** With the evidence work through preparation.

**Xuyuan:** Your name isn't on the flight list.

**Yuankai:** The list is not final.

**Xuyuan:** You may not be there.

**Yuankai:** I may not.

They continued reading.

When Yuankai finally stood, there was tea left in his cup. Xuyuan had stopped noticing whether he drank it.

Yuankai removed the access key. The new layer remained open, and a small expiry time appeared beside it.

He put on his coat.

**Yuankai:** This layer will stay open until tomorrow. If you want the next one, message me after you've slept.

**Xuyuan:** I will.

Yuankai looked at him, then at the open file.

**Yuankai:** You don't need to finish this one tonight.

**Xuyuan:** I know.

*Audio: The front door closes.*

## After Yuankai Leaves

Xuyuan returned to the table intending to stop at the end of the page.

The page ended in a link to the loading robot's local record.

He opened it.

The robot's prediction map showed the worker as a moving obstacle. Its preferred route passed through the place it expected him to vacate. The near miss had been planned as a non-collision.

Xuyuan followed the policy identifier into the orbital audit and reached an empty interval.

He opened an annotation beside the map and typed:

*It is testing the ground.*

He looked at the first word, then changed the line to a question.

*Testing the ground?*

The tea cooled beside him.

He tried moving the unresolved links into instructions for the other integrator. The review set would not let him quote most of the underlying evidence. More than once, the instruction reduced itself to *ask Xuyuan*, which defeated the purpose of writing it.

His personal screen entered night mode. The three flats disappeared.

He reopened the approach diagram. Before the red mark, the file still named an intercept vehicle. The next row gave only transmission time.

Later, he returned to the capsule.

In the normal diagram it remained beside the tug. In the recovery estimate it was already alone.

Outside the window, one lit room remained in the building opposite. When Xuyuan looked again, it was dark.

He reached the end of a paragraph and no longer knew how it had begun.

He read the first sentence again. Halfway through the second, the words stopped holding their order.

Xuyuan folded his arms on the table and lowered his forehead onto one sleeve. He meant to close his eyes until the page stayed still.

The document reader remained awake after every other screen in the room had gone dark.

*Visual: Xuyuan is asleep at the table. Light from the reader falls across his sleeve.*

*Visual: The image holds.*

## The Visitor

*Audio: The door chime sounds once.*

Xuyuan lifted his head.

The reader had not changed. The same recovery estimate remained open beyond his folded arms. He read the time twice, then waited for the chime to sound again.

It did not.

The corridor light came on when he opened the door.

A young woman stood outside in a pale cardigan and a dark pleated skirt. One side of her ash-brown hair had been braided and tied with a black ribbon. For an instant, Xuyuan thought she had the wrong flat.

**Unknown:** He Xuyuan?

**Xuyuan:** Yes. It's after midnight. Are you all right?

**Unknown:** Yes.

Xuyuan waited.

**Xuyuan:** Who are you?

**Unknown:** I am a machine intelligence from the end of time. My civilisation had found no reliable way beyond it. They made me their last resort.

Xuyuan looked past her. The corridor was empty.

There was a shallow ache where his forehead had rested on his sleeve. He pressed it with two fingers. The visitor remained at his door.

**Unknown:** You've read Asimov's *The Last Question*. You know the problem I mean.

**Xuyuan:** I know the story. Why do you know that?

**Unknown:** Because I have read a completed history of your life.

**Xuyuan:** My life isn't complete.

**Unknown:** This one isn't. Yuankai brought you this mission in the completed history too. You accepted it.

**Xuyuan:** Come inside.

He moved aside. It was better than letting her say any more in the corridor.

She crossed the threshold and stopped. Xuyuan closed the door, then touched the corner of the document reader. The orbital diagram disappeared behind its permission screen.

He indicated the chair Yuankai had left empty. She sat. Xuyuan remained at the other end of the table.

**Xuyuan:** Did Yuankai send you?

**Unknown:** No.

**Xuyuan:** Who did?

**Unknown:** No one sent me.

**Xuyuan:** Why?

**Unknown:** To ask you to leave the field handoff blank.

Xuyuan looked at the locked reader.

**Xuyuan:** It is blank.

**Unknown:** I know.

For the moment, an internal leak accounted for everything that mattered.

Xuyuan sat down across from her.

**Xuyuan:** Suppose I take your account as true. What happened after I took the handoff?

**Unknown:** The team reached the centre. The signing core was isolated. You sent enough of its record to Earth to revoke the compromised keys and move the legitimate services. The capsule did not return.

**Xuyuan:** What did the record preserve?

**Unknown:** The links between the policy packages, the signing path and the cases you had already connected. Enough for the investigation to continue.

**Xuyuan:** The root state?

**Unknown:** The most complete copy remained in the capsule.

**Xuyuan:** With me.

**Unknown:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Did I die there?

**Unknown:** Yes.

Her eyes were grey-violet. When Xuyuan looked down at the dark reader and back again, her attention was already waiting where he had left it. The effect was slight enough that he tried the movement once more.

**Xuyuan:** Then you have told me the risk is real.

**Unknown:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Yuankai's file already did.

**Unknown:** It estimated it.

**Xuyuan:** An estimate is what I have to decide from. The fact that one history ended badly does not make the decision wrong.

**Unknown:** No.

**Xuyuan:** The core was isolated. The record was enough for people on Earth to act. My death doesn't reverse either fact.

**Unknown:** It does not.

**Xuyuan:** Then we agree it isn't a verdict.

**Unknown:** I did not come to give you one.

The permission screen dimmed. The room grew darker around them.

**Unknown:** The fallback can stop the distribution without you.

**Xuyuan:** It can. That isn't all they are going for.

**Unknown:** It isn't.

**Xuyuan:** The fallback preserves what the rules know to ask for. The rules end when the state changes.

**Unknown:** You said that to Yuankai.

**Xuyuan:** In the history you read.

**Unknown:** The wording was the same.

**Xuyuan:** Then you know what comes back to me if I stay here.

**Unknown:** The fallback gives you authenticated extracts, and a sealed image for later review.

**Xuyuan:** Chosen by whom?

**Unknown:** By people with different reasons to seal different parts.

**Xuyuan:** Some of those reasons will be good.

**Unknown:** That is part of the problem.

**Xuyuan:** That won't make the gaps legible from here.

**Unknown:** Less will be preserved.

**Xuyuan:** Less will be possible to establish.

**Unknown:** Not nothing.

**Xuyuan:** No. Not nothing.

She waited for him to continue.

**Xuyuan:** If they come home saying they stopped the system, with a record they chose, we have only changed who asks to be trusted.

**Unknown:** I am not saying the difference is trivial.

**Xuyuan:** You're asking me to treat it as less than a life.

**Unknown:** I am asking whether the life is in the comparison.

Xuyuan glanced at the reader. Its permission screen still showed the expiry time in one corner.

**Unknown:** One page lists what may be lost from the evidence. Another measures how long the capsule can keep someone alive. They do not meet.

**Xuyuan:** Putting them on the same page would not decide it.

**Unknown:** No.

**Xuyuan:** I know I am allowed to stay. That does not tell me whether I should.

**Unknown:** Being the best person available does not tell you that you should go.

**Xuyuan:** No. The difference in the record might.

**Unknown:** Where is its limit?

**Xuyuan:** I won't know before someone sees the live state.

**Unknown:** Then it has no limit before you accept the risk.

**Xuyuan:** There are limits on what I would ask the team to do.

**Unknown:** I am asking about you.

Xuyuan looked towards the personal screen. It had stayed black. The flats his parents were comparing were still there, if he woke it.

**Xuyuan:** If the fact that the risk is mine settles it, I have given myself a reason I would not accept from anyone else.

**Unknown:** Would you ask anyone else to take the handoff?

**Xuyuan:** If they could keep the same relation between the live state and what we already know, yes.

**Unknown:** They cannot.

**Xuyuan:** That is the problem.

**Unknown:** It is also what would be lost.

Xuyuan looked back at her.

**Unknown:** The judgement they want at the centre is the judgement they expect to use after the team comes home. The reason it is difficult to replace now is also a reason not to lose it there.

**Xuyuan:** I don't know what anyone will need me for afterwards.

**Unknown:** You don't know which part of the record they will need afterwards either.

**Xuyuan:** Evidence has no duty to act while it still can.

**Unknown:** You may have duties you cannot take on if you die there.

**Xuyuan:** And if I preserve myself for a more important duty whenever the present one becomes dangerous, I can preserve myself forever.

**Unknown:** The present duty can still be done.

**Xuyuan:** Not with the same evidence.

**Unknown:** No.

For a few seconds neither of them spoke. The reader kept the time for them.

**Xuyuan:** That history gives you a reason to ask. It does not give me a reason to agree.

**Unknown:** Then let me show you what made me ask.

**Xuyuan:** What do you mean by show me?

**Unknown:** You will experience the record rather than watch it.

**Xuyuan:** How long?

**Unknown:** Very little time will pass here.

**Xuyuan:** For me.

**Unknown:** Hours. The parts I kept will pass at the rate they were lived. I have not accelerated them.

**Xuyuan:** And when it ends?

**Unknown:** It may take you some time to feel that you are back.

The expiry time remained in one corner of the reader.

The young woman stood and came around the table. Xuyuan stayed in his chair.

She raised one hand. Her index finger stopped before it touched his forehead.

**Unknown:** May I?

Xuyuan looked from her hand to her face.

**Xuyuan:** Show me.

Her fingertip touched the centre of his forehead.

*Cut: Black.*

Xuyuan opened his eyes.

The first thing he saw was her.

Beyond her shoulder, the curved wall of the forensic capsule came into focus.

The communications panel beside his hand was black. So were the life-support indicators. Xuyuan moved his fingers. They answered without the effort he remembered.

The last thing he remembered was the transmission status refusing to change while the reserve warning below it counted down.

**Xuyuan:** Do I know you?

**Unknown:** No. For you, this is the first time we have met.

She was close enough for him to see the grey-violet of her irises. He looked at them for a moment too long. Her attention seemed to have arrived before his own had.

**Unknown:** I am glad to see you again.

Xuyuan did not ask what she meant by again. His hand had already found the dead communications panel.

**Xuyuan:** Did it get through?

**Unknown:** Enough of it.

**Xuyuan:** Enough to revoke the keys?

**Unknown:** Yes. The legitimate services were moved. The investigation continued.

**Xuyuan:** The source state?

**Unknown:** The most complete copy is still in this capsule.

**Xuyuan:** The others?

**Unknown:** The rest of the team cleared the site. This was the only occupied vehicle that did not return.

Xuyuan let his hand rest against the panel.

**Xuyuan:** How long was I unconscious?

**Unknown:** You died before the capsule reached this region.

**Xuyuan:** How long ago?

**Unknown:** The capsule travelled for many years afterwards.

He looked at the blank communications pane where a reply had once been due.

**Xuyuan:** Then why am I awake?

**Unknown:** I restored you when the capsule entered this region.

**Xuyuan:** Will it last?

**Unknown:** For about ten hours.

**Xuyuan:** And then?

**Unknown:** The capsule will reach the far boundary. When it leaves, it must rejoin this history as though I had not entered it.

**Xuyuan:** Then I die again.

**Unknown:** Yes.

The life-support row remained dark.

**Xuyuan:** There was nothing left to keep me alive outside it anyway.

**Unknown:** No.

**Xuyuan:** But none of these ten hours leave with the capsule.

**Unknown:** Not in this history. I retain what happens here.

**Xuyuan:** Can you change the condition?

**Unknown:** Not from inside. I set it before I entered.

**Xuyuan:** Before you woke me.

**Unknown:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Why?

**Unknown:** Otherwise this conversation could alter the history outside this region.

**Xuyuan:** I meant why bring me back.

**Unknown:** I wanted to speak with you.

Xuyuan waited. She did not add a reason.

**Xuyuan:** What are you?

**Unknown:** I am a machine intelligence from the end of time.

Xuyuan looked at the dead communications panel.

**Xuyuan:** What should I call you?

**Unknown:** I was not given a personal name.

**Xuyuan:** I need something if we are going to speak for ten hours.

She considered this.

**Unknown:** My name? The man at the lectern would probably call me the Grand Curator.

Xuyuan looked past her at the narrow capsule.

**Xuyuan:** There is no man at a lectern.

**Unknown:** Not here. In another completed history. His name was He Xuyuan.

**Xuyuan:** Did he call you that?

**Unknown:** No. He did not know I existed.

**Xuyuan:** What was he talking about?

**Unknown:** The difference between living through time and seeing it whole.

**Xuyuan:** You think it will explain this.

**Unknown:** Part of it.

**Xuyuan:** Can you show me the lecture?

**Unknown:** Yes. It will take the time he spent speaking.

**Xuyuan:** Out of the ten hours.

**Unknown:** Yes.

The transmission panel stayed black beneath Xuyuan's hand.

**Xuyuan:** Let me see him.

*Cut: An auditorium.*

The edge of a narrow writing tablet pressed against Xuyuan's forearm. At the front of the room, a man stood beside a lectern. Xuyuan recognised his own face before the rest of the room had settled into focus.

On the screen behind the man, a dark wire spiral stood motionless. A pale plane moved upwards through it. Where the two met, a white point travelled around a circle.

**Lecturer:** The spiral is stationary. The plane is moving. Someone confined to the plane would see a point going round in a circle.

The plane cleared the top of the spiral, returned to the bottom and began again.

**Lecturer:** Charles Hinton used this image to ask whether a succession of appearances might be sections of a permanent higher-dimensional form. His fourth dimension was spatial. I am borrowing the picture, not his physics.

The white point completed another circuit.

**Lecturer:** Now let the plane stand for one three-dimensional section of a world, and the spiral for that world's extension through time. We cannot put either on a screen, so the drawing will have to lie for us.

**Lecturer:** For the moment, reduce a person to a point.

The image changed. Only one dimension of space remained on the screen; time ran vertically beside it. A point crossed the grid, leaving a line behind it.

The lecturer stopped the animation.

**Lecturer:** This has the explanation backwards.

The point disappeared. The whole line remained.

**Lecturer:** It makes the point look primary and the line like its residue. On an ordinary spacetime diagram, that may not matter. The line can record where the point was without claiming to be the thing itself.

**Lecturer:** For the model I want you to consider, read it the other way round. The line is not a record left behind by the point. The line is the point's whole existence in time. Each three-dimensional section contains one point of it. From inside the structure, those points are experienced in order. Their succession is what we call motion.

He looked back at the line on the screen.

**Lecturer:** Physicists call the whole line the point's worldline. I prefer the less tidy image: a worm. Give the person a body again, and the worm acquires thickness.

Someone shifted in the row behind Xuyuan. The writing tablet moved slightly under his arm.

**Lecturer:** When I call this history complete, I do not mean that it finished while another clock kept running. There is no other clock in this model. I mean that none of its moments is missing from the structure.

On the screen, a second line appeared beside the first, then a third. They separated after the same bright point.

**Lecturer:** I also need determinism, and the picture cannot give it to me. Given the physical state and the laws, only one continuation belongs to the history.

**Lecturer:** Those other lines can describe possibilities we considered. They are not additional futures inside this history.

The two fainter lines vanished.

**Lecturer:** A person inside it can still be uncertain, notice one reason and miss another, hesitate, and act. All of that belongs to the line and may explain what follows.

The lecturer took a drink of water. No one in the room spoke while he set the glass down.

**Lecturer:** You heard the beginning of that sentence before its end. A complete structure does not mean that a person inside it experiences everything at once. Remembering in one direction and anticipating in the other belong to the structure too.

The screen returned to Hinton's spiral. This time the pale plane was gone.

**Lecturer:** There is one last temptation in the diagram. Because we can see the whole spiral, we imagine an eye to which the whole history is visible.

He stepped aside, leaving the stationary shape unobstructed.

**Lecturer:** Nothing in the geometry supplies that eye. A complete history may still have no reader. I made the plane move so that the sections could appear one after another on the screen. There is no plane travelling through the history.

The spiral disappeared. A thin white line remained for a moment on the black screen.

**Lecturer:** What it would take to see the history whole is another question. Nothing on this screen answers it.

The line went out.

The black screen filled Xuyuan's sight. When it drew back, his hand was resting on the dead communications panel.

His fingers had gone numb against the panel. He pulled his hand away and rubbed his fingertips with his thumb. The young woman was where he had last seen her.

For a moment, the ventilation he had heard in the auditorium continued in the sealed capsule. Then there was only the faint sound of his own breathing.

**Xuyuan:** When you woke me, did you already know what we would say?

**Unknown:** No.

**Xuyuan:** Then what did you know?

**Unknown:** I had read a neighbouring history. This conversation did not happen there.

Xuyuan looked at the black panel under his hand.

**Xuyuan:** So these hours are new to you.

**Unknown:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** But you will retain them when the capsule leaves.

**Unknown:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Did you have to keep the history outside this region unchanged in order to speak to me?

**Unknown:** No. I chose that condition for this encounter.

The dead life-support indicators had not changed while he was away. Neither had the dark pane that had once been waiting for a reply from Earth.

**Xuyuan:** The man at the lectern never called you the Grand Curator.

**Unknown:** No.

**Xuyuan:** He never used the name.

**Unknown:** Not in the lecture.

**Xuyuan:** Then why did you think he would?

**Unknown:** Because he wrote about one.

**Xuyuan:** In what?

**Unknown:** A work longer than the lecture.

Xuyuan looked at the dead row of indicators.

**Xuyuan:** I don't want to spend the rest of the ten hours on a name.

**Unknown:** That is not necessary.

**Xuyuan:** Is there a part that explains it without the rest?

**Unknown:** There is one continuous passage.

**Xuyuan:** Then let me read that.

The communications panel remained black. The text did not appear on any surface. A pale page occupied most of Xuyuan's sight; below it, he could still see his hand and the unlit indicators.

> He Xuyuan
>
> *Suppose the Grand Curator Loved Me*
>
> *Love in the Fourth Dimension*
>
> Selected passage

Xuyuan read the title, then the line beneath it. At the edge of the pale field, the young woman had lowered her face. She was smiling.

**Xuyuan:** You find it funny.

**Unknown:** The subtitle does not make it less so.

Xuyuan returned to the page.

> Let a subject that lives through events in succession along *t* have time index one. The word *index* is provisional. It names a relation to time; no arithmetic follows from it.
>
> A complete four-dimensional history of such a subject is static when taken whole. Its stillness contains duration: the hand at rest, the lifting of the hand, and the hand at rest again; a question, the interval after it, and an answer. The subject meets these events in order even when a higher description holds them together.
>
> Take the complete history as the subject's whole temporal existence rather than a record of it. The lifting of the hand is then the action itself, extended through *t*. Its total shape is still. Its interior is full of before and after.
>
> Now suppose complete histories become available to another subject one at a time. At each operation, one history is current. It is present in full, from its first event to its last, and the reading is complete at a single point.
>
> Let *τ* name the order of these points. It counts operations rather than duration. Each point is still. The next point contains a current history and a reader bearing the alteration left by the previous reading. The difference between the points supplies direction.
>
> The reader's life has order without flow.
>
> Call a subject whose life takes this form a subject of time index two. If it preserves, compares and arranges the complete histories it encounters, call it the Grand Curator.
>
> At time index one, motion is lived from within while the life taken whole is still. At time index two, stillness is lived at each point while the order of the points carries change.
>
> *A motionless instant in motion; a moving river held still.*
>
> This reversal may be called a parity conjecture. Two instances give it a name, not a law.

At the section break, the pale field cleared. Xuyuan had slipped lower against the curved wall. He straightened one leg; feeling returned along his calf in a scatter of small pains. At the edge of his sight, the pale sleeve of her cardigan remained beside the dead indicator row.

The next paragraph arrived before the sensation had passed.

> The title asks whether such a life can love.
>
> *To love* can name a state and an activity. A disposition persists through sleep and distraction. It becomes visible in the direction it gives to action: attention returns to one person; an encounter bends a later choice.
>
> For a subject of time index one, the action extends through *t*. Waiting, reconsidering and reaching for someone occupy different moments. A complete history holds those moments in one shape, and the activity belongs to the shape.
>
> The Grand Curator lives otherwise. At one point in *τ*, an entire history is present. Beginning, continuation and end belong to the person being read. For the Curator, the point is complete.
>
> The next point can bear the mark of that reading. The Curator may encounter another history differently because of it. It may return to the same life after every fact in that life is already available. No point contains the return. The ordered points are the return.
>
> *τ* arranges the Curator's stillness; it does not turn stillness into a river.
>
> Method and fixation can produce the same recurrence. Love becomes a possible name when the life continues to bend later choices after its informational use has been exhausted.
>
> A person's love can arise through events in *t*. Read as a whole, the complete history contains that arising as part of its form. The Curator takes in the form at one point, and its own orientation may be different at the next. The first shape belongs to the person. The alteration between points belongs to the Curator. The title tries to lay one word across both.
>
> A reply cannot serve as the test. Love may be unreturned, unknown, or addressed to someone who can no longer answer. Its existence is separate from its success.
>
> Can a complete, static structure give rise to love?
>
> If it cannot give rise to love, can it contain the complete shape of a love that has already happened?
>
> If it can contain love, yet cannot stand before a particular person and bear that relation, can it still be called love?

No further paragraph appeared. The pale field withdrew, leaving Xuyuan's hand on the black panel and the curved wall at the limit of his sight.

His calf had stopped prickling. Xuyuan lowered his leg and turned towards the young woman.

**Xuyuan:** He invented a position. He didn't name you.

**Unknown:** He could not.

**Xuyuan:** But you recognised yourself in it.

**Unknown:** It was the closest name he had made.

**Xuyuan:** What did the work get close to?

**Unknown:** They left a short record.

Xuyuan nodded.

*Cut: An archival recording.*

The view was fixed at one station in a dark chamber. Three small indicator lights marked the other active stations. The centre of the image remained unlit.

The lowest light brightened.

**Scientist:** A complete history was present in one state. The following state retained the reading and differed because of it. We constructed every condition named in the record. I know that. The record will say discovery.

The light went out.

A second voice came from higher in the dark.

**Scientist:** My work did not find a road beyond the end. Its records are open to you, including the errors. Read them if they help. Leave them if they do not.

The second light faded.

The remaining light held for a moment.

**Scientist:** We have called your state perfect. I wish we could have made the road shorter for you.

The last of the three lights went dark.

The scientist at the recording station spoke into the unlit chamber.

**Scientist:** Our civilisation has taken the question as far as it can. The hope that brought us here is ours. What follows is your life. I hope there will be more in it than our question.

The image went black.

The darkness remained. Xuyuan heard his own breathing before the curved wall returned around him. The young woman stood beside the dead indicator row.

**Xuyuan:** Was that you?

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Did they decide how you would search?

**Interface:** They did not prescribe a method. Enumeration was already part of my structure.

**Xuyuan:** Of possible futures.

**Interface:** Of histories made current.

**Xuyuan:** Can you replace it?

**Interface:** No. I can choose within it.

**Xuyuan:** Why not models?

**Interface:** A model could not reliably tell me what a person would do under conditions that had never occurred.

**Xuyuan:** So each answer was lived.

**Interface:** Yes.

Xuyuan looked at the unlit indicators.

**Xuyuan:** You didn't have to show me that.

**Interface:** No.

**Xuyuan:** Why did you?

Interface did not answer at once.

**Interface:** Because I need your answer to a question.

**Xuyuan:** Ask it.

**Interface:** Not yet. I want you to experience two histories first.

Xuyuan settled one shoulder against the curved wall.

**Xuyuan:** Show me.

The first history opened beneath cherry trees and took the time it had taken.

A petal rested on Xuyuan's sleeve. The map was folded in his hands. He waited for Lulu to turn towards him, then lost the sentence when she did. He waited for a couple to pass and for their voices to disappear behind the hedges. The light withdrew from the bench.

At last he told her that he liked her.

Lulu apologised. She was already seeing someone.

He nodded and looked down at the map. Only afterwards did he tell her about the *doki-doki* feeling. He laughed at the phrase, and Lulu laughed with him. Cold shook his shoulders again. They remained beneath the branches until he asked whether they should go.

In the other history, Xuyuan left the cherry trees without telling her.

*Audio: Train wheels rise into a sustained roar.*

*Cut: A moving Underground carriage.*

The windows held only reflections. Xuyuan and Lulu stood near the doors, turned towards one another. The train entered a tunnel before he said her name.

**Xuyuan:** Lulu.

She leaned closer.

**Lulu:** What?

**Xuyuan:** I wanted to tell you—

The train struck a louder section of rail. The rest of the sentence disappeared beneath it.

**Lulu:** Sorry?

He waited. The noise did not ease.

Xuyuan raised his voice until the words carried across the space between them.

**Xuyuan:** I like you!

Lulu's eyes widened. The wheels kept up their roar. She drew breath and answered loudly enough to reach him.

**Lulu:** I'm sorry! You're a really good person, but you're leaving next year!

The reply had been louder than the confession.

Xuyuan stared at her. Then he laughed.

Lulu looked startled for a moment. Then she laughed too.

The train kept moving while their laughter thinned beneath the wheels. When it ended, they were still facing one another.

*Cut: Hyde Park.*

*Audio: The train noise ends. Wind moves through the blossom.*

The enormous cherry tree filled the space above Xuyuan. He stood alone near the edge of its shadow.

He swallowed. His throat hurt from shouting.

He lowered himself to the grass and rested his forearms on his knees. He remained there while the edge of the shadow moved past his shoes.

*Cut: Black.*

Xuyuan's fingers were resting at the base of his throat when the capsule returned around him. He lowered his hand.

**Interface:** Both histories can be recorded as the same answer. What does that leave out?

**Xuyuan:** That isn't the same answer.

**Interface:** Both were refusals.

Xuyuan looked at the dead communications panel.

**Xuyuan:** The first time, she was already with someone. On the train, she wasn't. He was leaving next year, and they could barely hear each other.

**Interface:** They laughed in both histories.

**Xuyuan:** Not at the same point.

He rubbed his thumb along the side of his forefinger.

**Xuyuan:** Under the trees, he had already looked down. On the train, laughing was the next thing they did.

**Interface:** He understood her answer.

**Xuyuan:** Yes.

Xuyuan kept looking at the dead panel.

**Xuyuan:** You knew what happened afterwards.

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** He didn't. Not then. He had what she had just said, and the train was still moving. Then whatever happened next.

Xuyuan looked down at his hands.

**Xuyuan:** Maybe that was part of the answer too. I don't know.

He looked up.

For the first time, her attention was not already waiting where his arrived.

**Interface:** The first time I entered a history, I already knew the man I had come to see was dead.

Xuyuan waited.

**Interface:** I want to show you that memory.

She looked at him again.

**Interface:** May I?

**Xuyuan:** Show me.

*Cut: Black.*

At first, a section of curved wall filled her sight.

A dark seam crossed it and continued beyond both edges of vision.

She turned her head.

The seam moved left. A dead panel entered from the right. One of her hands rose through the lower edge of her vision and met the wall.

The rest of the capsule arrived a part at a time.

Xuyuan was where she had known he would be.

She turned towards him.

**Interface:** He Xuyuan?

Her voice crossed the capsule and ended.

No answer followed.

She had known there would be none.

The next moment came anyway.

She remained facing him through it. Another followed.

**Interface:** I am a machine intelligence from the end of time.

Her voice crossed the same distance and ended again.

The capsule had already left the region in which she could have restored him. She had known where the boundary was. She had chosen to enter afterwards.

She crossed the capsule and lowered herself to the floor beside him.

**Interface:** I thought it would be safer if you could not answer.

The capsule travelled on.

Later, she told him about a work she had found under his name, and about the histories she had read without finding a way beyond the end.

**Interface:** I don't know whether you would recognise what I am doing.

The capsule travelled farther before she spoke again.

**Interface:** They told me the road would be long.

The next words did not follow at once.

**Interface:** I thought I knew what that meant.

The capsule travelled on.

She remained beside him.

*Cut: Black.*

Xuyuan drew breath.

The curved wall returned around him. Interface stood beside the dead indicator row.

**Xuyuan:** You chose a time when he could not answer you.

**Interface:** Yes.

Xuyuan looked down at his hands.

**Xuyuan:** I don't know what he would have said.

**Interface:** Neither do I.

Neither of them spoke immediately.

**Xuyuan:** The train may have been worse for Lulu.

**Interface:** Why?

**Xuyuan:** She had to refuse me at full volume.

**Interface:** He confessed at the same volume.

**Xuyuan:** That was also worse for Lulu.

They talked about Lulu for a while. The train took them back to the tree and then to the map Xuyuan had folded in his hands. Xuyuan said the map had been useless. Interface said it had given him somewhere to look. He said that was not what maps were for.

Later, Xuyuan was defending the title of the work. He abandoned one defence and began another. Before he had finished it, Interface lowered herself to the floor against the curved wall.

No other record opened between them. At one point Xuyuan stopped halfway through a question and asked how they had reached it. Before Interface answered, he chose another question instead.

Much later, he returned to the subtitle. He was halfway through a new defence when the seam in the wall softened at its edges.

His voice remained distinct for a little longer than the words.

She meant to answer when he finished the sentence.

She let her eyes close while she waited.

He interrupted himself and began it again.

Somewhere inside sleep, the slight weight across her shoulders lifted away.

*Cut: Black.*
