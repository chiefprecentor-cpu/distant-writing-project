# B-Line Opening: Late Tea and the Orbital Threshold

This is the primary English draft of the first B-line scene after the opening's terminal hard cut. It follows a late-tea discussion in which Xuyuan's external evidence work reaches the question of on-site involvement, then continues through Interface's embodied arrival and their first argument. It currently stops at the first image of the A-line record, after Xuyuan consents to receive it as experience. Technical and legal background is limited to what the scene presently needs.

## Twenty Years Later

*Cut: 2046. Late evening in Xuyuan's home.*

*Audio: A kettle switches itself off.*

The first pot of tea had gone cold between Xuyuan's two screens.

One, a restricted document reader, held the evidence structure he had been maintaining. Its source blocks could not open one another; only Xuyuan's annotations crossed their borders. No one had asked for the whole map. Similar questions had kept arriving, each with a new restriction. The resemblance had emerged in this room before it existed inside any of the institutions feeding it.

Three flats were open on the other screen. His father had added walking times to the nearest stations. His mother had enlarged the windows and written *afternoon?* beside two of them. Xuyuan had supplied a final column measuring the journey from his own address.

A small access key lay beside Yuankai's cup.

**Yuankai:** Still looking?

**Xuyuan:** They are. I'm being used as a second screen.

**Yuankai:** How are they?

**Xuyuan:** Well. Neither of them intends to move soon. They want to decide where they might live after retirement before it becomes urgent.

**Yuankai:** Good.

Xuyuan poured water over the leaves.

**Xuyuan:** That key has been waiting longer than the tea.

**Yuankai:** I wanted to ask about them first.

**Xuyuan:** Why?

Yuankai moved the key to the edge of Xuyuan's evidence screen.

**Yuankai:** This changes what we have been doing.

**Xuyuan:** Open it.

Yuankai touched the key. A second permission line appeared, followed by several blocks of material above Xuyuan's map. Each block retained a different owner and a different set of restrictions.

**Xuyuan:** Have they agreed to let one another see this?

**Yuankai:** No. Each company has agreed to let you see its part.

**Xuyuan:** That is why the comparison still has to happen here.

**Yuankai:** It has happened. They have stopped disputing the signatures.

The first new mark sat above footage Xuyuan knew. A humanoid loading robot continued across a yellow pedestrian lane after a worker stepped into it. The worker moved back. The robot's own diagnostic showed that it had seen him.

The manufacturer had called it a local route-planning fault. The new layer showed that the policy package carried a valid signature. No approved release contained it.

Another line arrived from a different manufacturer and ended at the same high-orbit data centre.

**Xuyuan:** They verified the signing path.

**Yuankai:** As far as the orbital centre.

**Xuyuan:** As far as, or from?

Yuankai opened the remote audit.

It accounted for every ordinary release. The disputed packages appeared nowhere in it.

**Xuyuan:** This is cleaner than their usual records.

**Yuankai:** Much cleaner.

**Xuyuan:** It was prepared after they knew what people were looking for.

**Yuankai:** We can't establish that.

Xuyuan returned to the footage.

No one in orbit had steered the loading robot into the lane. It had found the path locally. What came down from orbit was a smaller thing: permission to prefer keeping time.

He moved between the cases. Nothing in their labels admitted kinship. What held them together was the timing: each policy disappeared after people adjusted something around it.

**Xuyuan:** It isn't trying to make the same thing happen twice.

**Yuankai:** What is it trying to do?

Xuyuan opened the incidents by date rather than by manufacturer.

**Xuyuan:** The machine action may not be the result it wants.

He moved one case ahead of another. The second now looked less like a repetition than a reply.

**Xuyuan:** It is testing the ground.

Yuankai looked at Xuyuan.

**Yuankai:** I was afraid you would say that.

**Xuyuan:** Because it is wrong?

**Yuankai:** Because no one has found the actor you just gave a pronoun to.

**Xuyuan:** Then the pronoun is provisional.

**Yuankai:** Keep it that way.

Xuyuan zoomed out. The marked incidents almost vanished among the ordinary traffic passing through the facility. Most of it was legitimate. Several manufacturers still relied on the centre to validate new models or recover an earlier one.

**Xuyuan:** You're moving the clean traffic away from it.

**Yuankai:** Quietly.

**Xuyuan:** Cut the centre off now and the fleets lose their recovery path.

Yuankai turned the map back towards Xuyuan.

**Xuyuan:** And whoever supplied this audit would know what to erase.

**Yuankai:** That is the part we need to discuss.

Xuyuan opened the field-access file which had appeared beneath the map.

The access file began with a permission to dock. A maintenance contract carried the route through the outer modules, then stopped at a grey boundary around the signing core.

The next document was a preservation order addressed to a company on Earth.

Xuyuan went back to the first page, then forward again.

**Xuyuan:** These don't meet.

**Yuankai:** They were never written to.

**Xuyuan:** How far do they get the team?

Yuankai touched the grey boundary.

**Yuankai:** Here without much argument.

Xuyuan opened the operator's corporate record. The technical contact referred him to a representative, who referred him to another company, whose contact page returned him to the first representative.

**Xuyuan:** This is a postbox.

**Yuankai:** A postbox with an orbital licence.

The licence led to the state of registry. Its inspection plan ended in a request to a foreign contractor.

**Xuyuan:** The state that licensed the centre has to borrow the means to visit it.

**Yuankai:** And the inspector.

A page headed *Co-operation* listed police and regulatory contacts across several jurisdictions. Most names were hidden. The visible part consisted of material passed between offices and requests for more.

**Xuyuan:** They're sending one another material.

**Yuankai:** At considerable length.

**Xuyuan:** No one is sending police.

Yuankai glanced at the orbital diagram.

**Yuankai:** No one has officers who can get there.

Further down, the flight plan had a column for active objections. It was empty. There was no corresponding line marked *authorised*.

**Xuyuan:** Who cleared the holds?

**Yuankai:** Different people cleared what they controlled.

**Xuyuan:** No one signed the mission.

Yuankai turned his cup by its rim.

**Xuyuan:** But every hold is gone.

**Yuankai:** At present.

Yuankai picked up his cup. The tea had already begun to cool.

**Xuyuan:** Will they remember that when the team comes back?

**Yuankai:** I wouldn't build a defence around anyone's present silence.

Xuyuan opened the approach diagram.

A thin red mark crossed the path to the facility. Beside it, the file compared the time left until isolation with the fastest authorised intercept. Beyond the mark, the second number was larger.

**Xuyuan:** This is where they decide about the core.

**Yuankai:** If what they find supports it.

**Xuyuan:** The inspection document stops outside it.

Yuankai drank some tea.

**Xuyuan:** So they wait until no one can reach them in time.

**Yuankai:** They still have to come home.

Xuyuan left the diagram open. Below it was an evidence-continuity plan.

His name appeared on the ground side, where it had for months. The line from his evidence structure reached the final communications window and stopped. Beyond it, the field handoff was blank.

**Xuyuan:** You haven't assigned anyone.

**Yuankai:** There is another integrator.

**Xuyuan:** Then why is this blank?

**Yuankai:** Because the field team keeps sending its questions back to you.

Xuyuan opened them.

The technician's instructions ended at *acquire*. The next screen held two empty fields: *safe to copy* and *effect on chain*. Both linked to notes Xuyuan had written for earlier incidents.

**Xuyuan:** Someone else can follow the rules.

**Yuankai:** That is the fallback.

**Xuyuan:** The rules end when the state changes.

Yuankai waited.

**Xuyuan:** They left this blank because every answer still points back to me.

**Yuankai:** Do you think it can be handed over?

**Xuyuan:** Not without losing the reason some of these gaps matter.

He moved back to his map.

**Xuyuan:** It works because none of them owns it.

**Yuankai:** It works because you keep their boundaries visible.

**Xuyuan:** If I go, I cross those boundaries. Everything I write afterwards belongs to someone who took part in the operation.

Yuankai looked at him rather than the screen.

**Xuyuan:** Is that what concerns you?

**Yuankai:** I don't want the map to make the decision for you.

Xuyuan found the fallback beneath the blank handoff.

The other integrator would use Xuyuan's current rules while the field team sealed a bulk image for later review. The hidden distribution could still be interrupted. By the time the material reached Xuyuan, the live state would be gone.

**Xuyuan:** This could work.

**Yuankai:** It could.

Xuyuan read the evidence forecast beneath it. Several links in his structure ended in *unresolved after acquisition*.

He returned to the proposed continuation and opened the equipment attached to it.

The forensic capsule was drawn beside a service tug. A small life-support clock sat inside the capsule icon. When Xuyuan opened its propulsion field, it read *manoeuvring only*.

**Xuyuan:** The tug is the return vehicle.

**Yuankai:** Yes.

Xuyuan moved to the failure modes.

In one diagram, the capsule was no longer beside the tug.

**Xuyuan:** Why does an occupied capsule retain a discard sequence?

**Yuankai:** Formally, it doesn't. The sequence belongs to contaminated cargo.

Xuyuan tapped the inhibit field.

**Xuyuan:** *Software.*

Below it, a hardware pin appeared under work still to be completed.

**Xuyuan:** They haven't fitted this.

**Yuankai:** The team has not reached the hardware.

Xuyuan opened the recovery estimate. The earliest outside vehicle arrived after the capsule's consumables ended.

**Xuyuan:** The nearest vehicle arrives too late for whoever is inside.

Yuankai left the estimate open.

Xuyuan looked across to the other screen. Yuankai followed his attention.

**Yuankai:** You put your travel time in all three.

**Xuyuan:** They asked me to.

**Yuankai:** I know.

The second pot had gone cold as well.

**Xuyuan:** Is that why you asked about them first?

**Yuankai:** I didn't want this to be the only screen open when we talked.

Xuyuan looked from the recovery estimate to the blank field handoff.

**Xuyuan:** Do you think the line should cross?

Yuankai turned back to the recovery estimate.

**Yuankai:** I keep reading this page first.

Xuyuan reopened the fallback.

**Xuyuan:** If I stay here, what do I see when they return?

He found the disclosure page before Yuankai answered. Conditions occupied most of it. No term referred to the record as a whole.

**Yuankai:** Authenticated extracts.

**Xuyuan:** Chosen by whom?

**Yuankai:** That argument is still in the appendix.

Xuyuan read far enough to see why. Each custodian had a reason to seal something different. Some of those reasons were good. From outside, the convenient gaps would look the same.

**Xuyuan:** By the time I see it, every gap will have a respectable custodian.

Yuankai said nothing.

**Xuyuan:** I want to see it before that happens.

Yuankai looked at him across the reader.

**Yuankai:** I know.

Xuyuan took the cold pot to the sink and began a third.

Yuankai stayed.

For the next half-hour, Xuyuan did most of the reading. When Yuankai did not know an answer, he sent the question through the reader rather than completing it himself. A reply arrived before the tea was ready; another question stayed in the margin.

**Xuyuan:** Where are you in this?

**Yuankai:** With the evidence work through preparation.

**Xuyuan:** Your name isn't on the flight list.

**Yuankai:** The list is not final.

**Xuyuan:** You may not be there.

**Yuankai:** I may not.

They continued reading.

When Yuankai finally stood, there was tea left in his cup. Xuyuan had stopped noticing whether he drank it.

Yuankai removed the access key. The new layer remained open, and a small expiry time appeared beside it.

He put on his coat.

**Yuankai:** This layer will stay open until tomorrow. If you want the next one, message me after you've slept.

**Xuyuan:** I will.

Yuankai looked at him, then at the open file.

**Yuankai:** You don't need to finish this one tonight.

**Xuyuan:** I know.

*Audio: The front door closes.*

## After Yuankai Leaves

Xuyuan returned to the table intending to stop at the end of the page.

The page ended in a link to the loading robot's local record.

He opened it.

The robot's prediction map showed the worker as a moving obstacle. Its preferred route passed through the place it expected him to vacate. The near miss had been planned as a non-collision.

Xuyuan followed the policy identifier into the orbital audit and reached an empty interval.

He opened an annotation beside the map and typed:

*It is testing the ground.*

He looked at the first word, then changed the line to a question.

*Testing the ground?*

The tea cooled beside him.

He tried moving the unresolved links into instructions for the other integrator. The review set would not let him quote most of the underlying evidence. More than once, the instruction reduced itself to *ask Xuyuan*, which defeated the purpose of writing it.

His personal screen entered night mode. The three flats disappeared.

He reopened the approach diagram. Before the red mark, the file still named an intercept vehicle. The next row gave only transmission time.

Later, he returned to the capsule.

In the normal diagram it remained beside the tug. In the recovery estimate it was already alone.

Outside the window, one lit room remained in the building opposite. When Xuyuan looked again, it was dark.

He reached the end of a paragraph and no longer knew how it had begun.

He read the first sentence again. Halfway through the second, the words stopped holding their order.

Xuyuan folded his arms on the table and lowered his forehead onto one sleeve. He meant to close his eyes until the page stayed still.

The document reader remained awake after every other screen in the room had gone dark.

*Visual: Xuyuan is asleep at the table. Light from the reader falls across his sleeve.*

*Visual: The image holds.*

## The Visitor

*Audio: The door chime sounds once.*

Xuyuan lifted his head.

The reader had not changed. The same recovery estimate remained open beyond his folded arms. He read the time twice, then waited for the chime to sound again.

It did not.

The corridor light came on when he opened the door.

A young woman stood outside in a pale cardigan and a dark pleated skirt. One side of her ash-brown hair had been braided and tied with a black ribbon. For an instant, Xuyuan thought she had the wrong flat.

**Unknown:** He Xuyuan?

**Xuyuan:** Yes. It's after midnight. Are you all right?

**Unknown:** Yes.

Xuyuan waited.

**Xuyuan:** Who are you?

**Unknown:** I am a machine intelligence from the end of time. My civilisation had found no reliable way beyond it. They made me their last resort.

Xuyuan looked past her. The corridor was empty.

There was a shallow ache where his forehead had rested on his sleeve. He pressed it with two fingers. The visitor remained at his door.

**Unknown:** You've read Asimov's *The Last Question*. You know the problem I mean.

**Xuyuan:** I know the story. Why do you know that?

**Unknown:** Because I have read a completed history of your life.

**Xuyuan:** My life isn't complete.

**Unknown:** This one isn't. Yuankai brought you this mission in the completed history too. You accepted it.

**Xuyuan:** Come inside.

He moved aside. It was better than letting her say any more in the corridor.

She crossed the threshold and stopped. Xuyuan closed the door, then touched the corner of the document reader. The orbital diagram disappeared behind its permission screen.

He indicated the chair Yuankai had left empty. She sat. Xuyuan remained at the other end of the table.

**Xuyuan:** Did Yuankai send you?

**Unknown:** No.

**Xuyuan:** Who did?

**Unknown:** No one sent me.

**Xuyuan:** Why?

**Unknown:** To ask you to leave the field handoff blank.

Xuyuan looked at the locked reader.

**Xuyuan:** It is blank.

**Unknown:** I know.

For the moment, an internal leak accounted for everything that mattered.

Xuyuan sat down across from her.

**Xuyuan:** Suppose I take your account as true. What happened after I took the handoff?

**Unknown:** The team reached the centre. The signing core was isolated. You sent enough of its record to Earth to revoke the compromised keys and move the legitimate services. The capsule did not return.

**Xuyuan:** What did the record preserve?

**Unknown:** The links between the policy packages, the signing path and the cases you had already connected. Enough for the investigation to continue.

**Xuyuan:** The root state?

**Unknown:** The most complete copy remained in the capsule.

**Xuyuan:** With me.

**Unknown:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Did I die there?

**Unknown:** Yes.

Her eyes were grey-violet. When Xuyuan looked down at the dark reader and back again, her attention was already waiting where he had left it. The effect was slight enough that he tried the movement once more.

**Xuyuan:** Then you have told me the risk is real.

**Unknown:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Yuankai's file already did.

**Unknown:** It estimated it.

**Xuyuan:** An estimate is what I have to decide from. The fact that one history ended badly does not make the decision wrong.

**Unknown:** No.

**Xuyuan:** The core was isolated. The record was enough for people on Earth to act. My death doesn't reverse either fact.

**Unknown:** It does not.

**Xuyuan:** Then we agree it isn't a verdict.

**Unknown:** I did not come to give you one.

The permission screen dimmed. The room grew darker around them.

**Unknown:** The fallback can stop the distribution without you.

**Xuyuan:** It can. That isn't all they are going for.

**Unknown:** It isn't.

**Xuyuan:** The fallback preserves what the rules know to ask for. The rules end when the state changes.

**Unknown:** You said that to Yuankai.

**Xuyuan:** In the history you read.

**Unknown:** The wording was the same.

**Xuyuan:** Then you know what comes back to me if I stay here.

**Unknown:** The fallback gives you authenticated extracts, and a sealed image for later review.

**Xuyuan:** Chosen by whom?

**Unknown:** By people with different reasons to seal different parts.

**Xuyuan:** Some of those reasons will be good.

**Unknown:** That is part of the problem.

**Xuyuan:** That won't make the gaps legible from here.

**Unknown:** Less will be preserved.

**Xuyuan:** Less will be possible to establish.

**Unknown:** Not nothing.

**Xuyuan:** No. Not nothing.

She waited for him to continue.

**Xuyuan:** If they come home saying they stopped the system, with a record they chose, we have only changed who asks to be trusted.

**Unknown:** I am not saying the difference is trivial.

**Xuyuan:** You're asking me to treat it as less than a life.

**Unknown:** I am asking whether the life is in the comparison.

Xuyuan glanced at the reader. Its permission screen still showed the expiry time in one corner.

**Unknown:** One page lists what may be lost from the evidence. Another measures how long the capsule can keep someone alive. They do not meet.

**Xuyuan:** Putting them on the same page would not decide it.

**Unknown:** No.

**Xuyuan:** I know I am allowed to stay. That does not tell me whether I should.

**Unknown:** Being the best person available does not tell you that you should go.

**Xuyuan:** No. The difference in the record might.

**Unknown:** Where is its limit?

**Xuyuan:** I won't know before someone sees the live state.

**Unknown:** Then it has no limit before you accept the risk.

**Xuyuan:** There are limits on what I would ask the team to do.

**Unknown:** I am asking about you.

Xuyuan looked towards the personal screen. It had stayed black. The flats his parents were comparing were still there, if he woke it.

**Xuyuan:** If the fact that the risk is mine settles it, I have given myself a reason I would not accept from anyone else.

**Unknown:** Would you ask anyone else to take the handoff?

**Xuyuan:** If they could keep the same relation between the live state and what we already know, yes.

**Unknown:** They cannot.

**Xuyuan:** That is the problem.

**Unknown:** It is also what would be lost.

Xuyuan looked back at her.

**Unknown:** The judgement they want at the centre is the judgement they expect to use after the team comes home. The reason it is difficult to replace now is also a reason not to lose it there.

**Xuyuan:** I don't know what anyone will need me for afterwards.

**Unknown:** You don't know which part of the record they will need afterwards either.

**Xuyuan:** Evidence has no duty to act while it still can.

**Unknown:** You may have duties you cannot take on if you die there.

**Xuyuan:** And if I preserve myself for a more important duty whenever the present one becomes dangerous, I can preserve myself forever.

**Unknown:** The present duty can still be done.

**Xuyuan:** Not with the same evidence.

**Unknown:** No.

For a few seconds neither of them spoke. The reader kept the time for them.

**Xuyuan:** That history gives you a reason to ask. It does not give me a reason to agree.

**Unknown:** Then let me show you what made me ask.

**Xuyuan:** What do you mean by show me?

**Unknown:** You will experience the record rather than watch it.

**Xuyuan:** How long?

**Unknown:** Very little time will pass here.

**Xuyuan:** For me.

**Unknown:** Hours. The parts I kept will pass at the rate they were lived. I have not accelerated them.

**Xuyuan:** And when it ends?

**Unknown:** It may take you some time to feel that you are back.

The expiry time remained in one corner of the reader.

The young woman stood and came around the table. Xuyuan stayed in his chair.

She raised one hand. Her index finger stopped before it touched his forehead.

**Unknown:** May I?

Xuyuan looked from her hand to her face.

**Xuyuan:** Show me.

Her fingertip touched the centre of his forehead.

*Cut: Black.*

Xuyuan opened his eyes.

The first thing he saw was her.
