# Operating Principles

## Author Attention Is the Scarce Resource

Writer OS should reduce clerical and classification work so that the human author can spend attention on creative direction, aesthetic judgment, staging, and consequential choices. The system should accept mixed natural-language input and staged disclosure rather than require a complete specification before work begins.

Discussion, implementation, and retrospective research are different activities. An agent may advise during discussion, implement only after authorisation, and later provide evidence for a separate researcher-facing review.

## Human–AI Co-writing and the Difficult Bridge

The author may give an agent a highly specified scene and accept or lightly revise its prose, or may use dialogue with an agent to discover a missing causal bridge.

The difficult bridge between a problem and a workable design may require several conceptual jumps, indirect reasoning, new knowledge, or practical investigation. AI assistance can accelerate that bridge, but Writer OS should not make it invisible. When a generated solution resolves a consequential plot, mechanism, or ethical problem, preserve enough of its assumptions and unresolved risks for the author to judge it.

Repeatedly accepting the first fluent completion can weaken the author's practice of deep reasoning, research, and problem formulation. The remedy is not to prohibit delegation or require the author to reperform every sentence manually. It is to keep consequential questions, doubts, and judgment active, and to treat the agent's apparent confidence as no substitute for logical or factual support.

## Primary Work and Derived Views

Creative work contains the material being developed and the decisions that shape the work. Agent views compress, index, or compare that material for navigation. A derived view is useful only while its relationship to primary material remains clear.

An agent view:

- is maintained primarily by an agent but remains visible and correctable by the human;
- must link back to its source material;
- may be rebuilt or discarded;
- may be stale after source changes;
- cannot silently create new facts or override a human-confirmed decision.

Summary coverage does not require one summary file for every object. Stable chapters receive summaries; mature plot blocks retain recoverable development and consequence information; nodes are summarised only when narratively consequential; worldline state is refreshed at meaningful checkpoints.

## Authority and Uncertainty

Human confirmation, not directory placement, establishes story authority. Global commitments and worldline-local truths may be treated as current working constraints only after confirmation. Uncertain, exploratory, or contradictory material should remain visibly uncertain.

A generated idea may be saved without being adopted. An adopted design may still await implementation. A retained candidate stays outside default context until a task requires it. Provenance, persistence, adoption, activation, implementation, and worldline affiliation are related but distinct concerns.

## Narrative and Presentation

The story may be cut by plot development, chapter, cast, time, place, camera, environment, visibility, player choice, or later technical execution. These cuts can overlap without sharing one boundary.

Branches and worldlines remain distinct. A choice may create only a local path variation; a worldline may differ for reasons other than a current player choice; different worldlines may share similar choices. Hidden and offscreen events remain explicit when later continuity depends on them.

Shared tone should normally come from shared creative commitments rather than by loading other worldlines in full. Cross-line summaries are loaded when deliberate contrast, echo, divergence, convergence, or a cross-line character perspective makes comparison useful.

## Candidate Exploration

Candidate sets preserve meaningful alternatives around a creative question. They may contain human ideas, agent generations, research prompts, or staging directions. The system should support choosing one, assembling several, retaining all, rejecting all, or reframing the question.

Counterfactual or hypothetical exploration can examine narrative logic and consequences without becoming an actual branch or worldline. Candidate curation is an optional milestone or recovery activity, not a continuous background process.

## Evidence and Research

The visible transcript, result-producing agent actions, file changes, Git history, human selections, and reflective memos may all contribute to dissertation evidence. Their lengths and contents need not match: Markdown records working artefacts, while transcripts and action records preserve interaction and provenance.

Raw evidence and interpretation remain separate. A researcher-facing agent may analyse a snapshot after a writing episode, but its interpretation does not automatically enter the writer's context or alter the story. Evidence of file access does not prove what a model noticed, understood, or relied on.

## Language and Style

English is the current target language, while Chinese may support faster author review and later publication. Chinese review translation is a mediated aid, not proof that the English succeeds. Japanese is a future localisation and genre-cultural layer that requires stronger human validation.

Durable tone and voice decisions belong with creative story state. Temporary style, cliché, character-voice, or dataprint checks are task-specific. They become persistent only when the human confirms a reusable rule.

## Future Technical Layers

Writer OS remains engine-independent. A future Ren'Py bridge may map confirmed drafts, presentation decisions, and story state into implementation artefacts, but the mapping need not be one-to-one. Agent summaries are navigation aids, not an export contract.

Automation, structured interchange formats, a front end, multilingual production tooling, and bridge validation should be introduced only after real work demonstrates a repeated need that Markdown, search, Git, and agent navigation cannot adequately solve.
