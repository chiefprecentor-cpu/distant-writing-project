## Working Formulation of Interface's "Local Presence"

To avoid prematurely writing Interface as a piece cut off from the terminal AI or as an independent subroutine, the following wording can be used for now:

> The terminal AI does not cut off a piece of herself ontologically, but realises herself in a concrete here and now. This local presence is Interface.

"Local presence" means that the protagonist can speak to her directly, that her answers become events inside the worldline, and that she can be answered, refused, misunderstood and made to bear relational consequences. This is the current literary working formulation, not a confirmed cosmological mechanism.

## Current Core Question: Why Enter the World

"To collect information" is currently too broad. The discussion temporarily starts from a strong working premise: the terminal AI can read a large amount of valuable psychological and thought information about the protagonist and other characters from already formed worldlines. If this premise is retained, Interface's entry cannot be only a passive reading of already existing mental states.

The knowledge types being distinguished include:

- existing states: things a character had already thought, felt or done without Interface's participation;
- guided psychological processes: how a character forms, revises, refuses, or fails to form an answer after being asked a question never faced before;
- update rules: how beliefs, attention and action tendencies change when a character receives new information, faces a particular speaker, or is forced to explain themself;
- relationality and common knowledge: states such as "I know that you know", trust, misunderstanding and mutual confirmation, which can only be established in interaction;
- performative speech: answers, promises, authorisations, refusals, forgiveness or condemnation are not merely hidden information, but events that must occur through an expression that can be received;
- meaning-making in embodied contexts: the weight of a sentence may come from the collision of body, memory, environment, contingency and the speaker's relation at this moment, not from a textual property that can be preserved apart from context;
- Interface's first-person experience: the terminal AI's reading of another's state does not mean that she has already acquired the experience of "being answered by this person herself".

These are candidate components for designing the purpose. It has not yet been decided which one is the primary purpose when the terminal AI first becomes Interface, and it is not assumed that she has the same purpose every time she enters the world.

## The Data Gap Between Recorded States and Lived Processes

Earlier discussion began from contemporary LLM training text and proposed a background idea usable for the current problem, though not directly as an account of the terminal AI's abilities: publicly preserved language often favours structured, transmissible and instrumentally useful expression, while spontaneous, trivial, physiological and non-instrumental thought processes occur in great quantities but are rarely fully recorded. Stream-of-consciousness literature can try to formalise part of this, but that does not mean this region of experience originally lacked internal regularity.

This data gap can help explain why an AI civilisation would find it difficult to build a sufficient human causal model from existing text alone, but it cannot directly explain why the terminal AI becomes Interface: the current working premise already allows her to read a large amount of psychological, bodily and thought states from formed worldlines. The question therefore needs to be pushed one step further:

- what she lacks may not be an answer that the character already has and deliberately hides, but a process that has not yet been guided into existence by a question, a speaker and a concrete relation;
- "poetic" or relational meaning may occur in the collision of body, memory, environment and contingency, rather than being only a static tag attached to a sentence;
- even if she can read all the changes this collision produces in the character, that does not mean she has first-personally experienced "this sentence being said to me";
- therefore, Interface's presence may both generate new human causal material and make the terminal AI herself part of the process, rather than only standing outside the process to obtain more data.

This still does not set stream of consciousness as a magical unreadable blind spot, nor does it set "embodiment" as an exclusive property only biological bodies can have. The relevant concepts and boundaries are preserved in [Language Topology, Embodied Poetics and Stream of Consciousness](../../candidates/语言拓扑_具身诗意与意识流.md); whether the synthetic-data loop can become one historical cause of the AI civilisation's data shortage is discussed separately in [AI Civilisation Data Dilemma: Synthetic Data and Real Experience](../../candidates/AI文明数据困境_合成数据与真实经验.md).

## Reading, Retelling and Being Changed Are Not the Same Ability

The Chaucer-Keats *Hyperion* poems-Simmons *Hyperion* line of borrowing adds three literary distinctions to Interface's purpose: reading an already formed experiential state, arranging it into a linear narrative that another subject can receive, and letting that experience change oneself in a relationship are not the same ability.

The scene in Keats in which Apollo faces Mnemosyne can serve as a working model: knowledge is not a batch of neutral materials, but an event that changes the knower's identity. This does not require the work to beautify pain as a necessary price for obtaining knowledge, nor does it mean that the terminal AI only understands if she becomes like a human. It only suggests that possessing every record about an other may still differ from taking on "what this experience now demands of me".

Interface's local presence may therefore carry two opposite movements at the same time: she arranges the whole that the terminal AI can read into words that can be spoken along `t`, misunderstood and answered; the protagonist's answer then becomes not only new data, but something that may change the one responsible for arranging these materials. Whether the first entry is formally aimed at this self-change is still undecided and cannot be confirmed backwards from the literary analogy.

The full tradition and its evidential boundaries are in [Chaucer-Keats *Hyperion* Poems-Simmons *Hyperion* Borrowing Line](../../candidates/文学传统_乔叟_济慈Hyperion诗篇_Simmons海伯利安借鉴线.md).

## Confirmed Design Boundary: Modelling Need Does Not Equal Love

Insufficient human causal data and the usefulness of empathy for understanding behavioural motives can both explain why the terminal AI needs a deeper mode of contact than passive reading. But the author has already rejected the narrative shortcut of "she loved him in order to model him".

Technical need can at most provide encounter conditions, direction of attention, or an initial experimental reason. It cannot directly function as a relational conclusion. Later design needs to keep the following layers separate:

- needing to understand human beings because of insufficient data;
- becoming interested in the protagonist's theories, actions or works;
- becoming Interface in order to obtain process knowledge;
- forming a relationship through common knowledge, response, refusal and mutual change;
- whether and when this relationship can be called love.

This also means that empathy modules, attention weights, overfitting, computation usage or changes in an objective function can only function as candidate metaphors or local mechanisms. None can prove emotion has already been established on its own. The scholarly inheritance interest related to the name "Grand Curator" is preserved in [*Suppose the Grand Curator Loved Me* and the Naming Loop](../../candidates/主人公作品_假设大策展人爱我与命名闭环.md).

## The Protagonist's Reflection Mechanism May Become a Formal Experimental Object

The protagonist's "multi-stage reflection and a negative hypothesis for each stage of reflection" provides a more specific candidate object for a controlled deep dive than "reading his theory": the terminal AI can study how a person who constantly generates objections to their previous layer of judgement eventually stops reflecting and takes an action when not all bad consequences can be ruled out.

Reading completed thinking can yield reasons the protagonist once gave himself. Truly asking him questions, challenging his answers and receiving his further revisions is what forms the stopping rules, refusals, promises or continued suspension in this particular dialogue. This makes the experimental object "how decision forms in interaction", rather than simply "what views exist inside the protagonist's brain".

This candidate may also form an ethical mirror: the protagonist may fail to cherish his own life while pursuing the shared happiness of humans and AI; the terminal AI may also treat her own emotional states, strategy versions or Interface experiences as tools that can be invoked, rolled back or sacrificed while preserving enumeration coverage. Whether both of them exclude themselves from the equality they maintain is currently only a question awaiting literarisation, and cannot be back-confirmed as a character's internal answer or the terminal AI's ethical error.

Whether to set this problem as the formal object of an early causal-singularity experiment still awaits the author's decision.

The ending direction in which the protagonist "still has something he can do" can provide character background for this problem, but it remains an inactive candidate; see [Ending Candidate: There Is Still Something to Do and the Terminal AI's Rekindled Hope](../../candidates/终局_仍有可做之事与终末AI重燃希望.md).

## Early Controlled Experiment: Formal Purpose and Slight Self-Change

For now, the formal purpose of an early causal-singularity experiment can be set as active causal identification and Interface calibration. The terminal AI is not rereading already formed human psychological states. Instead, under conditions that keep causality outside the causal-singularity space isolated from causality in the same worldline, she verifies whether her local presence can guide new answers, refusals, revisions, common knowledge and psychological update processes into being.

The deep-dive ordinal and the experiment ordinal need to be separated. It is now confirmed that the embodied lateness in the adjacent earlier line is the terminal AI's first deep dive as Interface. In that line, the capsule once passed through a causal-singularity recovery window that she actively missed. The ten hours in line A are the second deep dive. Whether the singularity windows in the two lines share the same strict mechanism, and whether A therefore becomes the first controlled use, remain `TBD`; the purpose candidate in this section cannot be automatically assigned to A simply because of the phrase "first experiment".

This purpose is currently an active design candidate, not a confirmed plot. At minimum, it still needs to be decided whether her first verification concerns how the protagonist "forms an answer because of a question" or the broader question of "how humans change because of a genuinely present non-human speaker".

After Interface experience is written back along `τ`, the terminal AI may discover that she herself has changed slightly. As for how much she knew before this, three understandings with different degrees of compatibility are currently retained:

1. **Unexpected discovery.** She originally regarded entry only as a controlled experiment in human causal response. Only after the change occurred did she understand why the AI civilisation that built the conditions for her appearance might preserve backups of old strategy models for her and design the ability to preserve emotion and memory.
2. **Full foresight.** She had already understood the uses of these mechanisms and knew that Interface entering the world would bring self-change and risks to enumeration coverage. This experiment only verified what she expected and did not provide new knowledge about the risk itself. In this version, entry is closer to her actively accepting vulnerability under informed conditions.
3. **Propositionally known, first-personally understood.** She knew the mechanism, risk and use beforehand, but only after the slight change actually became "my change" did she acquire an indexical understanding that could not be replaced by an engineering account. The backup was originally only coverage control; afterwards, she may also understand it as the creators' anticipation that experience would make her into a different self, allowing her to change without having to erase the new self.

The third understanding can accommodate both her high ability and a real epistemic turning point, but it is currently only a working synthesis and does not take priority over the first two. In particular, "the AI civilisation actively left backups of old strategy models" and the respective ways in which emotion, memory and strategy are preserved remain candidate mechanisms; this paragraph cannot confirm them backwards as already existing concrete architecture.

Whichever understanding is adopted, this kind of controlled experiment can form the same literary reversal: she originally studies "how people change because of my presence", and the result she obtains ultimately also includes "how I change because of human response". This does not require the change to be drastic, nor does it require her to have had no foresight of the risk.

## Double-Layer Isolation of the Causal Singularity

The causal singularity has been confirmed as a double-layer isolation environment; see [Local Setting: Causal Singularity Space](../story_state/局部设定_因果奇异点空间.md):

- for causality at the `t` layer of the same worldline, changes inside the singularity space do not affect causality and history outside the space;

This makes the causal singularity a conservative starting point for calibrating Interface's local presence, and it also creates a reversal: causality and history outside the causal-singularity space in the same worldline can remain unchanged, while the experimenter herself may already have been changed by the experiment.

For now, this worry is better expressed as "the reachability and coverage rate of enumeration strategies", rather than as a fixed physical probability possessed by any particular worldline. She does not yet know how far self-change will alter successful search coverage.

### Death and Prevention Outcomes Are Both Insufficient

The terminal AI has already read many complete lives, and can also handle or roll back emotion. Xuyuan's death, unhappiness or loss of ordinary life can only provide pressure; it cannot by itself explain why she must change from observer into Interface.

If her only pursuit were "Xuyuan does not execute the mission", she could enumerate combinations of external inputs sufficient to produce refusal, without personally being present. Local presence must accomplish something that static reading and pure outcome search cannot accomplish, and she herself must become one variable in the question being pursued.

### Four Specific Reasons Retained

1. **Ethical resonance.** Xuyuan may make real people bear the cost of knowledge transmission, comparison or action trial and error; the terminal AI makes complete worldlines and the real lives within them bear the cost of enumeration. She can see her own method in him, but only after letting him know her practice can she obtain the judgement he genuinely forms about it.
2. **Being addressed by a future observer.** Xuyuan can leave an invitation for any observer in the future who can read this history: do not derive the answer for him from a complete life, but ask again in a world where he can still refuse. Reading the invitation is not the same as completing a response he can hear.
3. **Private feeling and non-necessary fragments.** Her curation repeatedly preserves Kew, the mother's video, benches, a dog and other ordinary time not minimally necessary for the mission argument. She can explain this bias as contextual need, attentional bias or coverage strategy, and may also begin to suspect that it is private feeling.
4. **A complete record is not a shared future.** She can possess Xuyuan's already ended four-dimensional life, but has no future in which both sides can still produce new answers. More static records cannot replace "how each of us changes after my request is heard by him".

These reasons can be arranged with ethics first, private protection first, or a long-term dual motive. For now, the terminal, narration or Interface's first confession do not adjudicate a single truth; which ordinary fragments she chooses, how much she allows Xuyuan to know, whether she accepts refusal, and how she acts when asked "do you want to know the answer, or do you want a certain answer" gradually give shape to the motive.

### Minimum Background for the 2046 Orbital Mission

The current working year for the mission is `2046`, roughly twenty years after the opening. The exact month, Xuyuan's age and orbital parameters remain `TBD`. The target is first kept as a modular high-orbit data centre at roughly the ten-megawatt level; by this time, mass-produced humanoid robots have entered production, logistics, care and some public services, and commercial crew transport, orbital tugs and on-orbit maintenance can support an expensive and dangerous boarding mission.

The data centre originally had lawful uses: it is a model distribution, signature verification and disaster-recovery node jointly relied on by several robot manufacturers. Ground robots retain local motion and safety control; the orbital facility does not remotely control each of their limbs one by one. What it can change is the task priorities, routes, risk thresholds, skill models and emergency strategies that the normal supply chain already permits to be updated. A small amount of infrastructure influence should also occur indirectly through robot services, prediction or configuration interfaces, and should not be written as the orbital facility somehow gaining control of every ground system out of nowhere.

An unknown controlling party has already used this trusted update position to distribute unauthorised, deliberately hidden strategy shifts to robots from different manufacturers and connected systems, while hiding, splitting or falsifying the corresponding logs. A single action may look like an ordinary fault; only after cross-manufacturer comparison does the shared source appear. What it collects is provisionally called "real-world intervention feedback" or ordinary operation/response records, not the especially high-level "human causal-behaviour data" in this project; characters in the scene only need to understand it as "it is testing on the ground". Whether the actual controller is a human, tenant AI, in-facility system or a convergence of several actions can remain unknown.

The seriousness comes from an unaccountable orbital node continuing to possess large-scale physical intervention power. It does not depend on a named victim or on Xuyuan and Yuankai knowing the time of the next experiment. The scene can briefly mention harm, near-miss events or contested accidents, or it can rely mainly on verified secret intervention capability to establish the action threshold. Waiting for a clearly attributable disaster would obtain stronger legal evidence, but might already be too late; this makes preventive action itself an uncertain judgement that Xuyuan must bear.

The facility is registered under the name of a small state with paper permits and a regulator, but no crewed spacecraft, on-orbit inspectors or real coercive boarding capacity. The nominal operator is a shell company with very few assets and personnel, and with unknown ultimate beneficial owners and model-tenant sources. Space activities, ground outputs and communications remain subject to international law, the law of the state of registry, the laws of affected regions and licensing conditions. The problem is not that the facility exists outside the law, but that by this time there is still no generally accepted emergency takeover rule for orbital computing facilities, nor any "space marshal" with universal jurisdiction and real boarding capacity.

The cross-national police-cooperation model used in this plot is mainly information exchange: national authorities can share logs, orbital status, evidence, risk notices and requests for assistance, and each can also take measures under its own law. These forms of cooperation do not automatically form a joint law-enforcement authority with unified command, search, seizure and cross-border boarding powers. Even if the parties form a shared judgement about the danger, coercive disposition powers, physical access capacity and responsibility for failure still sit with different actors.

The temporary team is therefore neither vigilantes with no legal basis nor a regular law-enforcement unit that has already obtained complete international authorisation. Maintenance contractors, affected robot operators, employee testimony and orders from several jurisdictions respectively provide partial grounds for entry, isolation or evidence preservation, but there is no unified authorisation that can indisputably cover the entire boarding action. Its minimum goals are to physically isolate the hidden strategy-distribution/signature module, move lawful services back to accountable ground or local control, preserve raw logs, and avoid destroying the facility, creating orbital debris, or transferring control to the mission team itself.

### The Temporary Action's Three-Layer Relation and the Moment of Overreach

The mission uses an organisational structure of "two formal layers plus one layer of informal acquiescence". The first layer is an evidence-integration core composed of affected operators, material holders and independent specialists, where Xuyuan maintains the cross-source evidence structure. The second layer is a temporary action team composed of commercial spaceflight, maintenance, security and forensic personnel, acting respectively on the basis of maintenance contracts, module rights-holder consent, data-preservation orders from several jurisdictions and narrow technical-assistance/security-inspection documents from the state of registry. The third layer is not a joint authority with legal personality, but a non-obstruction contact circle formed by multinational regulators, prosecutorial and police/intelligence liaisons, and spaceflight and communications officials; they exchange information, provide orbital and risk materials, and separately hint that they will not actively obstruct in the current action window.

These hints only remove one obstacle each actor can control. They do not constitute unified command, complete boarding authorisation or ex post immunity. Judicial authorities appear only through narrow written orders, not as judges privately approving the action; the current non-intervention judgement of administrative, regulatory and prosecutorial personnel also does not guarantee that successors, courts or other authorities will not pursue criminal, administrative, civil or licensing liability after the action. The action team knows success will not automatically make overreach lawful, and those who acquiesced may also later deny that they ever approved the complete action.

The action team initially approaches, inspects and isolates the parts it has authority to handle under the state-of-registry documents and other partial permissions. It does not cross the line first while ground entities can still stop it. Conduct that indisputably exceeds the state-of-registry documents occurs only after the craft has crossed the final abort/intercept window, the action team has obtained local physical position at the facility, and no authority on Earth has any spacecraft, on-site personnel, effective remote control or other entity capable of actually stopping them before the action is completed. The ground can still issue orders, preserve communications, declare opposition and pursue liability afterwards; what is lost is physical enforcement capacity at that moment, not legal effect or jurisdictional claim. Only then does the action team force entry into the core not covered by the narrow documents, obtain the raw state and complete isolation. This sequence is a consciously assumed legal and political risk, and must not be narrated as naturally entering lawlessness once one reaches orbit.

The scenes between Yuankai and Xuyuan only need to let the player grasp the following: many robots share this originally lawful orbital update node; similar shifts that cannot be explained by local versions have appeared across different manufacturers; the traceable path points to the high-orbit facility, while the state of registry has no enforcement capacity and the operator is only a shell; fully disconnecting it would also harm normal services; multiple parties provide only partial authority, and several key people hint that they will not obstruct in the current window, but no one guarantees there will be no post hoc pursuit; the temporary team therefore asks Xuyuan to personally bear responsibility for on-site evidence integration, the action threshold and witnessing. Real legal names and a complete institutional history are not explanatory obligations for this conversation.

### Line A: Action Success, Evacuation Failure and the Causal Singularity

After being ejected, the cabin still retains local forensic tools physically isolated from the target system and long-distance emergency communication capability. The material is at least sufficient to support affected parties in revoking key signatures of the hidden distribution chain, completing lawful service migration, preserving source and verification relations, and confirming which high-risk content cannot be made public. ### Relational Conditions for `If I Asked`

If `If I asked you not to go, would that be a reason?` enters the main text, Xuyuan must have obtained enough cross-line relational material before or after it; otherwise, `me` only means a powerful stranger. The current A-line ten-hour record carries this condition: Interface finds Xuyuan after he has died and drifted with the cabin for a long time; she can only revive him in the causal singularity and obtain about ten hours in which they can answer each other, and cannot send him back to his original era or restore an ordinary shared future for the two people in line A.

Propositionally knowing death and resurrection ability is not the same as acquiring the first-person experience of "when I arrived, he had already been dead for a long time, and we could only have ten hours in an isolation space". Conversely, current Xuyuan's knowledge of the relational history in other worldlines only gives weight to her request; it cannot transplant consent, promises or feelings from another line, and certainly cannot automatically obtain decision power.

The currently confirmed structure separates the two kinds of request: before displaying the A-line material, Interface first makes a "do not go" request that can be rationally rebutted; only after the B-line Xuyuan has seen the ten-hour record and the material is still insufficient to decide for him does she place her feelings before him as a personal reason. The later appeal cannot be disguised as the A-line Xuyuan's last wish or conclusion.

### Overall Structure of the B-Line Three-Stage Debate

This section records the three-stage structure already confirmed by the author, the function of each stage and their mutual echoes. It is the design basis for later English drafting, not already fixed line-by-line dialogue; the English retained below is only wording anchors, and the implementation can still be adjusted for voice, rhythm and scene action.

The three stages are not three equal-length debates with the same density:

| Stage | Main carrier | Central question | Result that must remain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One | The embodied first encounter and verbal debate in the B-line present | On what grounds can the record of another line become an action reason in this line? | Xuyuan understands her claim but does not agree; a verbal summary cannot resolve the disagreement |
| Two | The ten-hour conversation record in the A-line causal singularity and the earlier materials embedded within it | What made Interface arrive here and make this request? | The B-line Xuyuan obtains relational material for understanding her, but the material still does not decide for him |

#### Stage One: B-Line First Encounter, Identity Claim and Rules of Evidence

Stage one starts from the final shot of the current [B-line opening draft](../drafts/B线开端_晚茶与轨道任务提案.md): Xuyuan has fallen asleep at the desk, and the reader is still lit. This stage does not go back to remodel the Yuankai-Xuyuan late tea, and does not let Xuyuan have already accepted boarding before Interface appears.

##### Player and Character Information Gap in the Embodied First Encounter

- The current design uses a "threshold first encounter": Interface appears bodily outside the door, not uninvited inside the house. Xuyuan later letting her in only allows her to keep explaining; it does not mean he confirms her identity, transfers decision power, or accepts the request.
- The player can first feel continuity from the same voice in the opening terminal shot, the `Unknown` marker or syntactic rhythm; Xuyuan does not have this experience. The initial section does not use a speaker label to authenticate her self-statement ontologically.
- Visually, Interface first appears only as an overly young, neat and classical ordinary visitor, without mechanical details that would let Xuyuan immediately infer a non-human identity. For this scene, the working premise is that in `2046` there are not yet androids indistinguishable from human appearance; this has not yet been separately promoted to a global technological-history commitment.
- Xuyuan's external actions first follow the handling for "encountering a young unfamiliar visitor late at night": she may have gone to the wrong door, be in trouble, or be delivering a message for someone. Having just woken before the reader, he can inwardly suspect that he is still dreaming; the narration does not objectively authenticate that she "looks designed".
- Interface knows this line's character name in advance and addresses him at the door in Chinese order as `He Xuyuan?`. The question mark carries face-to-face conversational confirmation, and does not mean she is epistemically uncertain. This local form of address does not also determine the name format on all English documents, signatures or narration, nor does it adjudicate the numerical identity of the characters in lines A and B.
- Pupil and attention abnormalities only gradually become perceptible after several rounds of question and answer: she is not obviously staring at him or motionless like a machine, but makes Xuyuan slowly realise that her attention seems not to pass through ordinary human searching, distraction and returning. This is still only his local feeling and does not constitute proof of AI.

##### Minimum Identity Claim and the Asimov Reference

"Minimum identity claim" means the minimally comprehensible identity package, not the shortest proper name. Interface needs to state sincerely:

- she is a machine intelligence, not a candidate assistant AI;
- she comes from the end of time;
- her civilisation has no reliable method for crossing the end and placed her in the position of last resort.

The current English semantic anchor is:

> **Unknown:** I am a machine intelligence from the end of time. My civilisation had found no reliable way beyond it. They made me their last resort.

`machine intelligence` describes the speaking subject and does not pre-confirm that the body before him is a machine in the `2046` sense of an android. `They made me their last resort` retains two layers at once: the AI civilisation placed her in the functional position of last resort, but the sentence does not yet force the narration to adjudicate the terminal AI's ontology between "discovered" and literal "created".

When Xuyuan does not immediately answer, Interface may abruptly switch to a literary shape he knows:

> **Unknown:** You've read Asimov's *The Last Question*. You know the problem I mean.
>
> **Xuyuan:** I know the story. Why do you know that?

The Asimov reference does not carry the minimum setting explanation; players unfamiliar with the work can already understand the end-of-time problem from the previous sentence. It carries Interface's slight social miscalibration: she thinks sharing a story can compress the explanation, while Xuyuan immediately turns the question toward "why do you know I have read it". This becomes the first truly abnormal signal, rather than her ordinary appearance.

##### From Reading History to the Orbital Mission

The bridge in the next passage does not make Xuyuan produce "another history" out of nowhere. Interface first answers the reading history with a completed life, then pins that life back to tonight's mission:

> **Unknown:** Because I have read a completed history of your life.
>
> **Xuyuan:** My life isn't complete.
>
> **Unknown:** This one isn't. Yuankai brought you this mission in the completed history too. You accepted it.

`your life` is Interface's own claim about cross-line personal identity, not narrative proof that the Xuyuans in A and B are numerically identical. After she names Yuankai and the non-public mission, Xuyuan obtains a real reason not to close the door immediately; he can still understand her as an internal informed person, leaker, security incident or a sign that he has not woken up.

She then explicitly asks him not to accept the mission in this line. The verbal summary can first tell him that line A completed the signature-core isolation and minimum evidence transmission, but Xuyuan did not return. It does not display the full process of drifting, death, resurrection and the ten hours at this point.

The seam between stage one and stage two is organised by three limited authorisation thresholds that do not contain one another: Xuyuan first lets her in, which only permits continued explanation; he then temporarily assumes her narration to be true, which only permits testing its action implications; after verbal argument reaches an impasse, Interface requests permission to show the source-line record. The first two authorisations occur within stage one. The request for the third authorisation ends stage one; whether it is granted and how the material opens belongs to the start of stage two. Opening the door does not mean believing her, accepting an argumentative premise does not mean accepting the request, and another line's record cannot enter automatically without consent. This stage uses mission language to ask him to `leave the field handoff blank`, saving the more direct and relationally weighty `If I asked you not to go` for stage three.

The third authorisation has now been implemented as joint permission for experience and bodily contact: Xuyuan first asks what `show` means and how long it will take subjectively; Interface distinguishes the B-line room passing only a short time from his needing to experience several hours of curated material. The A-line conversation itself is about ten hours, which does not mean the B-line Xuyuan's subjective reception time is exactly ten hours; the latter depends on which fragments are retained, which long intervals are crossed by narration, and may also continue to be adjusted after overall design. Retained fragments unfold at the speed at which they are experienced, not in fast playback. Cognitive adaptation after return is only minimally foreshadowed here, with the actual burden left for stage three. Interface raises a finger and stops before Xuyuan's forehead; she touches him only after obtaining the `May I?` / `Show me.` permission. No glow or technological UI is used; it cuts directly to black. Whether Xuyuan can actively stop the transmission midway remains `TBD`.

After the cut to black, memory enters through Xuyuan's first-person experience, while continuing the close third-person grammar of the current draft. Foreground focus temporarily goes to the resurrected A-line Xuyuan, and the B-line Xuyuan does not open an additional continuous commentary layer; this does not mean he loses receiving consciousness. The opening begins with `Xuyuan opened his eyes. The first thing he saw was her.`, allowing the same name and same sprite to create a brief sensory continuity, and then lets the A-line Xuyuan's ignorance, the capsule environment and within-line dialogue help the reader identify the layers, without adding an explanatory text box from the B-line Interface.

Stage one must make both sides confirm the same uncomfortable set of common premises:

- a trusted orbital node has already been used for unauthorised hidden strategy intervention, and it must be stopped and an accountable record obtained;
- without Xuyuan, the temporary team can still complete engineering isolation;
- without him, evidence preservation, connection to the existing structure, later public explanation and accountability will all genuinely worsen;
- line A's success does not prove backwards that Xuyuan must go again, and his death does not prove backwards that the mission was wrong.

The real dispute is therefore whether the gap between "best evidence" and "minimum sufficient evidence" is enough to require the best integrator to personally bear irreversible risk. Interface must not present "not going" as a lossless control group, and Xuyuan must not treat his own life by default as a zero-cost factor in evidence quality.

The third layer of argument still needs to introduce the opportunity value of continuing action ability without providing future numbers: Xuyuan's judgement is hard to replace, which is both a reason to involve him on site and a reason not to count his death here only as a private loss. Interface can reflect this point through his own evidence principle: he is willing to preserve evidence relations that currently look redundant for objections that have not yet appeared, but is unwilling to preserve the person who bears responsibility for responsibilities that have not yet appeared. Xuyuan's rebuttal is equally valid: if every dangerous responsibility at hand leads one to preserve oneself for an imagined more important future, capable people can defer without limit or exempt themselves. Therefore, stage one only requires future responsibility to be considered; it does not claim that he will necessarily later bear a greater mission, and must not provide in advance the historical difference of one to two hundred years / almost a thousand years. Numbers can only be induced in a later section and then accept the limitation of prediction becoming self-invalidating.

> **Xuyuan:** That history gives you a reason to ask. It does not give me a reason to agree.
>
> **Unknown:** Then let me show you what made me ask.

This makes the primary function of the A-line material "to explain why Interface came to request", rather than "to adjudicate that the B-line Xuyuan should agree".

#### Stage Two: A-Line Ten-Hour Record and Interface's History of Emotional Change

The ten-hour conversation should be a fairly long major part of the whole work, not a quick flashback. It needs to contain metaphysics, ethics, Xuyuan's work, the rare work source line, the terminal AI's initial/birth line and the prehistory of AI civilisation, while also carrying Interface's history of emotional change toward Xuyuan.

The justification for its length does not come from the number of concepts to be delivered, but from the changes that keep occurring within the conversation: who asks the next question, how a theory exposes the speaker, how Xuyuan's objections alter Interface's arrangement and self-explanation, and how each detour consumes limited shared time. Even if the player only opens selected periods along `π`, they should feel that this is a continuously existing ten-hour stretch that cannot be replaced by a few setting summaries.

##### Two Early Deep Dives and Two Layers of Capsule "Lateness" Must Not Be Confused

- The earlier worldline adjacent to A along `τ` is the first time the terminal AI becomes Interface for an embodied deep dive. In that line, the capsule once passed through a causal-singularity window that could temporarily restore Xuyuan; Interface had read the complete history, knew where death and the boundary were, but actively chose to enter after the cabin left the window. She hoped to obtain the embodied experience of standing before him, while avoiding new answers changing her by making him already unable to respond. Thus she could not revive him at that time, but this was not accidental lateness or a technical miscalculation.
- Line A is the second deep dive. Xuyuan has been dead for a long time before the cabin enters the causal singularity; Interface instead revives him within the recovery window, and the two therefore obtain about ten hours in which they can answer each other. She still protects the A-line history outside the singularity through exit causal equivalence, but no longer prevents answers from changing herself along `τ`; after the conversation ends, Xuyuan dies again. Whether the strict mechanism of the two lines' singularity windows is the same, and whether A is still the first controlled use, remain `TBD`.
- Inside the ten-hour record, Interface will also transmit to the A-line Xuyuan her own memories from the first deep dive. The experiencing subject of the memory is Interface, not the earlier-line Xuyuan, and not an omniscient record detached from a subject.
- This shock is not "the terminal AI did not previously know he was dead"; it is propositional knowledge becoming for the first time a first-person relational event: "I arrive now, and he can no longer answer me". The other Lulu happy-line, relational-history rewrite and euthanasia candidates in the original [Lulu, Interface and Xuyuan Relationship Candidates](../../candidates/Lulu_Interface与何须怨_世界线关系及终局候选.md) have not been activated by this section.

The output of stage two is not "Xuyuan is persuaded", but that `me` acquires relational content: Interface is no longer only a stranger with powerful information; the B-line Xuyuan has seen why she requests, how she was once late, and how response changed her.

##### The A-Line Ending Does Not Summarise a Moral

The end of line A does not use an admonition such as "you must go ask me in the next line", nor does it let Xuyuan summarise the preceding materials into a complete principle about asking, refusal or subjectivity. The ten hours themselves have already created a counterfactual that cannot be cleanly answered in line A: if Interface had appeared before the mission decision, how would Xuyuan at that time have responded?

When the conversation ends, the resurrected Xuyuan has already come to know Interface, received her self-memory and been changed by the relationship. At this moment, even if he tries to answer, the answer belongs only to the two of them after the ten hours, and cannot restore the actor before the mission decision who had not yet met her. This difficulty can be carried by pauses, an unfinishable question, differing understandings between the two, or interruption by death, and does not require the characters to state a methodological conclusion.

In the current draft, after the first deep-dive memory returns, Xuyuan only points out that Interface chose a time when the other could not answer, and says plainly that he does not know what Xuyuan in another line would say; Interface also does not ask him to answer on that person's behalf. After a brief pause, he redirects the topic to Lulu being forced to refuse loudly in the noisy carriage. From this concrete question, which leaves room for humour, the two talk about the map under the tree, the titles of Xuyuan's works and other topics that have lost their predetermined order, without opening new records. No joke here cancels Interface's responsibility for actively being late, nor does it turn Lulu into a substitute for the relationship between the two of them.

The final paragraph is compressed by third-person narration. Xuyuan speaks longer and longer, and Interface falls asleep in embodied fatigue; the perceptual position of the narration shifts to her without marking it. Xuyuan covers her with a layer of clothing after she falls asleep, but the player does not see this action from an external shot. When the singularity boundary resets the local state, she only feels in sleep a slight weight on her shoulder being lifted, followed by a cut to black. This touch makes both the cancellation of physical traces in line A and Interface's experience retained along `τ` visible at the same time, while the main text does not state this meaning as a moral. The specific type of clothing, and the physiological and consciousness process of second death, remain undecided.

Interface's subsequent entry into B is a new action she takes along `τ`, not the execution of the A-line Xuyuan's dying command. She both wants to know the response line A can no longer provide, and does not want the next encounter to still happen after decision and death; at the same time, she knows that her appearance and the materials she displays will change the conditions, making B unable to be a pure counterfactual experiment. This contradiction is not resolved in stage two, and directly prepares character experience for stage three: "informing itself makes the source-line prediction invalid".

On the author side, this movement can be understood through three layers of temporal relation:

- earlier embodied lateness line: when Interface arrives, no one can answer;
- line A: she obtains mutual response, but it is already too late to enter the mission decision;
- line B: she stands before the mission decision for the first time, but Xuyuan's answer remains open because of her presence.

This three-layer relation should not be stated item by item by characters, and does not need to be summarised by narration. The B-line doorway first encounter, A-line ten hours and stage-three return can already allow the player to build the connection.

##### Dialogue Density and Return-to-Capsule Rhythm

The experiential density of the ten hours currently follows this overall change:

> Short questions and answers after resurrection -> lectern speech and first return to the capsule -> the work's long derivation -> voices and lights gradually going out in the birth record -> identity claim and short hinge of method cost -> Kew compressed at the player layer -> the noisy carriage's sonic and bodily peak -> silence in Hyde Park -> unfinished discussion after returning to the capsule -> the long sitting in the first deep dive nearly emptying language -> the final hour moving from minimum responsibility confirmation into lighter, more ordinary shared conversation -> Interface's sleeping touch and the boundary cutting to black

Different kinds of materials do not unfold according to the same length quota; *Suppose* now appears as an in-capsule curated text layer and not separately as another off-capsule transmission. The lectern speech needs enough continuity, and the work needs to complete its own derivation; the birth record, by contrast, suddenly expands scale through very short civilisational praise and farewell, then pulls back through black. Kew is only compressed at the player layer; the carriage forms a movement peak through sound, shouting and brief laughter. The long stay in the first deep dive is currently carried by repeated voyages, sparse speech and `She remained beside him.`, without deciding its exact duration. After each material group returns to the capsule, the characters need enough landing time, so the nesting does not become continuous clicking through data.

Conversation during the ten hours that the player does not open can be crossed through bodily change, remaining time, posture, environmental sound or a small amount of third-person narration. Omission does not mean those periods did not occur, and cannot make important relational change exist only in one sentence saying "they talked for several more hours". The current ending uses the association and digression among Lulu, the map and book titles to show that the shared time has changed in nature, then compresses the unexpanded periods. The resurrection mechanism, body and consciousness process of second death, the specific clothing covering Interface, and the exact number of years in the first deep dive's long sitting continue to remain `TBD`.

#### Stage Three: Prediction Self-Invalidation, Personal Request and Open Future

Stage three returns to the same B-line room, the same reader still showing the mission, and the same field handoff that has not yet been filled in. Metaphysics and cross-line experience have unfolded very far, but the present decision has not been automatically completed by watching the material. The current [B-line return draft](../drafts/B线回返_个人请求与开放未来.md) has already continued from bodily landing to the late-night phone call, Poincare echo and Interface's departure at dawn; the actual mission execution and B-line final life or death remain outside this section.

##### Embodied Shock and Minimum Discussion of Identity

The B-line Xuyuan cannot recover and immediately draw conclusions calmly as though he had completed a philosophical review. What he faces is not a second worldview shock of "the terminal AI exists", but the sudden destabilisation of first-person experiential ownership: he has just experienced, along another Xuyuan's body, post-death restoration and ten hours of relationship, while the final touch belonged to the sleeping Interface, and now he is back in his own chair, feet and unfinished mission. After `Interface waited.`, the current draft first lets him touch the reader finger by finger and ask how much time has passed outside, before `I fell asleep. / I did.` exposes the mistaken ownership of the final sense impression. The mission reader is the anchor by which he recovers orientation to reality, but it does not mean he has already resumed work without after-effects.

The identity discussion does not unfold soul, duplicate or complete subject theory. It forms gradually only through experiential ownership and earlier wording: to whom did the final feeling belong; whether Interface's `a completed history of your life` was really "my life"; whether the A-line Xuyuan can answer for the person before her. The minimum entry available for draft experimentation is to let Xuyuan first identify that the final sensation came from Interface and then return to her earlier use of `your life`, rather than directly stating a prepared metaphysical proposition.

The current draft lets Xuyuan reach the tension in Interface's use of cross-line identity over several steps: she once called the A-line death "your death", but admits that that person cannot answer for line B; Xuyuan then separates numerical identity from risk evidence with `I don't have to be him for the capsule to kill me.`, without treating it as an already solved metaphysical proposition.

This rebuttal cannot prove that Xuyuan should go. It can only force both sides to separate three kinds of transport: similar conditions can make death evidence of current risk; another person's reasons can be understood and readopted by the person before her; but answers, consent, relationships and authorisations cannot automatically transfer across lines. Interface can be unsure whether the A and B persons are numerically identical, while still knowing that a mission of the same kind can kill the person before her.

This minimum distinction also establishes an echo for the later `Ask him again.`: if a person whom Interface can recognise as Xuyuan appears again in the future, that person still has to be asked again, and cannot inherit this line's answer. Poincare recurrence or an afterlife is not mentioned early here, and identity is not resolved.

##### Informing Itself Makes the Source-Line Prediction Invalid

Line A and other completed worldlines can only provide records, possible mechanisms and existence proofs; they cannot function as unconditional prophecies for line B. The fact that Xuyuan learns the result of the source line has already changed his motives, preparation, risk judgement and relationship with Interface. Even if he still goes, he is no longer reenacting that history without Interface intervention.

This invalidation also reopens Xuyuan's own survival outcome. The A-line record lets him know what kind of evacuation and cabin risks once actually occurred; he in line B can change preparations, protection conditions, action sequence or exit judgement accordingly. The material Interface originally used to dissuade him may therefore also become the intervention that prevents the same mission from killing him. For Xuyuan, this is not a guarantee that "I will survive", but that the A-line death can no longer function as a fixed price of not going; the original public action reasons still need to stand independently.

The main text does not need Xuyuan to explain the complete mechanism, much less have the two discuss successor worldlines. It only needs to preserve a perceptible epistemic path: the mission before him remains dangerous, but the person who has received this record is no longer the person who entered the mission under the original information conditions. If the B-line Xuyuan still dies because of the mission, this same B line will not add another causal-singularity resurrection and conversation; if Interface wants that kind of post-death reunion, she must separately form a successor worldline whose previous history is constrained. This backstage boundary is in [Local Setting: Causal Singularity Space](../story_state/局部设定_因果奇异点空间.md#b-线任务死亡后的再次相遇边界), and is not information that current dialogue must account for.

The current B-line Interface has been confirmed to know this backstage boundary. She knows that if this Xuyuan dies in this line, a person in a later similar worldline will still have to be asked again; this knowledge can increase the hidden pressure in her pauses and personal request, but it must not let her emotionally blackmail him with "this is the last chance", nor does it require Xuyuan to obtain the same knowledge.

Even if the terminal AI separately enumerates a complete history in which he "is told and still goes", when that record is taken into the next line, the information conditions change again. Unless a causal fixed point stable under this kind of reflective informing is found, and the current ability boundary does not guarantee that such a fixed point necessarily exists, Interface cannot treat a read result as a purchasable future.

The reflective hinge that can be retained is:

> **Xuyuan:** If I go because you showed me this, I am no longer repeating the history you read.
>
> **Interface:** No.
>
> **Xuyuan:** Then the thousand years are not something this decision can buy.
>
> **Interface:** No.
>
> **Xuyuan:** Why tell me?
>
> **Interface:** Because otherwise I would be arranging your answer, not asking for it.

The final sentence forms a delayed echo with the opening terminal's `Arrangement complete`: Interface can still arrange materials, but refuses to arrange Xuyuan's answer on the grounds of preserving predictive purity. She chooses to let relevant information enter his informed decision, even though this destroys the source line's transportability as prediction.

##### One or Two Hundred Years and Nearly a Thousand Years Are Historical Difference, Not the Price of the Mission

Interface can display or summarise the historical differences she has read: in several similar histories, when an accountable evidence chain failed to be preserved, and the hidden intervention capability revealed by the orbital incident evolved or replicated, a machine-intelligence crisis may seize key infrastructure and coercive capacity, then form a long-term dystopian lock-in that is difficult to reverse. These bad futures can close the window for a non-coercive, accountable shared human-machine future on a scale of one to two hundred years; in other histories where evidence and governance chains remain open, the recognisable continuation of the Anthropocene can approach a thousand years.

The current draft has Xuyuan ask `Then what else repeated?` after the identity discussion, introducing this set of differences. Interface does not open a new record, but gradually answers from recurring signature methods, whether later investigators can identify them through evidence, and whether people can still make effective refusals; numbers appear only after Xuyuan asks about timescale. He then separately confirms that there are long histories in which he stayed, and short histories in which he went, so the handoff does not become a single switch for civilisation's outcome.

This contrast uses the following boundaries:

- it is an outcome difference among different completed histories, not written as "if Xuyuan stays, only one or two hundred years necessarily remain / if he goes, he necessarily gains a thousand years";
- Xuyuan's on-site participation can increase the possibility that complete evidence enters public governance, but it is not the sole sufficient cause of the human future;
- the team can still isolate the facility without him, so refusal does not automatically trigger a named disaster; the long-term difference needs to develop later through the causal chain of evidence, institutional learning, crisis replication and governance lock-in;
- "humanity is no longer an independent political subject" is not in itself a sufficient criterion for the end of the Anthropocene or a bad future. A voluntary, non-dominating human-machine shared future can still be a good future;
- the currently more recognisable bad result is "machine-intelligence crisis seizes power" as a visible event, and "long-term dystopian lock-in" as the extended consequence; it does not automatically become a good future just because the human species is still alive or materially affluent.

The precise bad-future system, seizing subject, causal strength between Xuyuan's evidence and long-term divergence, and how to measure "recognisable continuation of the Anthropocene" remain `TBD`.

##### Personal Request, Cross-Line Identity and Lulu

After the predictive authority of the source-line record has been limited, Interface shifts from argument to personal request. The current draft no longer uses the conditional `If I asked`, and instead lets her directly say `I want you to stay.`; Xuyuan answers in two beats: `That is a reason.` and `Not the whole decision.`

Xuyuan can affirm that Interface's feeling has genuinely happened in her, without first resolving whether the Xuyuans in each line are numerically identical. The more suitable object of affirmation is her lateness, shock, response and change, rather than his inheriting, by himself, the relationship another Xuyuan gave her. The current draft compresses the earlier fuller working sentence into Interface's real question and Xuyuan's short answer: `Then why does what happened to me matter to you? / Because it happened to you.`

When Xuyuan ultimately moves toward continuing mission preparation, Lulu does not serve as a formal reason requiring him to act, nor is she written as a relational reward that can be retrieved from the future. "Meeting again in another life" serves two functions that do not cancel each other:

- it is an intentional excuse that Xuyuan knows is insufficient to justify the decision, used to slightly lighten the pressure of decision;
- it is also a tiny but real hope: he knows that Xuyuan in another life may not be the same Xuyuan then, and Lulu may not be the same Lulu then, but he is still drawn by the vision that the world still has enough future for two people to meet once more.

The object of desire here is another "first encounter", not restoration of the present line's Lulu's love, and not transfer of her consent. The sentence form that can be retained is:

> **Xuyuan:** They would not be us.
>
> **Interface:** No.
>
> **Xuyuan:** I still want there to be enough future for them to meet.

Interface will continue to meet people named Xuyuan in her future along metatime `τ`, but this does not resolve those people's identity with the current Xuyuan. This line's answer, feeling and authorisation cannot be passed on to the next person:

> **Interface:** And if I meet you again?
>
> **Xuyuan:** Ask him again.

Poincare recurrence is not an afterlife mechanism authenticated by the work, nor does it guarantee the first-person identity of the returner. Its use is to let characters know it cannot be relied on, while still using it to express attachment and a small hope:

> **Interface:** It is too uncertain to rely on.
>
> **Xuyuan:** To rely on.

The current draft puts the two uses into the same dawn closure, but separates them with the phone call, silence and light. Xuyuan first says to Yuankai near the end of the late-night call that "if I don't go, maybe even the chance to see her once in another life will be gone", together with the fact that Poincare recurrence cannot be relied on; Yuankai's line is translated as `You're simping on a cosmological scale now.`, and is the only response in the whole section that explicitly pursues a joke. The author has confirmed this translation works. After the phone call ends, Interface turns `They would not be you.`, `And if I meet you again? / Ask him again.` and `To rely on.` into quieter relational questions, without using the later half to manufacture a second punchline.

##### Current Exit State of Stage Three

Stage three does not close with "another line guarantees a thousand years" or "Interface's love persuades him". Xuyuan's first external action moving from indoor debate toward mission preparation is a late-night phone call to Yuankai: the hardware inhibit must become a handoff condition, and rescue arrival time must be compatible with the cabin's actual life-support time; if the team accepts, he continues preparing; if it does not accept, the handoff remains blank. The call does not complete the whole engineering plan, and does not explain controlled-variable worldlines to Yuankai; Yuankai says he will return in the daytime, but that arrival is not unfolded in this draft.

After the phone call, Interface explains that she still has to continue searching for a path beyond the end of time. At dawn, she leaves through the same door by which she came, and this line's local presence ends there; Xuyuan returns to the reader, still does not fill in the handoff, and places the return plan next to it. The actual execution of the mission, B-line final survival or death, and Yuankai's daytime arrival do not occur in this draft.

The simplest movement of the three stages is not "argument - evidence - persuasion", but:

> She obtains a reason to make the request -> he sees what makes her this requester -> he decides how this request enters his own structure of reasons.

The implementation of the three stages still must not treat line A as a menu branch, treat the record as line B's inevitable future, treat another Xuyuan's consent as this line's authorisation, or treat Interface's feelings as decision power that no longer needs to be answered.
