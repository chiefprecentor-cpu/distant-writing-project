## Pressure points to compare

- `The Pattern` to some extent repeats the four encounters the player has just seen, but it also establishes Xuyuan's abductive voice, `She was the refrain`, and Yuankai's challenge to selection bias. If the opening pace later feels slow, compare only whether one synonymous back-and-forth can be reduced; deleting the whole theoretical movement should not be the default direction.
- The map orientation from Great Pagoda back to Temperate House at Kew has distinct character quality, and also gives ordinary shared time duration; if actual reading slows down, first compare whether one orientation process can be compressed. This must not thereby remove the water at Temperate House, the ducks by the lake, or sitting down before the confession.
- In the church conversation, Yuankai continuously uses questions such as `What do you want?`, `For what?`, `How long?`, and `Why?` to push Xuyuan from rejection to lifelong judgement, abductive reasoning, responsibility to the era, and London, which may temporarily make him seem like a therapist or a dissertation defence chair. Later comparison can let one thematic turn happen from Xuyuan himself, or give Yuankai a longer response with a clearer friend position; no specific revision has yet been chosen.
- Hyde Park concentrates AI unemployment, AGI deadlines, divination calendars, and job-search resources in adjacent paragraphs. They have character as Xuyuan's private risk calendar, but may also be misread as narratorial prediction; whether to adjust this depends on whether later text can continue to show that this is a character judgement rather than work-level certification.
- `When I die, I think I want to be buried under a tree like this.` may lead some readers to understand the current state as containing suicide risk, rather than only heartbreak, exhaustion, and a frozen wish. The existing mother's response remains restrained; whether this additional weight needs to be changed is still for the author to judge.
