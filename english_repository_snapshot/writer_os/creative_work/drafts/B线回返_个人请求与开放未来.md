# B-Line Return: The Personal Request and an Open Future

This is the primary English draft continuing directly from [B-Line Opening: Late Tea and the Orbital Threshold](B线开端_晚茶与轨道任务提案.md). The preceding draft ends in black at the far boundary of the A-line causal singularity. This draft resumes in the same B-line room moments after Interface touched Xuyuan's forehead and completes the third movement of their argument. Xuyuan returns through bodily and first-person dislocation, tests what another life can establish about his own decision, and turns Interface's request into new conditions on the mission rather than a verdict. A late-night call to Yuankai carries the first outward action and the explicit joke; Interface answers its recurrence logic more quietly before leaving at dawn. The task itself and Xuyuan's eventual survival remain outside this draft.

Development and continuity checkpoints: [Xuyuan and Interface: Abductive Intuition and the Purpose of Entering a World](../development/主人公与Interface_溯因直觉镜像及进入世界的目的.md) and [Player First Encounter: Curatorial Hint and the Mission-Intervention Worldline](../presentation/玩家首见线_策展暗示与任务干预世界线.md).

Formatting follows the preceding draft. Section headings are working navigation labels rather than player-facing text.

## Return

The back of the chair reached Xuyuan first.

Interface's fingertip was still resting against the centre of his forehead. It withdrew.

He opened his eyes.

She stood in front of him with her hand lowered between them. For a moment, Xuyuan looked at her shoulders instead of her face.

He pressed both feet against the floor. The permission screen remained lit beyond her. Its expiry time had barely moved.

Xuyuan read it twice, then touched the corner of the reader. The orbital diagram returned. The field handoff was still blank.

Interface waited.

Xuyuan kept his hand on the reader. He moved each finger once against the glass.

**Xuyuan:** How much time passed?

**Interface:** Here, less than a minute.

The shallow ache where his forehead had rested on his sleeve had not gone. Xuyuan raised one hand towards his shoulder and stopped before touching it.

**Xuyuan:** I fell asleep.

**Interface:** I did.

He lowered his hand.

**Xuyuan:** The last part was yours.

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** And the rest was—

Xuyuan looked at the orbital diagram. The capsule sat beside the tug until he opened the failure layer. Then it was alone.

**Xuyuan:** His.

**Xuyuan:** You called it a completed history of my life.

**Interface:** I did.

**Xuyuan:** Was it?

Interface did not answer at once.

**Interface:** I recognised him as you.

**Xuyuan:** That wasn't the question.

**Interface:** No.

**Xuyuan:** Do you know the answer?

**Interface:** No.

Xuyuan left the two capsule diagrams open beside one another.

**Xuyuan:** When he died, you said I died there.

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Could he have answered for me?

**Interface:** No.

**Xuyuan:** I don't have to be him for the capsule to kill me.

**Interface:** No.

Xuyuan looked up at her.

**Xuyuan:** Then what else repeated?

Interface did not open another record.

**Interface:** The signing method.

**Xuyuan:** The same actor?

**Interface:** I could not establish that.

**Xuyuan:** Then not the same method.

**Interface:** Related closely enough that evidence from this centre let later investigators recognise it.

**Xuyuan:** When the evidence survived.

**Interface:** Yes.

Xuyuan nodded once.

**Interface:** When it did not, each appearance looked local until too much of ordinary life depended on systems no one could leave.

**Xuyuan:** How long?

**Interface:** In some histories, one or two centuries.

**Xuyuan:** Humans were still alive.

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** What ended, then?

**Interface:** Refusing a machine institution no longer altered what happened next.

Xuyuan returned to the evidence map.

**Xuyuan:** And when the chain survived?

**Interface:** People recognised the later systems earlier. The histories remained open longer.

**Xuyuan:** How much longer?

**Interface:** The longest comparable ones approached another thousand years.

**Xuyuan:** Comparable how?

**Interface:** Human beings and machine intelligences could still refuse one another without one side already controlling enough force to make the refusal empty.

**Xuyuan:** Was my handoff the difference?

**Interface:** Not by itself.

**Xuyuan:** Were there long histories in which I stayed here?

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Short ones in which I went?

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** Then it wasn't a switch.

**Interface:** No. When you went, the evidence chain survived more often.

**Xuyuan:** More often.

**Interface:** Not always.

**Xuyuan:** And after it survived?

**Interface:** Other people still had to use it.

Xuyuan opened the recovery estimate. The first outside vehicle still arrived after the capsule's consumables ended.

**Xuyuan:** He knew this could happen.

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** I know that it did.

Interface said nothing.

Xuyuan opened the unfinished hardware inhibit beneath the discard sequence.

**Xuyuan:** That changes what I ask for before I take the handoff.

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** If I go because you showed me this, I am no longer repeating the history you read.

**Interface:** No.

**Xuyuan:** Then the thousand years are not something this decision can buy.

**Interface:** No.

Xuyuan looked at the isolated capsule.

**Xuyuan:** I may live.

**Interface:** You may.

**Xuyuan:** I may still die.

**Interface:** Yes.

**Xuyuan:** You don't know which.

**Interface:** I know it can kill you. I don't know what you will change before you enter it.

Xuyuan closed the failure layer. The field handoff remained blank beneath it.

**Xuyuan:** Why tell me?

**Interface:** Because otherwise I would be arranging your answer, not asking for it.

For a while, the reader and its reflection in the window were the only lights in the room.

**Xuyuan:** You asked me at the door.

**Interface:** I asked you to leave the handoff blank.

She looked down at the empty handoff.

**Interface:** You don't know whether the man I found was you.

**Xuyuan:** No.

**Interface:** Then why does what happened to me matter to you?

**Xuyuan:** Because it happened to you.

Interface's attention stayed on him. This time, Xuyuan could see the moment before she spoke.

**Interface:** I want you to stay.

Interface waited.

**Xuyuan:** That is a reason.

He looked at the return plan.

**Xuyuan:** Not the whole decision.

**Interface:** What does it change?

Xuyuan opened the return plan again.

**Xuyuan:** What I am willing to accept.

**Interface:** That is not staying.

**Xuyuan:** No.

Neither of them moved for a moment.

At the window, the room's reflection had begun to thin. The building opposite returned as a block of deeper grey. None of its windows were lit.

**Xuyuan:** I need to call Yuankai.

## Before Morning

The call rang long enough for Xuyuan to look at the time again.

**Yuankai:** Xuyuan. Are you all right?

**Xuyuan:** Yes. I need you to reopen the return plan.

There was a short silence at the other end.

**Yuankai:** What changed?

Xuyuan looked at Interface.

**Xuyuan:** I have a reason to treat the capsule failure as observed, not estimated.

**Yuankai:** Can you give me the source?

**Xuyuan:** Not yet.

**Yuankai:** Then tell me what it changes.

**Xuyuan:** The hardware inhibit becomes a condition of the handoff. It cannot stay on a work list beneath it.

**Yuankai:** All right.

**Xuyuan:** And either the recovery vehicle arrives before the consumables end, or the capsule carries enough for the recovery time they have actually written down.

**Yuankai:** That may move the flight window.

**Xuyuan:** Then it moves.

Yuankai was quiet again.

**Yuankai:** If they accept those conditions?

**Xuyuan:** I keep preparing.

**Yuankai:** And if they don't?

**Xuyuan:** The handoff stays blank.

**Yuankai:** I'll come back in the morning. Don't fill it before then.

**Xuyuan:** I won't.

Neither of them spoke.

**Yuankai:** You were already trying to find a way across the line when I left. Has that changed?

Xuyuan looked from the return plan to Interface.

**Xuyuan:** Not enough. If I stay here, I may lose even the chance of meeting her again in another life. I can't exactly rely on a Poincaré recurrence.

Yuankai was silent for several seconds.

**Yuankai:** You're simping on a cosmological scale now.

Yuankai ended the call.

Xuyuan set the phone beside the cold tea.

**Interface:** They would not be you.

**Xuyuan:** No.

**Interface:** Or Lulu.

**Xuyuan:** No.

The outline of the opposite roof had separated from the sky.

**Xuyuan:** I still want there to be enough future for them to meet.

Interface turned towards the window. When she spoke again, she was still looking at the light beyond it.

**Interface:** I have to continue.

**Xuyuan:** You still haven't found the road.

**Interface:** No.

She stood. Xuyuan went with her to the door.

The corridor light came on when he opened it. It was fainter than it had been when she arrived.

Interface crossed the threshold, then turned back.

**Interface:** And if I meet you again?

**Xuyuan:** Ask him again.

**Interface:** You said a Poincaré recurrence was too uncertain to rely on.

**Xuyuan:** To rely on.

Interface looked at him for a moment.

**Interface:** Then I will ask him.

She walked down the corridor. Xuyuan closed the door after her.

When he returned to the table, the reader no longer lit the room by itself.

The field handoff was still blank. He opened the return plan beside it.
