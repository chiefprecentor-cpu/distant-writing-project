## Established Plot Continuity

- The player first follows Xuyuan at close range; the Chinese-text name is rendered here as "Xuyuan", and the English draft uses `Xuyuan`.
- Xuyuan and Lulu collide before a small talk, having not known each other before. The talk, several consecutive weeks of transport disruption, one shared walk, and a later same-carriage meeting when the transport is operating normally gradually make it possible for them to talk.
- Xuyuan first mentions Lulu to his friend Yuankai. Later, at Mallowbell Maid Café, the two share a table with Lulu and Anna; Xuyuan and Lulu exchange contact details.
- Lulu wants to see cherry blossoms before the flowering season ends. After comparing London locations, Xuyuan invites her to Kew; they set out at noon on Saturday from an unnamed central London station.
- The Kew visit passes through Palm House, Great Pagoda, Temperate House, below the Treetop Walkway, the lake, and the cherry tree near the return to Victoria Gate. Under the tree, Xuyuan clearly says that he likes Lulu. Lulu says sorry and says that she is already dating someone, leaving no false hope.
- They sit together for a while before leaving. On the Underground journey back, Xuyuan thanks Lulu for coming, and Lulu says she really enjoyed seeing the cherry blossoms that day. The text does not decide whether her words are only politeness.
- On a weekday less than a week later, Xuyuan talks with Yuankai beside a church, then walks west alone through St James's Park, Green Park and the memorials, reaching the large cherry tree in Hyde Park.
- Hyde Park restores the entrance into Xuyuan's psychology and makes him clearly realise that two forms of job-search preparation cannot run in parallel: fully preparing for China's autumn recruitment, or fully looking for work in London. Neither cancels the minimum arrangement of first returning to China after graduation to spend several months with family. The English draft still currently retains the old two-option menu; the author has confirmed that this work will not adopt a multi-branch structure, but this round does not implement the linear rewrite of the menu, so that menu is text still awaiting correction and no longer constitutes an adopted player branch.
- Xuyuan has a video call with his mother, talking about Lulu, the confession and the rejection for the first time. After the call, he lies down on a bench facing the cherry tree, is briefly woken by a golden retriever, and closes his eyes again.

## Narrative and Presentation Boundaries

- Interface does not appear at the physical site where Xuyuan is located in this group of human scenes.
- Before the confession, the third-person narration close to Xuyuan gradually increases psychological visibility. After Lulu's rejection, narrative access to her inner world closes immediately. External presentation continues until trivial counterfactuals about blossom-viewing locations at Hyde Park reopen the psychological entrance.
- After sleep at Hyde Park, retain the hard cut: first cut to black and end the park ambience, then show an unlocatable work interface. Xuyuan does not see this interface, and there is no in-world causality between the dog and the terminal.
- The current terminal records that material arrangement is complete, that this resolution requires approximately `120` periods, and that `Unknown` evaluates the material as still excessive while the current order imposes the least burden on human cognition. The precise English remains determined by the end of the draft.
