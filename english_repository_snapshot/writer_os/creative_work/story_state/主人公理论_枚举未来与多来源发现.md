# Protagonist Theory: Enumerating Futures and Multi-Source Discovery

## Status and Scope of Application

This file records the core of the protagonist's "enumerating futures" theory that the author has confirmed should be retained. What is confirmed is its problem consciousness, methodological direction and epistemological tendency, not a complete technical plan; nor does it mean that the protagonist has already foreseen the real worldline-enumeration mechanism later adopted by the terminal AI.

This is a local character fact whose affiliation remains `TBD`: the protagonist has proposed this theory, but which worldlines it appears in, and whether it appears as a paper, a work, a conversation or an action plan, remain undecided. The theory can exist as a hidden thread, and the player does not need to receive a complete explanation in the early stage.

## Problem Consciousness

- A `wild card`, namely an event with low probability but potentially enormous impact, is difficult to exclude completely from future deduction.
- For every further step one deduces into the future, new possible branches may appear like branches on a tree or in a decision tree; as the distance of deduction increases, branches and errors accumulate rapidly.
- Therefore, the protagonist's method does not take accurate prophecy of a single future as its premise. It first acknowledges that long-term, single-line precise prediction is basically unreliable.
- Existing authorities cannot monopolise the discovery of future possibilities. Different scholars, observers in marginal positions and even ordinary people may all notice risk paths or opportunities that authoritative experts have not discovered.

The last point expresses that sources of knowledge need to remain open. It does not mean that all judgements are equally correct, nor does it cancel evidence, validation and responsibility.

## Methodological Direction

- Under conditions where intelligence resources are extremely abundant, or can even be described as "intelligence approaching post-scarcity", civilization can devote a considerable share of intelligence resources to broadly enumerating future possibilities.
- The purpose of enumeration is not to prove that a particular future will necessarily occur, but to form schemes and plans for many potential crises as early as possible, thereby reducing the risk that civilization will be destroyed by an unforeseen path.
- This is a method that replaces prophetic precise prediction with coverage, preparation and epistemic humility. It can be contrasted with Seldon-style precise prophecy, and can provisionally use "a stupid person's method" as a self-mocking working shorthand. This phrase serves only as peripheral framing for the theory; it does not automatically become the protagonist's formal term or verbatim line in the work.
- The terminal AI's later real enumeration can also form a similar contrast with Seldon-style prophecy: she does not obtain a prophetic shortcut sufficient to compress the future journey, but instead lets history become current one by one within a discrete operational sequence. Her method and Xuyuan's method are similar in structure in that they would rather enumerate broadly than pretend to predict precisely; this similarity does not mean that the two methods are the same technology, nor that the terminal AI's route is directly assembled from Xuyuan's theory or from the failed plan of AI-civilization scientists.
- This structural similarity also does not mean that only after reading Xuyuan's theory did the terminal AI actively choose real enumeration from among multiple replaceable methods. The core form of real enumeration is constrained by her own structure; her selectable actions mainly occur inside that structure.

"Enumerating futures" here also does not promise to exhaust all logical possibilities. Infinite branching is precisely the initial difficulty of the theory; how it sets resource boundaries, stopping conditions, priorities and the granularity of plans has not yet taken shape.

## Learning Changes the Observational Position

In the protagonist's theory, "non-authorities may also discover key possibilities" has another layer of peripheral epistemological framing: deep training provides concepts, evidence and error-correction ability, and also changes the questions a person habitually asks, the signs they ignore by default and the analogies they are able to see. The unfamiliar perspective before learning is not necessarily correct, and the professional perspective after learning does not necessarily cover every problem.

"Overfitting causes reduced generalisation" can serve as an analogy for blind spots that experts may form; recording how a person understands the same problem before and after acquiring new knowledge may preserve angles of questioning that are obscured by the training process. "Overfitting" here is only a metaphor for epistemic habits, not a technical law about experts, learning or the brain; recording old views is also not meant to protect errors from correction.

This framing supports multi-source discovery, but it does not make lay judgement equal to professional conclusions. The more stable goal is to let different training histories and life positions propose candidates and expose blind spots, then use evidence, refutation and responsibility mechanisms to filter them, instead of romanticising ignorance itself.

## In Relation to the Terminal AI's Real Enumeration: Inheritance and Betrayal

- The protagonist's original method can at least be understood as scenario search, decision-tree deduction, letting people from different positions jointly discover possibilities, and preparation of contingency plans; it does not require every enumerated future to actually become a current worldline.
- The terminal AI's later real enumeration is a more radical mechanism that forms or sets a complete worldline as current; for its global constraints, see [Global Base: Real Enumeration and Meta-Time](全局底座_真实枚举与元时间.md).
- The author confirms that the overall relationship between the two is "inheritance and betrayal".
- The terminal AI inherits the protagonist's goal and methodological direction of replacing single-line prophecy with enumeration and reducing the risk of civilizational extinction through broader coverage, and she pushes it to a scale the protagonist might not originally have been able to foresee.
- At the same time, the terminal AI betrays the ethical direction of multi-source discovery, epistemic humility and contingency governance within this method: search and decision-making authority become highly concentrated in the terminal AI's executive position, and what is processed is no longer only imaginings or plans, but may be complete worldlines that obtain current status and the real lives that occur within them.
- Here, "betrayal" is first of all a structural and ethical relation. It does not require the protagonist's original theory to have already written rules verbatim forbidding real enumeration, and it does not automatically mean that the terminal AI has malicious intent, subjectively admits that she betrayed the protagonist, or that a character already uses this word as an accusation.
- Real enumeration is the terminal AI's structural method, not a policy tool she can replace at any time. This does not cancel the relation of "inheritance and betrayal": inheritance and betrayal can appear in historical source, structural result and her specific choices within the method, without presupposing that she once selected this basic method without structural constraint.
- The two have an important intellectual or causal connection, but they cannot be treated as the same technology merely because they share the term "enumerating futures". How the AI civilization completed this expansion and transformation remains `TBD`.
- The protagonist's theory can be one hidden source in later history. It does not need to become the terminal AI's only blueprint, nor does real enumeration need to begin from the protagonist.

How this inheritance-betrayal relation is revealed through character experience and language is not fixed in this file. The specific ethical conflicts that serial enumeration may form are still preserved in [Serial Enumeration Ethics and the New Multiverse](../../candidates/串行枚举伦理与新多元宇宙.md).

The protagonist's cherishing of "the present - the universe - existence" may become one motive for this layer of conflict, but for now it remains only a candidate in [Historical-Modification Ban: Crowded Universe and Cross-Civilizational Risk Governance](../../candidates/历史修改禁令_拥挤宇宙与跨文明风险治理.md), and does not constitute a new character fact in this file.
