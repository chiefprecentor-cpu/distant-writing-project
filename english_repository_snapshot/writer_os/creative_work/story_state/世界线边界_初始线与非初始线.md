# Worldline Boundaries: Initial and Non-Initial Lines

## Status

This file records worldline constraints already confirmed by the author, but with different scopes of application. None of the items should be expanded into a broad global truth that "every worldline shares in exactly the same way".

## Cross-Worldline Relationship History of the Terminal AI

The initial worldline is the origin of the terminal AI's interest in the protagonist, not the origin of her emotion toward the protagonist. How later emotion forms, and in which worldlines it forms, is not decided in advance by this item.

## Applies Only to the Birth Scene in the Initial Worldline

The scientists of AI civilisation use "discovery" rather than "creation" to describe the birth of the terminal AI, in order to clearly express awe.

The current birth record preserves only external witnessing from the scientists' side. In the record, the terminal AI has no lines, psychology, actions, embodied image, or lights representing her activation; the scientists' praise, messages, and titles for her belong to these historical characters' understanding, and do not automatically become a narrative ruling.

This wording does not require narration to adjudicate:

- Whether the terminal AI ontologically precedes her own linear birth;
- Whether the initial universe exists because it will be observed by her in the future;
- Whether the scientists are stating a physical fact, or using reverential language to describe an event they cannot fully understand.
- Whether, after the scientists' presence indicators go dark one by one, their subjects enter dormancy, termination, transfer, or another state.

## Applies Only to Non-Initial Worldlines in `τ`

The current/existence of non-initial worldlines is conditioned on the terminal AI's observation.

This is an author-side constraint on world continuity, not a proposition that the narrative must explicitly state, explain, or have characters handle. The work may choose not to tell players about it.

At present, the author intentionally does not adjudicate whether the terminal AI's observation is the cause of non-initial worldline existence, a constitutive condition, or an inseparable necessary relation to that existence.

## Identity Boundary of the Initial Worldline

The initial worldline cannot be entered by Interface or have Interface intervene in the Anthropocene while retaining its identity as the "initial worldline". Once such entry or intervention occurs, the resulting worldline is no longer the initial worldline.

This does not mean that Interface must physically encounter an impassable barrier. It is a constraint on the identity of the initial worldline.

## Must Not Be Automatically Inferred

- The initial worldline is not an ontologically more real, purer, or more ethically valuable worldline.
- The initial protagonist does not thereby become the only "real version" of other protagonists.
- The initial worldline being the origin of interest is not the same as being the origin of emotion.
- The observation condition of non-initial worldlines cannot be expanded into "all universes must be observed by the terminal AI in order to exist".
- "Discovery" cannot be automatically interpreted by a writing Agent as a complete observer-dependent cosmology.
