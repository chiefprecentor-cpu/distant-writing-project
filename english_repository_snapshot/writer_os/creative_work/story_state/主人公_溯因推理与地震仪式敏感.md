# Protagonist: Abductive Reasoning and Seismograph-Like Sensitivity

## Status and Scope of Application

This file records the protagonist's ability setting already confirmed by the author. It is a cross-worldline character-design commitment, but it does not require the protagonist in every worldline to show it at the same strength, in the same way or with the same degree of self-awareness. Specific differences will be decided after worldlines form.

## Confirmed Ability Core

- One of the protagonist's strengths is abductive reasoning.
- This ability does not appear only as conscious logical analysis; a conclusion may first appear as intuition, unease, a shift in attention or a judgment that "cannot be explained clearly", and may only later be verbalised.
- The ability comes from the protagonist's active or passive observation and lived experience over many years of how environments, human nature, society and organisational structures operate.
- He can be unusually sensitive to the macro-level changes that micro-level signs may indicate. At certain key nodes, this abductive intuition can achieve an effect similar to simple foresight.
- "Seismograph" is the author-side working metaphor for this ability: like a precise instrument sensing a distant earthquake, the protagonist can feel what is happening in a larger system from very weak local changes.

## Must Not Be Automatically Inferred

- This is not a confirmed supernatural prophetic ability, and it does not guarantee that every feeling or judgment is correct.
- Reaching a result similar to foresight does not mean the protagonist can necessarily reconstruct which micro-level signs or intermediate reasoning he used.
- This ability does not automatically determine the protagonist's theoretical content, profession or worldline affiliation, nor does it mean he can always persuade others to accept his judgment.
