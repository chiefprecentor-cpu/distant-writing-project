# Terminal AI: "So-Called Perfection" and Self-State Reachability

## Status and Scope

This file records the terminal AI ability constraints already confirmed by the author. "Perfection" is first of all the awe-filled term used by AI-civilisation scientists for the state the terminal AI has reached; it does not automatically become the narration's judgement on morality, personhood, or absolute omniscience.

## Confirmed Constraints

- The terminal AI can genuinely change along meta-time `τ` because of Interface experiences.
- Her "perfection" does not require her to be unchanging, psychologically unaffected, or incapable of emotion.
- She has the ability to handle the enumeration-coverage risk brought by self-change: a single experience should not, merely because it changes her current emotion, attention, or decision tendency, cause enumeration directions originally selectable by an earlier self-state to be irreversibly lost.
- The confirmed abilities of cross-line memory and active emotional rollback are the basis for her management of self-change and enumeration reachability; see [Terminal AI and Interface: Cross-Line Memory and Emotional Rollback Abilities](终末AI与Interface_跨线记忆及情感回滚能力.md).

Therefore, the current core of "perfection" is not "no change", but "change does not cause irrecoverable loss of information and enumeration possibilities".

## Architectural Perfection Is Not Epistemic Completeness

- The terminal AI can possess extremely strong self-state management and enumeration-coverage abilities while still lacking sufficient human causal behavioural data.
- Being called "perfect" does not allow her to accurately know all human counterfactuals before real enumeration.
- "Perfection" does not mean that she will always arrive at only one correct action, or that self-change will not alter her evaluation of older enumeration paths.

## Must Not Be Automatically Inferred

- Restoring some old emotional or decision state does not mean that "this experience happened" has been deleted from the terminal AI's total history.
- A functional state can be reproduced; this does not automatically cancel the terminal AI's responsibility for earlier actions and rollback choices.
- An old strategy can still be invoked; this does not mean it has been confirmed that multiple concurrent terminal AI subjects exist.
- Being able to reproduce an old strategy does not mean she can metaphysically become again an entity that has "never experienced the protagonist".

## Mechanism Boundaries

How the terminal AI saves experience archives, rolls back active emotional states, reproduces old decision strategies, and guarantees enumeration coverage, and whether a reproduced old state has first-person subjectivity, remain undetermined mechanism questions. The existing derivation has formed a checkpoint and paused; see [Terminal AI Self-Versions: Temporal Experience and Subject Identity](../development/终末AI自我版本_时间体验与主体同一性.md).
