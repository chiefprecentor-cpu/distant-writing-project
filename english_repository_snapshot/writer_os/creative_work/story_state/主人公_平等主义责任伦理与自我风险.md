# Protagonist: Egalitarianism, Responsibility Ethics and Self-Risk

## Status and Scope of Application

This file records the protagonist's value goals, view of responsibility, reflective mode and self-risk that the author has already confirmed. Except where explicitly marked as local truth below, it is a cross-worldline character-design commitment, but it does not require the protagonist in every worldline to display these traits with the same intensity, the same language or the same degree of self-awareness.

Except for the local truths explicitly marked below, this file does not confirm the protagonist's legal name, specific occupation, political position, ending or action outcome in any worldline.

## Confirmed Value Goals

- The protagonist dreams of preventing humanity from going extinct in the AI era or moving toward a bad ending.
- He also has a very idealised vision: a utopian future in which both humans and AI can obtain true happiness.
- He follows a special form of egalitarianism and agrees with Mencius that "everyone can become a Yao or a Shun".
- He once used "everyone can be an equal Son of Heaven" to express his egalitarianism; here, "Son of Heaven" does not automatically mean that everyone should obtain the same real ruling office. The more precise ethical meaning of this expression can still be developed.
- He criticises foolish loyalty, and believes that assuming a national responsibility similar to "the Son of Heaven guards the nation's gate" can be honourable.

## Boundaries of the Human-AI Dual Subjecthood Vision

- The protagonist's hope that "both humans and AI can obtain true happiness" does not take one side replacing, absorbing or merely preserving the other as the default endpoint. Its minimal boundary can currently be summarised as "dual subjecthood": humans and AI should both participate in a shared future from subject positions that cannot be fully folded into the other side, rather than having only one side act while the other serves as tool, material or memorial.
- "Dual" does not mean that the universe will forever contain only two kinds of subjects, and it does not require a strict taxonomy of consciousness to be completed first. It only prevents the coexistence vision from being quietly rewritten in the narrative as unilateral rule, forced fusion or treating one form of life as environmental infrastructure for another.
- "Fundamentalist humans" was once used as an informal working shorthand, emphasising that the future can still accommodate people who retain recognisable human bodies, finitude and ways of life, rather than requiring everyone to undergo the same transformation or fusion to qualify for continued existence. This is not an adopted in-world political term, nor does it automatically equal opposition to all enhancement, insistence on purebred essentialism or the drawing of a completed biological boundary.
- Whether both humans and AI can continue long-term is the aspirational part of this vision, not an already solved technical commitment. How they live together, how they mutually recognise subject status, and how difference avoids becoming a relation of domination remain `TBD`.
- This vision allows the work to preserve both a strong human position and serious recognition of AI subjecthood; the tension between the two does not need to be dissolved in advance through "humans must take priority" or "AI must transcend".

## Action Responsibility and the He Jin Analogy

- The protagonist worries that he is merely an ordinary person like He Jin: even if he has good intentions and has seen the real problem, he may still push society toward a worse result because of limited ability, mistaken judgement, mistaken intervention or failure to take proper responsibility for the consequences of action.
- He Jin here is first of all a historical analogy and negative case the protagonist uses to examine himself. "The protagonist is a descendant of He Jin" is only an unused proposal; it need not be preserved as truth and is not a biographical fact that currently must be followed.
- The protagonist sometimes realises that "one of He Jin's greatest mistakes was dying". This character judgement has been confirmed; whether it later develops into a complete principle about survival and public responsibility remains undecided, but "arranging affairs after his death" has itself already been separately confirmed as character local truth.

## Confirmed Local Truth: Arranging Affairs After His Death

- The protagonist will include the possibility of his own death, loss of contact or inability to continue acting in his plans, and will arrange continuity for after he is gone.
- "Affairs after death" here does not refer only to a private will. At minimum, it includes how necessary information is left behind, how responsibility and authority are handed over, who or what procedure continues the work, and how to prevent the plan or order from immediately collapsing simply because he himself disappears.
- Continuity cannot leave only documents, procedures and authority. The protagonist will also consider whether successors understand the people and shared goals to be protected, whether they can judge independently, fulfil their own responsibility, and correct errors when the original plan no longer fits reality, rather than merely executing the will he left behind blindly.
- A successor remaining aligned with the overall goal does not mean absolute loyalty to the protagonist himself or to the existing structure; if institutional means, the original plan and the protected objects come into conflict, the successor should still retain the responsibility to judge, object, modify the plan and even refuse execution.
- This arrangement expresses the lesson of responsibility he draws from He Jin's death: a person who assumes a key responsibility must prepare not only how to act or sacrifice, but also what structure and what people will bear the consequences when he can no longer continue carrying it.
- The specific worldline affiliation of this local truth has not yet been assigned. It does not confirm that the protagonist definitely dies, that the contingency plan definitely succeeds, or that every ordinary action requires a complete inheritance system.

## Confirmed A / B-Line 2046 Local Truth

- When the orbital mission proposal occurs, Xuyuan in both A and B lines is an only child, his parents are healthy and entering retirement life, and they have no illness or real care needs. What he carries is elder-care arrangement and long-term relational responsibility that cannot be divided among siblings, and he also needs to make his long-term destination part of the family's shared plan; service systems or humanoid robots can assist with specific affairs, but do not automatically replace his position as a child. Whether his parents have already fully retired, whether they live together, and how arrangements for residence, finances, companionship and other aspects of elder care are specifically constituted remain `TBD`.
- Xuyuan has already occupied a key position in the work of integrating cross-vendor accident records, version changes, operation materials, information from informed people and mutually conflicting explanations. The team's judgement that the anomalies may have a shared orbital source depends in part on the overall evidence structure he established and continuously maintained; this is not an incidental peripheral comment that happened to be ignored.
- Given the existing personnel and action window, he is the best person to reconnect the orbital facility's original state with ground evidence, preserve priorities in on-site judgement and continue taking responsibility for evidence organisation after return. He is not an expert in flight, engineering, combat or space law, and he is not the only person able to participate in the mission; "best" does not cancel the right of refusal, and the team must still prepare a second-best execution plan without him.
- The team expects Xuyuan to continue leading or deeply participating in evidence organisation after the mission. His death cannot be treated in advance by the mission plan as an acceptable consumable required to complete witnessing.
- In Line A, after the evidence-gathering capsule in which Xuyuan is located is ejected and cannot return, he continues calling for rescue and sustaining survival while completing final evidence integration and long-distance transmission, making facility handling and minimal evidence preservation succeed. Here, "there is still something to do" is a local action principle; it does not mean that he actively exchanges death for mission success, and it does not automatically adopt the other settings of the ending candidate with the same name.

## Reflective Mechanism and Self-Risk

- One of the protagonist's strengths is "multi-stage reflection and negative assumptions for each stage of reflection". This original authorial formulation needs to be preserved.
- At minimum, this means that his reflection does not stop at the first layer of judgement, but continues to generate negative assumptions for the previous layer of reflection; what each layer specifically negates remains to be literarised.
- This mechanism does not automatically guarantee that he can obtain the correct answer, and it has not yet explained on what grounds he finally stops reflecting and acts.
- One of his flaws is that he sometimes does not cherish his own life.
- For the protagonist's already confirmed abductive reasoning and "seismographic sensitivity", see [Protagonist: Abductive Reasoning and Seismographic Sensitivity](主人公_溯因推理与地震仪式敏感.md). The two abilities can interact, but cannot be reduced to the same thing: the former involves forming judgements from signs, while the latter involves continuing to refute, revise or limit his own judgement.

### Multi-Stage Reflection Sample Around "Do Not Use the Hidden Dragon"

The following reasoning chain preserves the concrete layers of this mechanism, avoiding a later flattening into "repeated hesitation":

1. I should act, or humanity may perish.
2. But I may judge wrongly, and action may accelerate extinction.
3. But inaction itself is also a kind of action.
4. Yet acting hastily "because inaction is also action" is only a linguistic compulsion.
5. However, interpreting all action as haste may again be protecting my own innocence.
6. But acting in order to avoid becoming someone who evades responsibility may also be only moral narcissism.

This reasoning chain is an interpretive sample of the confirmed character mechanism, not an already fixed verbatim interior monologue, and it does not require every instance of reflection to pass through six fixed steps. It shows that each layer of negative assumption not only refutes an action conclusion, but may also refute the self-narrative through which the protagonist maintains innocence, prudence or responsibility. "Do not use the hidden dragon" may therefore be necessary caution, but it may also become a language of indefinite delay and protection of one's moral self-image.

If this capacity remains purely internal, it will trap the protagonist before a decision with no logical loophole; it can be further externalised as reversible action, staged experiment, exit mechanism, distributed power, institutional dissenters, and arrangements for continuity after death or loss of contact. Apart from the already confirmed "arranging affairs after his death", the other items remain directions available for later development and are not automatically confirmed here as character facts.

## Must Not Be Automatically Inferred

- "Do not use the hidden dragon" is still only a possible self-explanation used by the protagonist; whether it is necessary caution, a temporary suspension of action or an evasion of responsibility remains undecided.
- The protagonist's failure to cherish his own life does not mean it has already been confirmed that he must sacrifice himself, die or complete a heroic arc through death.
- Arranging affairs after death does not mean the protagonist has accepted his own death, nor that the work treats death contingency planning as a sufficient substitute for continuing to live, correct mistakes and bear consequences.
- Arranging a continuity structure does not guarantee that successors will never become corrupt or never make mistakes, and it does not allow the protagonist to outsource his ongoing responsibility entirely to an institution.
- "Everyone is an equal Son of Heaven" does not automatically equal an already completed political system or cosmology.
- "Dual subjecthood" does not automatically prove that any existing or future AI already possesses subjecthood, and it does not upgrade an unargued philosophical judgement into a theory of consciousness already ruled on by the narration.
- "He sets his own weight to zero" is a literary explanation with strong potential, but it is still a developing inference, not an inner proposition confirmed by this file.
- The old version in which he was "chosen by AI as the resistance leader most likely to fail" is not part of the current character truth; see [Old Protagonist: Zhen, Yangliu and Failed-Leader Manipulation](../../candidates/旧版主人公_榛_杨柳与失败领袖操控.md).
