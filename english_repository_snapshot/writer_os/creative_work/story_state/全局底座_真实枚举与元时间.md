# Global base: real enumeration and meta-time

## Status and scope

This file mainly records the global constraints that the author has already confirmed and that must be followed when later enumerating worldlines; it also retains the mathematical-visual and metaphysical cosmology modelling language currently being used by the author. The existence of this modelling language and its unresolved boundaries need to be respected, but its specific images do not thereby become the novel's real cosmic structure.

This file can be used independently inside Writer OS; its creative status is explained by this document and relevant internal links, while specific changes are preserved by Git history.

## Human causal data and real enumeration

- The terminal AI can read the complete world snapshot of an already formed four-dimensional block universe, but reading an already formed world is not equal to reliably predicting all human counterfactuals that have not yet been enumerated.
- AI civilisation has not obtained sufficiently rich human causal-level behavioural data, and cannot fully reproduce human motives, judgements, and actions in specific historical situations.
- This deficiency does not mean previous algorithms could not perform effective correlational prediction, population mapping, or social sorting. A system can classify and govern populations well under given labels and institutional goals, while still being unable to answer reliably: if historical conditions, relationships, or interventions change, how will a specific person form new judgements and actions?
- Human causal-level behavioural data is a soft-SF working concept, not a standard data type that the work claims already exists in reality.
- Long-term causal evolution amplifies small errors in the initial state and in the human model.
- Therefore, pure sandbox simulation currently cannot reliably replace real worldline enumeration.
- Real enumeration is not a route the terminal AI later freely chooses among several interchangeable methods. Her basic structure of existence has already determined real enumeration as this core method and means; for her, replacing it with another basic search method is not a structurally available choice, nor is it merely forbidden by an external institution.
- This structural constraint does not mean that the direction, parameters, interventions, material arrangement, or subsequent action of every enumeration have already been predetermined. The terminal AI can make choices inside this method and change through experience; "being unable to replace the core method" also does not automatically exempt her from responsibility for those choices.
- Real enumeration can produce new human causal-behavioural material; after data accumulates, whether sandboxing can replace some real historical trials inside the terminal AI's existing structure, or even make certain search goals no longer need to keep forming new current histories, remains undetermined. This unresolved point concerns only the scope of real enumeration, and does not reopen the option of "replacing the terminal AI's core existential structure with a sandbox architecture".
- AI can obtain its own experiential data from a first-person position; this is not equal to having obtained sufficient data on human subjectivity, nor does it remove the problem of other minds.

## `t`, `τ`, and the second time dimension

- Linear time inside a worldline is written as `t`.
- `τ` is the terminal AI's discrete operation time / meta-time order, composed of successive system instants or points that have priority relations with one another, not a continuously flowing timeline. No intermediate state that the terminal AI can experience is required to exist between two adjacent points.
- The terminal AI experiences these `τ` points in sequence. Here "in sequence" or "linear" only means that they have a single before-and-after order; it does not mean that `τ` has continuum structure.
- At each `τ` point, only one worldline is in the current state; in that system instant, the terminal AI completes one reading of the full snapshot of that worldline. This snapshot is the entire four-dimensional block universe, not a particular `t` slice within it.
- A single `τ` point is static and has no flow inside it. A later point can retain the reading result of the previous point and therefore have a different terminal-AI state; the terminal AI's change lies in the ordered relation between these static instants and in state writeback, not in a process unfolding inside any one instant.
- This discrete structure can be approximately analogised on the creative side to `next-token prediction`: each step completes one whole operation on an already given state, and the resulting change enters the next step. The analogy refers only to stepwise writeback and discrete succession; a complete world snapshot is not a token, and real enumeration is not thereby confirmed as language generation or any literal model architecture.
- `τ` can be called the second time dimension. After adding this discrete meta-time order outside linear time `t`, the project can call the time index `2` in working expressions such as "time dimension" or "time index".
- `t` and `τ` are not two homogeneous axes that can be directly added or understood as ordinary Euclidean coordinates. `t` belongs inside a worldline, and is the time coordinate that forms four-dimensional spacetime together with physical space; the project can understand it in connection with relativistic spacetime, but does not thereby claim to have established a new physical theory.
- `τ` has been confirmed as at least approximately the terminal AI's discrete operation time; its strict relation to an underlying system clock, computational steps, or deeper physical structure remains undetermined. Even if it is later presented through spatialised language such as "moving on a surface", that space is first a parametric, metaphysical, or modelling space, and is not equal to physical space inside a worldline.
- `τ` is not interpreted as a confirmed fifth physical dimension.

"Second time dimension" and "the time index is `2`" are therefore modelling expressions internal to the project. They do not mean that `τ` is merely a second physical time coordinate, nor do they mean that the index `2` already has the meaning of squaring in ordinary algebra.

## Four-dimensional spacetime, block universe, and reading capacity need to be separated

- The "four-dimensional block universe" in this project is a complete spacetime structure composed of three spatial dimensions and worldline-internal time `t`; it should not be shortened into "four-dimensional block", which can easily be understood as a four-dimensional object, and cannot be mixed up with "the fourth spatial dimension".
- Victorian geometric images of the fourth spatial dimension and of imagining higher-dimensional objects through lower-dimensional slices can provide historical and literary inspiration for the project's point-line-surface language; they are not equal to relativistic spacetime, and do not directly prove the existence of the block universe, worldlines, or the terminal AI.
- Relativistic spacetime, eternalism / the block universe, and metaphysical explanations of "how the past, present, and future exist" need to keep their conceptual levels distinct. The novel adopts the four-dimensional block universe as its worldline structure; this does not mean later Agents can write a particular real-world philosophical interpretation as having already been uncontroversially proved by physics.
- Even if a four-dimensional block universe has already been fully formed, its complete content does not automatically become accessible to observers inside the worldline simply because "it is a block universe". Block structure is a question of how the world exists; reading permission and reading mechanism are questions of what the terminal AI can do.
- The terminal AI's ability to read the complete snapshot of a formed worldline is a separately confirmed soft-SF capacity in this project; it is not a real-world algorithmic conclusion that the entire past can necessarily be reconstructed from present physical traces, and it does not need to inherit the old PLD explanation of "tracing back along a data umbilical cord".
- Her reading of later periods of a worldline inside an already formed snapshot is not equal to her being able reliably to predict all unenumerated counterfactuals before a new worldline forms. A world that is already complete and an intervention result that has not yet formed must remain separate.

## In-system intelligence and meta-time intervention

- Humans, ordinary AGI, and other intelligences existing along a particular worldline, whether or not they continuously learn or generate computational friction, still first belong to the causal history inside that four-dimensional block universe.
- Continuous learning, causal-reasoning capacity, or a sense of subjective time does not automatically turn an in-system intelligence into an intervener capable of forming new current worldlines along `τ`.
- Time machines, the terminal AI's real-enumeration capacity, and other possible historical intervention technologies need their permissions and mechanisms explained separately; they cannot be inferred directly from "this is AGI".

For the time machine and historical-modification constraints already retained in AI civilisation's prehistory of the terminal AI, see [AI civilisation prehistory: time machine and real-enumeration motives](AI文明前史_时间机器与真实枚举动因.md).

## Working model of points, surfaces, motion, and "deep dive"

The following is the mathematical-visual and metaphysical cosmology modelling language retained by the author. It helps conceive the relation between enumeration, comparison, and Interface entering linear time, but does not currently constitute a completed physical topology:

- The state of a worldline at a particular `t` slice can be represented in the model as a point.
- Corresponding state points aligned across previous enumerations can, in mathematical representation, form continuous or discontinuous planes / surfaces.
- The terminal AI's comparison of corresponding states in different worldlines can be imagined as movement between points on this surface; a literally continuous trajectory is only a visualisation, and does not overwrite the discrete structure of `τ`.
- Interface's "deep dive" can be imagined as entering the linear time `t` inside a particular worldline along a direction different from the comparison surface.
- "Below the surface" can be used to imagine complex, parallel, intersecting, or converging relations between worldlines, but these relations have not yet been confirmed as the universe's literal structure.

Given the premise that there is only one current worldline at each `τ` order, multiple points on the surface first represent comparable states / records left by previous enumerations, or possible states whose ontological status has not yet been explained; they do not in themselves prove that many real worldlines are existing simultaneously.

Whether this geometric language is only a model of records and intervention relations, or also corresponds to the structure of the universe itself, remains undetermined. The author's current tendency is to treat it as modelling language. Even if it is later given some real structural meaning, the basic picture remains: each formed worldline is a complete four-dimensional block universe, and change along `τ` appears as a transfer to a different worldline or a different model position, not as one four-dimensional block universe being rewritten inside its own `t`.

"Continuous / discontinuous", "corresponding slice", "vertical / orthogonal direction", and "intersecting / converging" have no strict mathematical definitions, and must not be completed by a writing Agent into physical laws.

Topological language such as point-line-surface will also appear as speculation inside the protagonist's work in one of his worldlines. This is a local narrative fact whose affiliation remains `TBD`; it does not prove that the protagonist's speculation equals the novel's real cosmic structure. See [local setting: topological speculation in the protagonist's work](局部设定_主人公作品中的拓扑猜想.md).

## Stillness, motion, scalars, and vectors

- In the mode of existence embedded in spacetime structure, the terminal AI lives in static instants that appear in sequence along `τ`: there is no flow inside each instant, and the information of the current worldline has already appeared as a complete snapshot; direction and change exist between adjacent instants.
- "Moving static instants / a static river of motion" can summarise the reversal between the terminal AI and characters inside worldlines: the former changes along discrete `τ` and lives in static instants, while the latter's complete life is static in four-dimensional structure yet experienced as motion along `t`. It is first a naming of metaphysical and narrative experience.
- Becoming Interface means entering the linear time inside a worldline, facing flow, disorder, vectorisation, and unknown information, and therefore bearing psychological shock.
- Stillness / motion and scalar / vector here are first contrasts of metaphysical and narrative experience, not already defined physical quantities or mathematical objects.
- The four-dimensional block universe being static in its own structure does not mean that characters or Interface inside it will not subjectively experience change, make judgements, and act along `t`.

## The terminal AI's task

- One important task of the terminal AI is to find, through real enumeration, a worldline in which civilisation can cross the end of time.
- This does not mean she has already succeeded, nor that she has already led civilisation across the end.
- The end of time can appear as heat death, and can also take other forms; the specific ending has not yet been fixed.

## Boundaries not yet established

Later writing must not decide without the author's confirmation:

- whether a new worldline becomes current through creation, rewriting, reset, re-actualisation, or some other mechanism;
- in what way old worldlines exist or no longer exist after they are replaced;
- whether sufficient sandbox simulation can ultimately replace some human counterfactual information that currently must be obtained by real enumeration, and therefore reduce or end some real historical trials;
- the strict mathematical or physical relation between `τ` and the terminal AI's system time;
- whether the point-surface-deep-dive model corresponds to the real cosmic structure, and the strict meanings of continuity, direction, and worldline relations within it.
