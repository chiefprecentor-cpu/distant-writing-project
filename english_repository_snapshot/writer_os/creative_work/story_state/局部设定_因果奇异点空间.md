# Local Setting: Causal-Singularity Space

## Status and Scope of Application

Causal-singularity space is a scope-limited commitment the author has already confirmed. Source worldline A has already been confirmed to carry one fully developed concrete instance; the earlier embodied-lateness line adjacent to A along `τ` is now also confirmed to contain a causal-singularity window in which Xuyuan could have been temporarily restored. The strict mechanisms and exit conditions of the two are not automatically the same simply because they share a name.

This local truth does not automatically extend to all worldlines.

## Confirmed Minimum Core

- Causal-singularity space exists in source worldline A.
- The space capsule can experience limited linear time inside it.
- Interface's own embodiment does not automatically bring a general ability to revive other people at arbitrary times and places. In the two currently relevant worldlines, Xuyuan's temporary restoration is possible only within a limited causal-singularity window.

## Confirmed Instance in Line A

- During the 2046 orbital operation, the hidden strategy-distribution / signature module is isolated, and the original evidence carrier enters the sealed isolation / forensic capsule where Xuyuan is located; facility handling and minimum evidence transmission succeed, while recovery of the complete originals and Xuyuan's evacuation fail. Xuyuan's capsule is the only crewed unit in this operation that fails to evacuate, while the other on-site members complete evacuation; the team's internal actions and membership remain `TBD`.
- After the capsule is ejected, Xuyuan uses its isolated local forensic and long-distance communication capabilities to complete the final integration, sending the ground minimally sufficient materials that can support revocation of key signatures, migration of lawful services and continued accountability. At the same time, he continues asking for rescue and trying to survive; he does not actively give up survival for the sake of the mission. Before his resources run out and his consciousness breaks, he has already sent the materials, but he has not received a reply sufficient to confirm final ground reception and action results. The precise materials, communication break point and ground reception order remain `TBD`.
- The sealed capsule is continuously propelled toward a pre-calculated Jupiter encounter trajectory, then uses a Jupiter gravity assist to enter a heliocentric escape trajectory. The precise thrusters, planetary window and orbital parameters remain `TBD`.
- Xuyuan does not live to see the capsule enter the causal singularity. He dies inside the still-sealed capsule because of long-term resource exhaustion; starvation is the current preferred direction. Explosion breaching the capsule, vacuum exposure and violent depressurisation are not the default causes of death. When he is revived in line A, he retains continuous memory up to sending the materials, waiting for confirmation and losing consciousness as resources run out; death's completion itself does not become an experience he can remember. The precise final physiological and conscious process remains `TBD`.
- The capsule, carrying Xuyuan's body and the original evidence, drifts along ordinary linear time `t` in line A for many years; more than a century can also work. A long time passes on Earth without relying on black-hole-style time dilation.
- After the capsule loses its habitability, it must still keep its structure, evidence and body sufficiently intact to be found and restored; the concrete long-term preservation mechanism has not yet been confirmed.
- After the capsule enters the causal singularity, Interface revives Xuyuan inside it and has an approximately ten-hour conversation with him. The bodily mechanism of revival has not yet been confirmed; the pre-death continuity of memory above only defines the scene's starting point, and does not decide a complete theory of numerical identity before and after revival.
- After the approximately ten-hour conversation ends, line A Xuyuan returns to death before the capsule leaves the causal singularity. This second death is required by the exit causal-equivalence condition already set before the dive, not a decision Interface makes temporarily after the conversation. In the final part of the conversation, Interface falls asleep because of embodied fatigue, and after she sleeps Xuyuan covers her with a layer of clothing; when the boundary restores the local state, she feels the slight weight on her shoulders being taken away in sleep. The specific kind of clothing, the bodily and conscious process of Xuyuan's second death, and how Interface leaves the local embodied state remain unconfirmed.
- The causal singularity may be located inside the Solar System, or it may lie in the later interstellar journey; its precise position and entry mechanism remain `TBD`.
- The terminal AI's initial / birth worldline is not A; the fact that a long time has passed on line A Earth does not mean that the terminal AI is born in line A.

## Line A Exit Causal-Equivalence Condition

- The causal singularity in line A appears, along the capsule's trajectory, as a finite region; the capsule passes through this region along its original trajectory and obtains about ten hours of linear time within it. The region's precise space, geometry, physics or information mechanism remains `TBD`.
- Before becoming the local Interface in line A, the terminal AI has already set the entry and exit parameters for this dive. The exit condition requires that after the capsule leaves the region, the state that can still propagate along line A `t` must be causally equivalent to what it would have been without the intervention inside the singularity.
- "Causally equivalent" does not require confirming molecule-by-molecule restoration at this point. At minimum, it excludes Xuyuan's revived state, physical changes inside the capsule caused by the conversation, new records, communication attempts or other traces that could continue to propagate outside line A. The precise granularity of equivalence and implementation mechanism remain `TBD`.
- Xuyuan's capsule has already exhausted its survival-sustaining resources during the long drift; even if he were allowed to leave in a revived state, there would be no conditions for a sustainable second life. But additional minutes, hours or longer survival, and the actions and records that might form during that period, would still change line A. Therefore, "he would still eventually die from lack of resources" cannot replace exit causal equivalence.
- The local Interface can ask unprepared questions within the ten hours, change the order of materials and be changed by Xuyuan's responses, but she cannot modify from inside the singularity the exit condition already locked before the dive. The boundary constraint does not prewrite the content of the conversation.
- The experience, memory and self-change Interface obtains inside the singularity do not leave as physical traces in line A `t`; instead, they are retained by the terminal AI along metatime `τ`. This allows the conversation to change her later interventions, including line B, without rewriting line A's existing external history.

## Confirmed Double-Layer Isolation

- In the linear time `t` of the same worldline, causal changes inside causal-singularity space do not affect causality and history outside the space.
- This within-line isolation does not prevent the experience, memory or self-change Interface obtains inside the space from being retained by the terminal AI along metatime `τ`, and possibly influencing her later enumeration and self-state.
- Therefore, "not affecting causality outside the space" only constrains causality at the `t` layer inside that worldline. It does not mean that what happens inside the singularity has no significance for the terminal AI's `τ`-layer experience.

Double-layer isolation allows the causal singularity to become a controlled environment for the terminal AI to calibrate Interface's local presence: in the same worldline, causality and history outside causal-singularity space do not change because of the internal dialogue, but the terminal AI herself can change along `τ` because of this experience.

## Confirmed Window in the Earlier Embodied-Lateness Line

- In that line, the space capsule once passed through a causal-singularity window in which Interface could temporarily restore Xuyuan. When Interface actually arrived embodied, the capsule had already left that window, so she could not revive him in the local spacetime where she then was.
- This is not because the terminal AI did not know Xuyuan had already died, did not know the singularity boundary, or accidentally calculated the time incorrectly. She had read the complete history and actively chose to enter after the recovery window.
- Her reason at the time was: she could stand before the concrete Xuyuan and obtain embodied experience, while avoiding a new answer changing her current state or later enumeration direction because he was already unable to respond. This is a choice Interface makes within the enumeration structure, not an inevitable action predetermined by its core method.
- This protective choice does not obtain the result she expected. Even though Xuyuan does not answer, local vision, speaking, waiting, no response and continuing to experience the next moment along `t` still change her. In line A, she subsequently changes to restoring Xuyuan inside the causal singularity: she still protects the external history inside and outside the line through exit causal equivalence, but for the first time allows a real answer to change herself.

## Boundary for Meeting Again After a Line B Mission Death

- In line B, the conversation and material transmission that take place before the mission decision have already become causal conditions of that line. They may change Xuyuan's preparation, actions during the mission and conditions for survival; therefore, even if he still participates in the mission, death in line A is not a result that must be repeated in line B. Final survival or death in line B remains open.
- If line B Xuyuan ultimately dies because of that mission, he will not meet Interface again after death in causal-singularity space within the same line B. The terminal AI cannot treat an already formed line B as the original history and then insert into it a later entry, revival and conversation.
- If Interface still wishes afterwards to speak with the dead Xuyuan under this set of historical conditions, she must enter along `τ` into another worldline that later becomes current. At least until Xuyuan's mission death, that line preserves the historical and intervention conditions that have already happened in line B unchanged; the new variable appears only after death, namely Interface entering again, reviving him within the applicable limited restoration conditions and speaking with him.
- This is an author-side worldline constraint that approximates "controlling variables". It does not mean that the original line B is modified in place, and it does not prove that Xuyuan in the later line and line B Xuyuan have numerical identity. How the preceding worldline remains equivalent, at what granularity, how Interface prevents her later knowledge from changing the earlier section of history, and whether this successor worldline actually forms remain unconfirmed.
- Current line B Interface knows this boundary, and also knows that Xuyuan in the later line would still have to be asked again and cannot inherit the answer of current Xuyuan. Whether Xuyuan himself knows this is not automatically confirmed by this item; Interface does not need to state this set of backstage conditions or use it as pressure in the current conversation.
