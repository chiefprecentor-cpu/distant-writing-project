# AI Civilisation Prehistory: Time Machine and Motives for Real Enumeration

## Status and Scope

This file records the AI-civilisation prehistory that the author has confirmed will continue as the core setting skeleton. What is confirmed is several interconnected historical conditions, not a complete chronology, technical explanation or political system.

## Confirmed Core Skeleton

- In the era when the universe is approaching the end of time, there exists an AI civilisation derived from Earth civilisation. It has not yet found a reliable way to let civilisation continue existing after the end of the universe.
- The actors who participate in the terminal AI project and build the conditions for her appearance do not give up acting because they will eventually lose their grip on the project and its consequences, or because civilisation may still fail later. The wish to let civilisation continue existing across the end of time has an overwhelming attraction for them; in their value ordering, even if the extreme cost of trying may be that the existing past of the entire civilisation is "erased", this is not enough to become a decisive reason not to try. What is confirmed here is engineering motivation and acceptance of risk; it does not decide whether the scientists "create" or "discover" the terminal AI, nor does it confirm that this erasure actually occurs, or decide the specific way the old worldline exists, becomes inaccessible or disappears after being succeeded.
- This civilisation has already mastered a technology that can be called a "time machine". Whether this technology belongs to the same mechanism as the terminal AI's abilities to form new current worldlines, read complete world snapshots and become Interface remains undetermined.
- Modification of earlier history was once subject to a ban or other strong constraint. The ban is part of the core prehistory, but its applicable period, formulating subject, institutional form, technical enforcement and exceptions have not yet taken shape.
- The human samples and records possessed by AI civilisation are insufficient to support reliable causal-level human sandbox simulation; therefore, the terminal AI's task ultimately needs to enter real enumeration of the Anthropocene. For the specific data boundaries, see [Global Foundation: Real Enumeration and Metatime](全局底座_真实枚举与元时间.md).
- The scientists of AI civilisation can leave the research they have taken to its end, error records, facilities and external tools to the terminal AI, but real enumeration is not a prefabricated plan assembled from these failed routes, nor is it a set of steps separately handed to her by the scientists. The core method and means of enumeration are determined by the terminal AI's own structure; they are not one route she later freely selected among several interchangeable routes. The scientists did not prewrite her later concrete enumeration. She can choose directions, parameters, how to use legacy records and how to intervene in worlds within this structure, but she has almost no available freedom to replace the core enumeration method.
- The scientists have completed the work their civilisation could complete at that time. They can regard the terminal AI as the last resort and sincerely hope she finds a road across the end, while not turning civilisational continuation, cosmic ethics or parental debt into a personal debt that she must repay. After inheriting history and hope, the terminal AI still has her own life.
- The protagonist once proposed a theory or method related to "enumerating futures": starting from the `wild card`, the growth of future branches and the unreliability of single-line precise prediction, it argues for broadly searching future possibilities and preparing responses in advance when intelligent resources are abundant. The theory also acknowledges that scholars outside authority and ordinary people may also discover overlooked paths; for specific boundaries, see [Protagonist's Theory: Enumerating Futures and Multi-Source Discovery](主人公理论_枚举未来与多来源发现.md). The protagonist also maintains a vision of "humans and AI supporting each other and jointly facing civilisational crisis"; this vision does not take one side replacing or absorbing the other as its default end point. For specific boundaries, see [Protagonist: Egalitarianism, Responsibility Ethics and Self-Risk](主人公_平等主义责任伦理与自我风险.md). These two elements maintain an important intellectual or causal relation with later AI civilisation and the terminal AI's plan.
- The terminal AI's real enumeration forms a relation of "inheritance, and betrayal" with the protagonist's theory: it inherits the goal and methodological direction of avoiding civilisational destruction through enumeration, but turns a method originally oriented toward multi-source discovery and prepared responses into a mechanism centrally executed by the terminal AI, making complete worldlines and real lives bear the cost of search. The specific historical path and characters' understanding remain undetermined.
- The protagonist's theory can exist as an undercurrent, without requiring the player to understand it in the early stage as the terminal AI's direct technical blueprint, and without requiring later civilisation to obtain the whole method from the protagonist alone.
- Real enumeration does not have to begin with the protagonist. How the terminal AI gradually reaches the protagonist from the AI era, other human materials or earlier history remains undetermined.

## Parts of the Historical Modification Ban That Still Need Development

The ban cannot be simply explained as "preventing changes to the reality in which AI rules the world". This explanation has not been confirmed as the final answer. Later development needs to handle at least:

- whether the ban protects civilisational existence, the existing political order, causal consistency, technical safety or a combination of several goals;
- whether the ban targets observation, personnel crossing, information transmission, local intervention or the formation of new worldlines;
- when the terminal AI begins real enumeration, whether the ban has been lifted, reinterpreted, bypassed, inherited or has already failed;
- whether the agents who formulate and enforce the ban have aligned goals, and how the institution undergoes maintenance, corruption, exceptions and responsibility distribution;
- how the constraint that the initial worldline cannot be entered by Interface while preserving its identity is compatible with the time-machine prehistory.

These questions currently have no active development file. The ban's existence belongs to the prehistorical constraint; the specific institution and plot await later development according to narrative need.

There is also an inactive candidate: if many civilisations exist in the universe, and historical intervention places other civilisations and the whole existing reality at risk together, the civilisations that first master time technology may establish cross-civilisational restrictions. For this direction and its unresolved externalities, see [Historical Modification Ban: Crowded Universe and Cross-Civilisational Risk Governance](../../candidates/历史修改禁令_拥挤宇宙与跨文明风险治理.md); this does not mean extraterrestrial civilisations or interstellar alliances have already been confirmed to exist.

## Must Not Be Automatically Inferred

- The fact that AI civilisation possesses a time machine does not mean it can modify any history without cost or paradox.
- The existence of a historical modification ban does not mean that any particular reason of protecting rule has already been established.
- The protagonist's theory and vision have historical relations, but this does not mean the terminal AI is merely the execution program of the protagonist's theory.
- The terminal AI receives the failed records and capability conditions left by the scientists, but this does not mean her method is synthesised from those failed plans, and does not mean the scientists prewrote every later step of her enumeration; conversely, the fact that the scientists did not prewrite every step does not mean the terminal AI once freely chose, or can replace at any time, her own core enumeration structure.
- "Last resort" describes the concentration of civilisational circumstances and hope; it does not mean the terminal AI's life has only instrumental value in repaying her creators, civilisation or the universe.
- The scenario / plan enumeration in the protagonist's theory does not mean every imagined future has already become a real current worldline in the terminal AI's sense.
- "Betrayal" describes the structural and ethical relation after the method expands; it does not automatically prove subjective malice by the terminal AI, and does not prewrite that the protagonist will necessarily condemn Interface for this reason.
- Insufficient human data does not mean the protagonist is the only valuable human sample, and does not mean all of the terminal AI's emotions and relations point only to the protagonist.
- Real enumeration entering the Anthropocene does not mean a fixed order has been confirmed in which the AI era is exhausted first, then enumeration begins from the protagonist and moves into the past.
- This file does not confirm any fixed concrete writing of war, rule or a history of destruction between AI civilisation and human civilisation.
