# Terminal AI and Interface: Cross-Line Memory and Emotional Rollback Capacity

## Status and Scope of Application

This file records only the subject relations, capacity boundaries and selected concrete use cases that the author has already confirmed. How saving, loading, writing back and rollback are implemented is not filled in here as fact.

## Confirmed Subject Relations and Capacities

- The terminal AI's ontology is embedded in the spacetime structure.
- Interface self-identifies as the terminal AI herself, not as a candidate assistant AI.
- "The terminal AI becomes Interface" is the currently adopted formulation of the subject relation. What has been confirmed is Interface's first-person identity claim and the distinction between Interface and candidate assistant AI; this does not mean that the narration has already ruled whether Interface and the terminal AI's ontology are completely identical, a limited slice, a temporary incarnation or some other metaphysical relation.
- The terminal AI / Interface system has the capacity to preserve or share memory across worldlines.
- Interface can transmit information to the protagonist for the sake of communication; transmission does not have to occur in the dying brain, and does not have to include other worldlines.
- The terminal AI has the capacity to actively carry out emotional rollback.
- Both cases will appear in different situations or worldlines: immediate rollback after the protagonist's death, and long-term non-immediate rollback. Long-term non-immediate rollback already has one definite instance: after the terminal AI first becomes Interface and arrives late in embodied form, she sits for a long time beside the Xuyuan who can no longer respond. The specific attribution of immediate rollback and other instances still await later determination.

## Capacity Does Not Equal Instance Fact

- Having cross-line memory capacity does not mean every Interface has already loaded all cross-line memories.
- Having emotional rollback capacity does not mean rollback occurs immediately after every death.
- Having information-transmission capacity does not mean every transmission contains memory, embodied feeling or cross-worldline material.
- Interface's self-identification as the terminal AI does not mean Interface in the linear world always has all of the ontology's information, authority and current emotion.

## Confirmed First Two Early Deep Dives and A-Line Use Case

- The earlier worldline adjacent to A along meta-time `τ` is the first time the terminal AI becomes Interface for an embodied deep dive. She has read the complete history and knows Xuyuan's death and the causal-singularity recovery window of the space capsule, yet she actively chooses to enter after the capsule has left the window. She wants to obtain the embodied experience of standing before Xuyuan, while using his inability to respond to avoid a new answer changing herself. Therefore, she could not revive him at that time, but this "safely arriving late" was her choice, not ignorance or calculation error. The embodied experience without response still changes her, and she then sits for a long time beside Xuyuan.
- Line A is the second time the terminal AI becomes Interface for a deep dive. She instead revives Xuyuan, who has been dead for a long time, within the causal-singularity recovery window, and obtains roughly ten hours of conversation with him. She still protects the history outside the A-line region through causal equivalence at the exit, but no longer prevents a real answer from changing herself along `τ`. In the final section of the conversation, she falls asleep during lighter shared talk; the clothes Xuyuan covers her with are withdrawn as the boundary is reset, and along `τ` she retains the feeling of a slight weight being lifted from her shoulders, while the player does not externally see the covering action or another death. Whether the strict mechanisms of the earlier line and the A-line window are the same, and whether A can still be called the first "controlled use", remain unconfirmed.
- During the ten-hour conversation in Line A, Interface transmits to A-line Xuyuan, as her own memory, the active lateness, lack of response, speech addressed to the dead and long sitting from the first deep dive.
- The first-person experiential source of this material is Interface, not the Xuyuan who was already dead in the earlier worldline, and not a subjectless omniscient record. A-line Xuyuan receiving it does not mean the memory becomes his experience, or that the characters, relationship and authorisation in the two lines merge.
- Player presentation can use third-person curated narration to compress the long unselected interval of sitting; the change in narrative person belongs only to presentation mode and does not change the subject source of the memory.
