## Confirmed Human Opening

- The player first watches a stretch of human life in which Interface does not appear at Xuyuan's physical location, extending from the collision to the Kew refusal, the London aftermath, Hyde Park sleep and terminal hard cut.
- Specific plot events, changes in psychological access, the job-search menu and character relationships are not repeated in this file; defer to the near-final draft and its story-state checkpoint.
- Yuankai does not bring Xuyuan before Interface in the opening; later, in line B, he participates in the evidence and action argument for the orbital mission and passes on the request, but he still only makes the request worth hearing. He does not decide for Xuyuan.

## Currently Adopted Source / Intervention Continuation

### Source Worldline A

- A is a source worldline in which Interface does not intervene in advance in the orbital mission choice. It is not the initial worldline in which the terminal AI is born, and it also does not become B because it is later read.
- In a later stage of A, Yuankai hands over the orbital data-centre mission, and Xuyuan accepts and participates. The action objective and minimum evidence transmission succeed, while recovery of the complete originals and Xuyuan's evacuation fail; his capsule is the only crewed unit that does not evacuate. After Xuyuan dies, he drifts for a long time with the capsule, enters the causal singularity, and is then revived by Interface for an approximately ten-hour conversation. The current constraints for the mission and conversation are concentrated in [Xuyuan and Interface](../development/主人公与Interface_溯因直觉镜像及进入世界的目的.md) and [Causal-Singularity Story State](../story_state/局部设定_因果奇异点空间.md).

### The Terminal AI's Reading and Curation

- Before diving into A, what the terminal AI has read is an earlier worldline adjacent to A along `τ`, not A itself. That earlier line has already formed a complete four-dimensional world snapshot, and it is also the line in which she first becomes Interface in embodied lateness and sits for a long time. The capsule in that line once passed through a causal-singularity window that could have temporarily restored Xuyuan; she knows where the window and death are, but to avoid a new answer changing herself, she actively chooses to enter after the capsule has left the window. A is the second deep dive; the ten hours in A's causal singularity are not in the records she has previously read, but are new experience obtained by the local Interface along `τ`.
- After the ten hours in line A are complete, the terminal AI can arrange the line A materials, including this new experience, for Xuyuan later in line B. Pre-reading the adjacent earlier line, gaining new experience in A, and later curating A are three distinct events in sequence.
- The hint shot inserted after Hyde Park can occur after the terminal AI has already completed reading the entire source line and arranging the materials; the fact that it appears early in `π` does not mean that the terminal AI at that time only knows the history before Hyde Park.
- The terminal AI arranges the source life as material that Xuyuan in another worldline can receive and gradually understand. She can choose information and order, but she cannot decide what meaning the receiver will generate from the material.

### Mission-Intervention Worldline B

- In another non-initial worldline, Interface has a human prehistory approximately similar to the source line, but this does not constitute an in-place rewrite of A.
- In B, Interface intervenes in the orbital mission choice that has already occurred in A, and asks Xuyuan not to go; the request can be refuted or refused, and cannot acquire decision-making authority automatically from complete history.
- In B, Yuankai participates in the evidence and action argument and makes the mission request worth hearing, but he does not accept it for Xuyuan; Interface is another active agent, not someone Yuankai brings before Xuyuan.
- Interface's current visible design outside the door in line B is: grey-brown medium-long hair, braided on one side and tied with a black hair ornament, grey-purple eyes, a pale knitted cardigan and a dark pleated skirt. The whole impression is too young, tidy and classic, yet she still looks only like an ordinary young visitor. The clothing is not directly identified as a school uniform, and the pupils have no mechanical details; only after sustained observation does Xuyuan feel that her attention does not go through the ordinary processes of searching, distraction and settling back. The current design adopts only the visible appearance, and does not treat text or setting explanation accompanying the visual design as story fact.
- She can transmit source-line materials, but displaying one result does not automatically prove that a character should not act; the receiver may also generate reasons from the same material that she did not expect.
- If the terminal AI only needed an external result, she would not have to enter in person as Interface. The complete reason for local presence is concentrated in [Xuyuan and Interface: Abductive Intuition and the Purpose of Entering a World](../development/主人公与Interface_溯因直觉镜像及进入世界的目的.md), and is not repeated in presentation.
- B is the currently adopted main narrative present; A / B enter in a fixed narrative order and do not constitute menu branches chosen by the player.

For the current sequence from the terminal hard cut into line B in 2046, late tea, Xuyuan asleep at the desk, first meeting Interface at the threshold and the first-stage debate, then to line A revival, aftermath confirmation, lecture-hall record, first return to the capsule, excerpt from *Suppose the Grand Curator Loved Me*, the terminal AI birth record, the minimum methodological hinge, the paired refusal histories, the unfinished answer after returning to the capsule, Interface's first embodied memory and the ten-hour closure, see the accurate English in the [line B opening draft](../drafts/B线开端_晚茶与轨道任务提案.md). ## Twenty Years and 120 Segments

- The author currently provisionally sets source-line Xuyuan as living to about forty; the life material that needs to be transmitted at high resolution to Xuyuan in another line amounts to about twenty years, or roughly 240 months.
- 120 similar time segments would therefore cover about two months on average. The opening, from the initial collision through after Kew and Hyde Park, can become the first expanded unit close to this length.
- The reason that "only twenty years need to be understood" still needs to be clarified: perhaps the receiver and source character share an earlier life, or perhaps the current transmission only concerns adult life or life after the divergence. The terminal AI should not decide on her own that a forty-year-old person's life has only twenty years worth understanding before the reason has been established.
- The 120 segments are the high-resolution scale of in-world transmission, not a requirement that the player read 120 chapters one by one. Transmission can be completed within a shorter physical time, while Xuyuan's conscious understanding can unfold gradually; in `π`, the player only sees the small number of time segments that the narrative actually opens.
- If hundreds or thousands of worldlines also need to be transmitted, the more reasonable current candidate is multi-resolution curation: one anchor life uses 120 high-resolution segments, a small number of comparison lines open key fragments, and a large number of worldlines transmit only structural differences or indexes, then continue to open when needed. It cannot be assumed without explanation that every worldline separately receives a complete 120-segment transmission.
- "Transmitting sufficient material" does not automatically mean that the terminal AI can guarantee that Xuyuan fully understands another self. Reading, arranging, receiving and generating meaning remain different events.

## Linear Narrative and Worldline Relations

- The work currently does not adopt a player menu or multi-branch structure. Source worldline A, mission-intervention worldline B and other worldlines are histories that actually exist within the linear narrative and are read, entered or shown. They are not routes for the player to choose.
- The curatorial hint after Hyde Park is a fixed shot. It does not provide a choice about "whether to see the terminal AI".
- Hyde Park still makes Xuyuan clearly face the fact that the two sets of job-search preparation cannot proceed in parallel: one gives up the London direction and puts available time and energy into autumn recruitment in China; the other gives up preparation for autumn recruitment in China and invests in the London job search. Both first involve returning to China and living with family for at least several months. The English draft currently still shows the old two-option menu, but the work has already cancelled the multi-branch structure; this round does not implement the accurate linear replacement, and the temporarily retained menu cannot continue to be explained as valid player choice.
- Video, sleep and the curatorial hint shot happen in a fixed sequence. The reason Xuyuan calls his mother at this moment is not explained through branch convergence.
- Interface's intervention in the orbital mission in B has been confirmed; how Xuyuan responds and how the player later watches that response still need to be written, but plans that are not adopted remain only creative candidates and do not become player branches.
- The revival inside the causal singularity, the approximately ten-hour conversation and Xuyuan's second death after the conversation have been assigned to A, and serve as the first major information material for line B. A is the terminal AI's second deep dive as Interface; the first deep dive is the earlier embodied-lateness line adjacent to A along `τ`, which is now confirmed to have a causal-singularity recovery window that Interface actively missed. The line A singularity appears along the capsule trajectory as a finite region; before diving in, the terminal AI locks an exit causal-equivalence condition requiring that the revival and other physical changes inside the space cannot affect same-line causality outside causal-singularity space, and the local Interface cannot rewrite that condition from inside. This makes line A her change from the first protective lateness: external history is still isolated, but she herself allows a real answer to leave consequences along `τ`. The long sitting does not occur after the line A conversation; its exact length remains unconfirmed. The final part of line A has adopted the action in which Interface falls asleep during the conversation, Xuyuan covers her with a layer of clothing, and the weight on her shoulder is withdrawn when the boundary restores the state; the kind of clothing and the bodily and conscious process of dying again remain `TBD`. - When line B enters this material, Xuyuan explicitly agrees to let Interface touch his forehead with her index finger; the image uses no glow or technical UI, cuts directly to black, and then enters the revived line A Xuyuan through `Xuyuan opened his eyes. The first thing he saw was her.` Playback keeps third-person close grammar and line A Xuyuan's first-person experience. ## Constraints on the Opening Confession

- Later mission intervention does not depend on Xuyuan making Lulu an impossible-to-guarantee lifelong romantic promise at Kew.
- If a line such as "I will never like anyone else this way again" is still retained after the refusal, it only carries Xuyuan's emotional absolutisation and external language inertia at that moment. It is not a trigger condition for the mission line, the Interface relationship or worldline divergence.
- The current inclination is not to use a formal `I promise`; a promise truly made in full knowledge that it cannot be guaranteed can be left for later Xuyuan and Interface to form when facing subject identity, action or Poincare recurrence.

## Several Months Returning Home and a Later Time Jump

- No matter how the later geographical path develops, after graduation Xuyuan will first return to China and live with family for at least several months; this period cannot be entirely replaced by summary or terminal logs. The player will at least actually encounter some scenes of family life, recollection of the past and the protagonist continuing to think.
- The return-home stage can carry Xuyuan's thinking about stream of consciousness, first-person embodied feeling and the gap in representativeness between training text and life. It can also look back at his past through living with family; conceptual discussion cannot swallow the family members' own actions and relationships.
- The current structure candidate is to omit the intervening decade-plus after these several months, then resume detailed narrative when Yuankai returns to hand over the mission or external responsibility and from there enter the full Chinese post-human line. The omission occurs only in the player presentation order `π`; the middle life of source worldline A still exists in full, and can still be read by the terminal AI.
- Yuankai, who has a short talk with Xuyuan that day and advises him to keep walking for a while, and Yuankai, who returns more than ten years later to reconnect him to external action, are the same person in two functions separated by a long time. They should not be misread as one continuous mission invitation because of montage compression.
