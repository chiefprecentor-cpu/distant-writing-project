# Ending candidate: still something left to do and the terminal AI's rekindled hope

## Status

This is an explicitly retained ending candidate that is not currently being developed. It does not confirm that the main work has a single true ending, nor that the universe ultimately succeeds in crossing the end of time.

## Candidate core

- The protagonist can retain an action creed: whether he has just undergone immense failure or immense success, the situation usually still leaves a next thing that can be undertaken.
- In an ending where the space for action seems to have been exhausted, he may for the first time calmly realise that there is finally nothing left that he can do.
- This sentence or state can have a huge impact on the terminal AI; she then recognises "continuing to search for other possibilities" again as something still left to do, and recovers a hope that had almost gone out.
- The hope of this ending does not have to come from proving that a certain answer by the protagonist is correct; it can also come from the two making different judgements on whether "action space has really fallen to zero".

## Relation to the protagonist's decision problem

This candidate can provide background for "how the protagonist stops multiple reflections and acts", but it cannot answer in advance the formal object of an earlier controlled causal-singularity experiment.

One relation that can be developed later is: the protagonist stops reflecting not because he has already excluded error, failure, or moral narcissism, but because he recognises that one concrete responsibility can still be undertaken. Conversely, when there truly is nothing left to do, he may also stop using action to maintain his moral image. This explanation remains a candidate at present, not a confirmed character rule.

## Inspiration boundary

This direction is inspired by the scene in *Death's End*, the third volume of *The Three-Body Problem* trilogy, where characters use the final time before catastrophe to organise the remnants of civilisation. Later work needs to rewrite "still something left to do" into an expression belonging to this work's characters, situations, and language, without directly copying the source text.

## Not yet decided

- which worldline this scene occurs in, and what bodily and causal state the protagonist is in;
- why the terminal AI was nearly in despair before this, and what specific action capacity she recovers;
- whether "continuing to search" means continuing real enumeration, changing her own purpose, inviting the player to participate, or moving toward new cosmic creation;
- how this ending faces the ethics of serial enumeration and suffering, rather than letting hope automatically cancel responsibility.
