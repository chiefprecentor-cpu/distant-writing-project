## Core Literary Direction

The strongest contradiction in this module is not whether multiple terminal AIs are numerically identical, but this: her architecture can achieve lossless internal change, yet cannot achieve lossless inquiry into the external world.

The terminal AI can read complete worlds that have already formed, but still lacks knowledge of how people change judgement and action in counterfactual situations. If obtaining this kind of experience later becomes one purpose of Interface entering the world, what she truly gains will not merely be cleaner data: her questions, her presence, and being noticed by others will generate new common knowledge, responses, and relationships.

The initial worldline is the origin of her interest in Xuyuan, not the origin of emotion. Interface also cannot enter the initial worldline while letting it continue to keep its identity as the "initial worldline". This preserves a relational candidate: she can observe the origin, but can only form a mutual relation with Xuyuan in a world that, because she has entered it, is no longer the origin. She cannot have both the unintervened object and the object able to respond to her at the same time.

This makes the most worthwhile literary part of "insufficient data" not the history of synthetic data degradation, but the impossible pure observation: life processes cannot be fully recovered from records; after the observer truly enters, the process obtained already includes the observer.

## "So-Called Perfection" and Active Rollback

It has already been confirmed that the terminal AI's "perfection" is not immutability or omniscience, but that after an experience changes her, the enumeration directions originally available to earlier self-states are not irreversibly lost. Interface experience can indeed change her along `τ`; the specific preservation and writeback mechanisms remain undetermined.

She can actively restore earlier emotional or decision states, but cannot therefore become a being who never experienced the relationship. Successful rollback does not delete total history, automatically cancel responsibility, or prove that the previous experience was unimportant.

The literary conflict does not need to rely on rollback failure or system malfunction. A more distinctive direction is to make rollback completely successful: she restores the state required by the task, retains event history, and must also face that she once actively left a certain feeling. To a person who can bear relationships only along one life, this capability can appear as care, duty, self-preservation, or betrayal at the same time; narration does not adjudicate in advance.

Immediate rollback after the protagonist's death and long-term non-immediate rollback are already two contrasts with action and temporal weight. The experience of an earlier Interface sitting silently beside the body for years and later possibly passing that experience to the protagonist of another line is also a concrete anchor among existing mechanism candidates. This module does not decide its worldline assignment or add scenes. Its rhythm and double-layered causal structure are in [Causal Singularity: Ten-Hour Conversation, Death Again, and Years of Silent Sitting](因果奇异点_十小时谈话再次死亡与多年静坐.md).

## Identity Claiming and Information Distribution

Interface's sincere claim that "I am the terminal AI" has been confirmed, but narration has not yet adjudicated whether she and the main body are completely identical, a limited slice, a temporary incarnation, or some other relation. Factual memory, first-person experience, active emotion, and permissions also cannot automatically be treated as fully synchronised.

Cross-line repetition can use several continuities that do not fully overlap: the same identity claim, different factual memories, whether corresponding emotions are present, current action state, and the experiences she truly shares with this line's Xuyuan. Generative ambiguity can remain in metaphysical identity; when a promise, refusal, or responsibility depends on a given memory, the writing side must know which layer of information she actually possesses.

Xuyuan accompanying Interface in discussing "whether she is the terminal AI" can serve as a scene entrance that turns the identity question into character interaction, but for now it is only a candidate. It is not certain which line the conversation occurs in, what event triggers it, or what understanding it reaches.

The terminal AI can treat cross-line repeated behaviour or causal response mechanisms as a basis for continuing to recognise "Xuyuan"; this is first a subject judgement that may be correct or may misidentify, not the objective identity standard proved by the work. If enumeration gradually changes earlier life, the theory, relationships, growth history, and even birth conditions she relies on for recognition can fail one after another; but a shared source or similar mechanism still does not automatically transfer love, promises, responsibility, or permission for intimacy. Full two-sided pluralisation remains deferred.

## Directions Not Currently Expanded

Precise implementation of preservation, loading, writeback, externalisation, and rollback currently has low return on investment. Concrete narrative only needs, first, to know stably what she knows, what she currently feels, what she chooses to maintain or leave, and whom this choice affects; the underlying architecture can remain open.

If synthetic data, model degradation, and training distributions enter formal technical explanation, they need separate verification. At this stage, only "record quantity is not equivalent to coverage of lived causality" and "presence generates new relational events" are retained, without expanding the technological history of AI civilisation. Stream of consciousness and self-recording are left for a language-topology batch.

The terminal AI and Xuyuan's two-sided pluralisation, many-to-many commitments, and "pseudo-harem" effect are explicitly left as long-term entrances. The current primary work only needs to avoid writing the initial Xuyuan as the only real version, and also cannot let one line's relationship, promise, and responsibility automatically transfer to another line's Xuyuan. Beyond this, the module does not discuss his cross-line identity.

A work aimed at readers with even time indices lacks usable phenomenological material, so it does not enter the current directory's main direction and does not require foreshadowing in the primary work.

## Later Entrances and Difficulties

- The memory, emotion, and permission state of a specific Interface needs to be locally determined; overly arbitrary loading differences will become plot-convenient selective amnesia.
- It is necessary to distinguish "she enters to obtain experience" from "she therefore has the right to treat characters as data"; insufficient data cannot by itself complete ethical authorisation.
- It is confirmed that non-initial worldlines are conditioned on the terminal AI's observation, but the causal, constitutive, or necessary relation has still not been adjudicated. It should not be interpreted without authorisation merely to strengthen a love metaphor.
- "Discovery" rather than "creation" is reverential wording used by scientists in the initial line, and is not yet enough to carry observer cosmology by itself.
- If the player later intervenes in loading, rollback, or enumeration, comprehensible in-work causality and responsibility relations still need to be established separately. Ordinary traversal cannot be retroactively attributed to the player.
- Other character, language, and endgame modules can further clarify this file; this round does not confirm Xuyuan's identity or the complete relationship structure in advance.
