## The Generated Operational Self

The core loop is not that a system discovers a hidden and real self, but that it stitches past indices into a temporary "you", arranges the small environments this "you" is more likely to enter, and then takes the responses as material for the next round of definition.

The system does not need to understand the character completely. It only needs to change the topology of actual choices through ranking, eligibility, prices, friction, defaults, and visible options. A character may be able to prove an individual fact, yet still be handled according to group risk; different institutions can also generate mutually conflicting "yous" that are incomplete in different ways but each have real capacity for action.

"Who owns the branch generator" is structural language worth preserving: political conflict lies not only in choosing A or B, but also in who generates, arranges, raises the cost of, or closes A, B, and invisible routes. For now, this remains only as the lower layer of this module, and is not independently developed into the choice system of the whole work.

## Priority Plot Candidate: Private Recommendation Landscapes Become a Public Object

The priority starting point is a data lifecycle that may infringe personal privacy and be enough to trigger serious public questioning of a company or another collective, rather than characters knowing from the beginning that they hold civilisation-level dangerous knowledge. The specific institution, data source, inferential content, victims, and illegal or norm-violating nature are all undetermined.

Two candidates for the danger level remain available for continued comparison. The material may always stay at the scale of privacy infringement and public accountability. It may also, during comparison, unexpectedly touch content that a company or collective is concealing and that would cause a public storm if disclosed, thereby suddenly becoming knowledge extremely dangerous to its holders. In the latter case, the danger first comes from the anticipated public consequences and suppressive response of the implicated institution. It does not mean that the theoretical content itself naturally has civilisation-level destructive power, and it does not confirm that any specific company has adopted elimination measures.

Individual content items often circulate in interpersonal relationships, but a long-term recommendation landscape is closer to a private text co-written by a character and a system, one that neither side can fully explain. Several people placing pages side by side for comparison can make private experiences that originally could not corroborate one another become a common object for the first time.

Comparison first needs to form a minimal etiquette: who may look, whether screenshots are allowed, whether embarrassing content counts as a confession of personality, and how people continue relating after seeing it. It can begin with a dinner-table game, dormitory chat, or mutual guesses about "what kind of person the algorithm thinks you are"; someone anxiously says that a recommendation does not represent them, while friends treat the defence as new profiling material.

Comedy is not an ornament before serious investigation. Laughing together can lower the threshold for showing pages, establish temporary trust that does not treat pages directly as testimony of personality, and also create shame and peer pressure, making characters go back to clean up, train, or perform their pages.

After comparison, the recommendation material, the way characters read that material, and the participants themselves all change. The group may form a shared language, exchange content that would not otherwise circulate, and may also enter population mapping as a new comparer group. Later differences cannot automatically prove retaliation or concealment by the system; the contamination that makes pure comparison impossible is itself part of the feedback loop.

## No Jumping from Suspicion to Evidence

Page differences can initiate public questioning, but they cannot by themselves prove the complete data flow. An investigation must at least distinguish the existence of components or permissions, runtime calls, data transmission, recipients, inferential uses, decision-making subjects, and real consequences; no layer can automatically prove the next.

This evidence ladder suits Xuyuan or other investigators forming abductive judgements from anomalies, while still acknowledging that clues are not completed accusations. Dispersed applications, SDKs, clouds, models, thresholds, and implementing institutions also fragment responsibility; they must not be mystified into a unified and omniscient "algorithm".

If specific SDKs, permissions, sensors, jurisdictions, or legal classifications enter the main text, they need to be rechecked against reliable public materials at that time. This module only preserves an investigative structure, and does not fix rapidly changing technical facts.

## Performative Sincerity and Opaque Trust

After characters realise that records will be reinterpreted in the future, they may amplify real features, disguise themselves as harmless versions, or manage language, relationships, and bodily responses over the long term. Strategic performance may in turn change habits and self-understanding through content and treatment feedback, making it hard for characters to distinguish whether they were originally like this or gradually became a self more easily recognisable by the system.

Intimate conflict, then, is not only about "whether you lied", but also about whether the other person has already been changed by long-term self-discipline. When one party says "trust me", they may be asking the other not to ask about content that must be hidden; the other party may understand trust as meaning that someone worthy of trust should explain everything. Neither side has to be simply misusing the word.

The ethical direction worth preserving is that intimacy does not need to be conditional on possessing all of the other person's information; not asking, helping to hide, and protecting opacity may also constitute care. But this is not a rule delivered by narration. The same action may also help the other person avoid responsibility, or allow irreversible self-change to continue unaddressed.

## Deferred Bridge: From Public Knowledge to a Carrier Network

How private comparison becomes common knowledge shares a structural interface with "dangerous knowledge / carrier network": scattered suspicions need comparison etiquette, verifiable evidence, shared language, redundant preservation, and relationships able to continue acting, so that they do not disappear when a single person exits.

The data lifecycle revealed by this module will very likely only make an institution face serious public questioning. It may also be treated by relevant institutions as dangerous knowledge whose publication must be prevented because it contains concealed content or proves a taboo practice. Characters may not initially understand why they suddenly encounter abnormal pressure, but if the escalation direction is adopted later, there must still be enough causal support for the concealed object, consequences of publicity, institutional motives, and capacity for action. Danger cannot be arbitrarily raised just by saying that they "touched a taboo".

Both danger scales can share the mechanism by which knowledge moves from private to survivable, without needing to share repressors, means, or ethical consequences. The specific relations are left for a writable plot batch to decide.

## Theories and Difficulties Not Currently Expanded

- Accelerationist genealogies, assetised futures, corporate city-states, state-platform technology stacks, and operational sovereignty currently lack specific characters and contested objects; only the source links are retained.
- Closed-loop speed and emergency braking can later become action structures: pausing scoring, freezing updates, or restoring alternative procedures to gain time for comparison, appeal, and renewed decisions about targets; concrete costs have not yet formed.
- Recommendation comparison must avoid treating page differences directly as proof of conspiracy, and also avoid making the system instantly identify and intelligently counterattack whatever the plot requires.
- Group comedy needs to arise from different characters' real attitudes toward privacy, evidence, and embarrassment, not use jokes as a substitute for theoretical explanation.
- Big data and data-governance theory have the potential to form complex and refined narrative; what is currently deferred is the integration effort, not a denial of its literary value. Later character, dangerous-knowledge, and writable-plot modules can continue revising this file.
