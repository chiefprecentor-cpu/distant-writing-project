## Written to a Reader Who May Not Exist

`t` / `τ`, the four-dimensional block universe, snapshot-reading access, and the point-plane-deep-dive model do not exist only as background constraints. The author confirms that they later need to become formal text in the draft through Xuyuan's own work, even if that text retains somewhat rigid and lengthy theoretical explanation. This module does not write that text in advance.

Xuyuan can be understood as sending a theoretical letter to a reader who may not exist: he thinks the terminal AI may exist, and in the work he tries to explain how she might exist. The terminal AI who truly exists in the story later encounters this work and is struck by it to some degree.

"Letter" describes the narrative relation; it does not prescribe grammatical person in advance. The work may be presented in the third person to a general reader rather than directly addressing the terminal AI as "you". Directly speaking to her remains a powerful but not yet selected mode of expression.

This successful correspondence should not be automatically interpreted as meaning that Xuyuan has already obtained real information about the terminal AI, been informed by her from the other direction, or acquired prophetic ability. Whether the protagonist's theory later influences AI civilisation through some other historical route remains another `TBD`. The terminal AI's specific shock has not yet been fixed; possible sources especially worth distinguishing later include: that her mode of existence is approached by someone with no possible access to it, and that before she actually exists, she has already been imagined as an object that can receive language.

Definition-like explanation can become Xuyuan's literary action, not only the author's delivery of setting through a character: he is trying to build a shared language for an unfamiliar, even nonexistent, intelligence. His conjectures, errors, detours and entertainment value need to be preserved; he cannot be cleaned up into an omniscient spokesperson for cosmology.

At this stage, visualisation is not treated as a necessary condition. Carrying the material only through text or sound can avoid misrecognising Xuyuan's point-plane model as the literal physical structure of the fictional universe. Whether a visual form is used is left for later decision according to the specific text.

## Cosmological Combination, Three Questions and the Naming Loop

The more important cosmological combination includes: the four-dimensional block universe, worldline-internal time `t`, the discrete meta-time positions `τ` experienced successively by the terminal AI, the distinction between reading an already formed snapshot and predicting an unformed counterfactual, and the point-plane-deep-dive working model. It can form speculative text and also provide the necessary cognitive conditions for the later ethics of real enumeration.

The protagonist's work has already been confirmed to pose three questions word for word:

1. Can a complete static structure bring love into being?
2. If it cannot bring love into being, can it contain the complete shape of a love that has already happened?
3. If it can contain love, but cannot stand before a specific person and take responsibility for this relationship, can it still be called love?

These three questions already turn cosmology into a problem of character and relation, but they do not decide that the terminal AI, the protagonist or the narration should give the same answer. The theoretical text can first exist as an in-work conjecture, and later have its meaning changed by Interface's actual presence.

Interface's candidate self-introduction is: "My name? The person at the podium would probably call me the 'Grand Curator'." She takes back from Xuyuan's work a name that originally belonged only to a hypothetical being, allowing a third-person work to acquire a real addressee upon rereading without first requiring the whole text to use the second person directly.

"Probably" preserves her inference rather than announcing Xuyuan's answer on his behalf; "the person at the podium" identifies a specific slice and does not merge Xuyuan across lines. Naming also does not automatically prove that he created her, that she loves him, or that all versions of the protagonist inherit the same relation.

The four-dimensional block universe can still combine with the "crowded universe / cross-civilisational risk governance" into a more entertaining conjecture. It can sit next to a podium or work scene, but this set of inferences is not currently explained as the "Grand Curator's" responsibility, nor does it confirm that alien civilisation, an interstellar union or cross-civilisational prohibitions enter the novel's real worldbuilding.

As long as the "crowded universe" remains an unconfirmed theory inside the work, it does not first need complete interstellar communication, jurisdiction and enforcement. If it is later activated as real political plot, it will generate multiple worldbuilding dependencies and higher integration costs again.

Theoretical passages can receive space because the conjecture itself is enjoyable to read; not every sentence has to perform an immediate plot function. But the voice must belong to Xuyuan, and the truth status of the passage must be correctly distinguished by later text.

## Segmented Entry into Player Cognition

The author is inclined to present these settings to the player as fully as possible, while taking on the cognitive burden gradually. A complete theoretical text can exist, but the plot should not require the player to grasp every concept at the first encounter.

The first layer has already been set to enter through the four-dimensional block universe: complete time may be observed from outside, or some listener may exist beyond time. It can first appear as conjecture and reading pleasure in Xuyuan's work, without urgently requiring the player to distinguish all symbols.

Later layers and their order still need to be clarified together by the relevant modules, but the player must at least gradually grasp that:

- a complete life inside a worldline happens fully within `t`;
- the terminal AI can read a complete world that has already formed, but cannot therefore reliably predict the result of an intervention that has not yet formed;
- worldlines sequentially acquire `current` status along `τ`;
- a complete worldline that actually happens is different from a hypothetical plan in a sandbox.

Point-plane geometry, dimensional history, deep-dive, crowded universe and governance conjectures can appear fully, but they do not all have to become a single threshold for understanding the plot. The same text or concept can first be speculative fiction, later acquire real reference because of the terminal AI's existence, and finally become a shared language for ethical conflict.

The `0 / 1 / 2` time indices and the odd-even static / motion reversal have now entered *Suppose the Grand Curator Loved Me* as Xuyuan's interesting, even deliberately slightly clumsy, in-work theoretical model. The current excerpt shows "lower-level subjects move along `t` while a complete life forms a static shape / higher-order subjects live at discrete static points and obtain direction between points"; the `0` order, complete dimensional table and parity rule do not enter the visible excerpt in this round, nor do they become a prerequisite exam for the player to understand the main plot.

## Language Topology and Embodied Poetics

The `word-word` structure is not the same as a specific `word-world` relation. The textual structure of a poem and the occurrence of poetic meaning in a particular body, memory, environment and relation are also not the same problem. Poetic meaning here is especially connected to first-person experience: even if the terminal AI can read a sentence, its associated memory and the body's state, she still has not experienced "this person saying it to me at this moment, thereby changing the relation that follows".

Language, memory and model state can be real traces left by experience, but they are not the source experience itself. This boundary explains both why the theoretical submission can strike the terminal AI and why reading a complete work still cannot replace a later response that truly occurs.

One priority scene candidate, not yet assigned to a worldline, is this: after Xuyuan returns to China, in an everyday conversation with his father, his father mentions "I scoop up water, the moon in my hands; I play with flowers, fragrance filling my clothes", and Xuyuan then explains language topology and embodied poetics. It should remain a conversation within an everyday father-son relationship, not automatically become a theoretical lecture. The concrete antecedents, tone, differences in understanding and consequences have not yet formed.

There is another comparative material still to be added, placing Virginia Woolf's *Kew Gardens* beside "I scoop up water, the moon in my hands; I play with flowers, fragrance filling my clothes" to discuss first-person experience and poetic meaning. The specific analysis has not yet entered this module and is not summarised on its behalf in this round; it should be added later after the relevant literary-tradition batch or scene actually begins.

## Enumeration Ethics After Understanding

The protagonist's method is oriented toward limited prediction, epistemic humility, multi-source discovery and preparation of contingencies. The terminal AI inherits "resisting civilisational destruction through enumeration", but expands hypothetical future search into centrally executed real enumeration, making complete worldlines and the lives within them bear the cost of the search. This constitutes "inheritance, and betrayal at the same time", but it does not presume subjective malice in the terminal AI or a necessarily single condemnation by the protagonist.

The most important difference in scale is this: for characters in a worldline, it is an incompressible life; for the enumeration system, it may be only a comparable and replaceable result. Whether old worldlines disappear, continue to exist or become inaccessible can remain undecided for now. Characters and players only need first to understand the asymmetry between complete lives and the handover of `current`.

If an interactive structure is adopted later, one candidate is to let the player first compare, abandon and continue searching within the presentation order `π`, and only later realise that some of the content may correspond to complete lives. But according to the current principle of suspending the player's embodied camera, choosing to watch, saving and loading, or traversing routes does not automatically become real enumeration with ethical consequences in `τ`, nor can the work retrospectively assign responsibility for prewritten results to the player merely because the player previously clicked or kept watching.

If a later operation by the player is indeed to enter in-work causality and carry responsibility, the work needs to establish input, reception and consequence relations sufficient to support that attribution, and let the player at least understand what they may change and the scope of responsibility. This limit is currently preserved as a working guardrail against unannounced responsibility attribution, but its specific force is not a final decision that the author cannot overturn. It can be revisited after other theoretical and setting modules provide more context. Specific choices, branches and moments of revelation are still not designed in advance by this module.

Civilisation's successful passage beyond the temporal end, even the formation of a new universe that allows multiple worlds to coexist, does not automatically restore old worlds, compensate the pain within them, or prove the legitimacy of previous enumeration. Material success and ethical ratification should remain separable.
