## A space that reroutes consequences

The causal singularity is not a safe room where "whatever happens has no consequences". Within `t` of the same worldline, changes inside the space do not affect external history; however, the experience Interface obtains there can be retained by the terminal AI along `τ`, changing her later state and enumerations.

It concentrates the external consequences onto the terminal AI participating in the conversation: Xuyuan cannot rewrite the external history of this line through this meeting, but he may change the being that will later be responsible for enumerating worlds. The controlled environment could originally be used to calibrate Interface's local presence, yet Interface herself can also become the result of the experiment.

This enables the space to turn the three questions in the protagonist's work from text into a relational event. The terminal AI having already read the work and the complete world does not mean she has already experienced "this person saying it to me at this moment, and changing the subsequent relation because of my response"; the ten-hour conversation produces a previously nonexistent `word-world` event, not another batch of text collected afterwards.

## Ten-hour conversation

In line A, Xuyuan has already been dead for a long time. Interface revives him inside the causal singularity and talks with him for about ten hours. The ten hours refers to conversation time; medical recovery, continuity of consciousness, and bodily state remain unconfirmed.

This conversation can allow theoretical text genuinely to occupy space, without being compressed into "they talked for a long time". Its rhythmic value comes from sustained change: who raises the next question, how an answer changes the other party, when theory becomes self-exposure, and how each detour consumes limited shared time. The actual draft can display this fully, or compress it selectively; it should not allocate quota by literal duration.

The preceding main chain in line A has already been confirmed as follows: after successful facility handling, the sealed evidence-preservation capsule is ejected. When return becomes impossible, Xuyuan completes the final integration of evidence and sends the minimally sufficient material to the ground; the complete originals and Xuyuan fail to evacuate, and his capsule is the only crewed unit not evacuated. Before losing consciousness, Xuyuan does not obtain final confirmation of reception. The capsule then enters a heliocentric escape trajectory through continuous propulsion and a Jupiter gravity assist; Xuyuan dies after long-term resource exhaustion, and the capsule drifts for many years with his body and the most complete original material before entering the singularity. Precise communication, physiological process, long-term preservation of the capsule, orbital parameters, and mechanism of entering the singularity remain `TBD`; these unsettled details must not be casually completed in the draft into another meaning of death or recovery.

Why the conversation happens must also come from the desires and limits of both sides at that moment, rather than only from the author's need to deliver theory. At the opening of the passage, Xuyuan will receive the minimal explanation that out-of-line history will not change, that he has about ten hours, and that the exit requires restoration to a dead state; the exact wording, why Interface chooses to restore him, and how both sides continue to understand the remaining time remain undetermined.

## Orbital data-centre mission: an optional prehistory

An orbital data centre that may harm human interests, and that cannot be stopped in time because of competitive/cooperative jurisdiction and a gap in execution capacity, can lead to a temporary mission of a dozen or so people. It does not need to involve BCI; data, inference models, or AI infrastructure are already sufficient to carry the conflict.

Xuyuan is not a flight, engineering, combat, or legal expert, nor is he the only person who understands the crisis; lines A and B have already confirmed that before the mission he keeps integrating cross-vendor materials, source relations, counterevidence, and action thresholds, becoming the best on-site evidence integrator among the existing personnel and windows. Yuankai participates in the team's evidence and action argument and transfers the request; he cannot designate Xuyuan as a mission member by friendship alone, nor can he accept on his behalf.

The main chain of line A's successful action / failed evacuation has already been resolved in active development enough to support what follows: Xuyuan enters the sealed isolation / evidence-preservation capsule because he is verifying the raw evidence that is about to be sealed. After ejection, he still completes the minimally sufficient material and transmits it back over distance; afterwards he follows the capsule into a solar escape path, dies and drifts for a long time, and finally enters the causal singularity. The team's unauthorised on-site actions, the outcomes of other members, and the mechanism of entering the space are still not developed in this module.

The image in the old work where "a hostile AI defeats humanity and then exiles the protagonist out of respect" is not the default bridge. It should only be compared again if the hostile AI, the result of the war, and the logic of respect themselves deserve to become major narrative elements.

## Death again and years of sitting in stillness

It has been confirmed that, after the conversation, Xuyuan returns to a dead state again. Before diving in, the terminal AI sets the exit causal-equivalence conditions; local Interface can be changed by the conversation, but cannot cancel this condition inside the space. The capsule already has no resources to support Xuyuan's continued survival, but even letting him live for only a very short time would form an external difference in line A, and therefore "he would die anyway" cannot replace the exit constraint. The bodily process, how both sides discuss the pre-set condition, and Interface's action and responsibility at the final moment still need to be handled; metaphysical conversation cannot be used to beautify or skip causal ethics.

After the protagonist's death, Interface might not immediately perform emotional rollback, and instead remain with the corpse inside the still-travelling space capsule, sitting in stillness or dazing for several years. "Crashing" can only serve as a literary description, and cannot be covertly changed into system failure; the truly distinctive point is that her functions have not failed, yet she has not chosen to leave this relationship immediately.

The rhythm of ten hours and that of several years should not use the same density. The long conversation can give language duration; the years are better carried by very few compositions, shifts in sound, repeated images, or enormous time cuts, so that time is felt but not filled with dialogue.

## Same composition, different relationship history

If Interface enters only when Xuyuan is dead and has not completed the conversation while he is alive, the sitting in stillness centres on lateness, missed encounter, and never truly having met. If the sitting happens after the ten-hour conversation and death again, the same silence carries a shared experience that has just formed and been lost again.

The two variants can reuse similar compositions, but when the player sees it the second time, what truly changes the meaning is not a new image, but their knowledge of whether those ten hours had happened before. Visual reuse does not mean the current Interface has loaded memories from another line, nor does it merge the relationship histories of two Xuyuans.

The years of sitting in stillness will also turn the three questions into a tableau that cannot easily be explained: it may look as if a static structure has preserved "the complete shape of love having happened", or it may expose the cruelty of only the shape remaining without a responder. Narration does not deliver the final verdict for the image.
