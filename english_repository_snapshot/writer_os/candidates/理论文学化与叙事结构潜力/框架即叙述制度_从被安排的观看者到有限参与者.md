## The frame allocates narrative authority

The terminal AI can read complete world snapshots, but the player can only receive them segment by segment along `π`. When the whole becomes a work, it necessarily passes through selection, ordering, omission, and connection; curation is not neutral transport, and it also changes whether a piece of material is understood as private memory, work, system record, testimony, defence, data, entertainment, or communal event.

The frame at least allocates who speaks first, how long they can speak, who can respond or interrupt, who can demand retelling, who evaluates the material, and what standard decides whether an experience is worth preserving or treated as noise. An apparently random or equal order also does not automatically eliminate the characters' existing authority.

This module does not designate the Great Curator as the host, nor does it assign the protagonist, Interface, or player to fixed Chaucerian roles. The functions of hosting, ordering, evaluation, and reception can be distributed among different characters, institutions, and presentation layers.

## Collage, repetition, and intertextual deceleration

Theory, everyday speech, characters' works, system records, poems, and materials from different worldlines can be juxtaposed, but the joins must produce relational judgements that a single fragment cannot produce independently. When the same short sentence, sound, or composition reappears in a different `π` position, its attribution and emotional meaning can change because the player already knows another worldline.

Montage is not only for acceleration. Poems, letters, character biographies, or unfinished texts can also force action to pause, require rereading, and let later narrative answer earlier narrative without cancelling it. A later text can form a companion relation with what came before: "yes, but still not enough" or `but no`, instead of overwriting earlier characters with final truth.

No fixed montage density, split-screen log, glitch art, or quotation UI is presupposed. If editing only omits transitions that one does not want to write, or throws multiple concepts at the player simultaneously without producing changes of scale, relation, responsibility, or viewpoint, it is not worth adopting.

## The player gradually obtains a position

Player participation can be broken down into layers that are not automatically equal to one another: watching; a position not fully possessed by the characters being noticed; being able to respond; the response being received; the response being remembered; obtaining authorisation; only finally, possibly having limited in-work causal effect.

These stages do not all have to be adopted or fixed in order. Their value is to prevent "seeing", "being struck by a sentence saying you", "being able to respond", "being perceived by a character", "obtaining authorisation", and "being able to control the result" from being merged all at once.

Participation in the successful universe is not equal to the successful universe having been caused by the player. The stronger candidate is to let the player gradually enter the narrative institution, rather than suddenly revealing at the end that the player has secretly been the actor creating and discarding worlds all along and bears all responsibility.

If this is later activated, it will be necessary to decide separately who notices the player's position, what input counts as response, how it is received and remembered by in-work subjects, when comprehensible responsibility is established, and what can be changed after authorisation. Ordinary watching, clicking, saving/loading, and exiting still do not automatically enter real enumeration within `τ`.

## Structural principles provided by literary tradition

- The frame can turn accidental fellow travellers into a temporary community, but a shared direction does not equal equal speaking rights.
- A successor being stronger, more effective, or successfully continuing civilisation cannot thereby cancel the pain of the replaced and of failed worlds.
- The whole experience must be retold linearly; ordering, emphasis, and omission already participate in the production of meaning, and traces are not equal to source experience.
- Intertext can change narrative speed, enabling the player to reread earlier scenes with language obtained later.
- Characters can already bear relation and responsibility when they cannot fully understand a non-same-kind other; otherness neither automatically proves hostility nor constitutes exemption or blind trust.

These principles connect respectively to the existing modules on enumeration ethics, language topology, and Interface. This file does not reproduce their full arguments, nor does it create a new table of literary-historical role correspondences.

## The glass cage as unallocated literary asset

"The Lord's spirit hound - the glass cage - crimson suchness" is already an available authorial poem. Its most useful internal movement is the gradual intensification of keeping watch, watching, and hunting, while glass reverses complete visibility into inability to touch and imprisonment by an observing apparatus; it can resonate with the terminal AI's movement from complete vision into a local presence that can be acted on by the world in return.

The speaker in the poem is not automatically the terminal AI, and the glass is not equal to a screen, system boundary, or causal singularity. The Latin, English, and Chinese versions are not word-for-word equivalent, and translation difference itself can become a collage join; before formal use, the Latin still needs to be proofread and the concrete presentation position confirmed.

No separate module is currently being created for this poem. It can later return as an epigraph, a character's work, recurring imagery, or pure source material.

## Materials and difficulties not currently developed

- The detailed inheritance from Milton to Keats, the comparison between *Samson Agonistes* and "The Fall of Hyperion", the *Violet Evergarden* association, and KCL's Keats "cultural cybrid" all remain in the original candidate, and require primary texts, character context, or site / institutional verification before deciding whether they enter the story.
- If classical intertext only proves that the character or author has read books, it cannot carry literary structure; when used, it must change who can speak, narrative speed, relational judgement, or attribution of responsibility.
- If player participation consists for a long time only of ambiguous address without perceptible reception and consequences, it will become hollow pseudo-interaction; if causal authority is granted too early, it will break the suspended embodied player camera.
- The specific frame, host, worldline order, and player ontology all remain to be compared after other modules have been read; this round does not choose the main axis of the whole work.
