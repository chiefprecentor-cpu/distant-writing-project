## Existing literary form

The westward walk after rejection, the family phone call, and the hard cut to the Hyde Park terminal have already entered the opening; the exact text is not repeated in this file. Cooking, going out, chatting, and shared life after returning home also already have plot material, and do not need many new events created separately for "quiet life".

What remains mainly theoretical in this batch is: whether action space can truly fall to zero, how the terminal AI understands another subject's stopping, and how *Attending While Seated* becomes an ending form for the relationship from a classical model.

## A direction worth retaining

Xuyuan can rely for a long time on an action creed to pass through failure: situations usually still leave one next concrete thing that can be undertaken. This capacity may also make him keep handing himself over to the era, mistaking stopping for moral failure.

Family time after returning home provides an earlier correction of scale: companionship cannot be stored. Later time together cannot make up for the fact that on a certain day they did not live together; complete records also cannot automatically become shared presence at that time. Ordinary days are therefore not empty space left by the temporary absence of grand action, nor only a recovery period after heartbreak.

In an ending that has not yet been designed, Xuyuan may accurately recognise that the action space belonging to him has indeed fallen to zero. He does not need to despair because of this, nor does he need to keep using action to maintain a moral image.

The terminal AI likewise does not need to be close to despair before this. Xuyuan's stopping only shows that one finite subject has nothing left to do; it cannot judge her action space for her, nor can it hand "continue searching" to her as a final command. Whether she continues, changes direction, accompanies, or stops should become her own judgement.

The literary impact comes from a change in the authority of the same creed: the earlier "there is still something left to do" belongs to Xuyuan's way of surviving; in the ending, he finally allows it to cease applying to himself, and also allows another subject to give a different answer.

## The form provided by *Attending While Seated*

*Attending While Seated* serves only as a relational and rhythmic model: sitting together, aspirations being spoken and heard, the positions of judge and judged becoming capable of loosening, and grand affairs finally returning to actions or scenes of shared life.

It does not require reenacting the original text, fixing character correspondences, or completing the number of people. The protagonist and the Great Curator can be the only people present, but this is still only the minimal scope of the existing metaphor candidate.

"Sitting" also does not mean that nothing has happened. It can make shared time that cannot be stored truly occur at the ending, without immediately translating companionship into a task, solution, or civilisational achievement.

## Possible structural functions

- Ordinary days after returning home first let "companionship cannot be stored" obtain character experience, instead of appearing only as a proposition.
- The terminal AI's reading of complete worlds and one actual shared presence form a cross-chapter contrast: preserving all information is still different from spending time together.
- "There is still something left to do" can appear earlier as action motivation, and finally be calmly reversed by Xuyuan; the terminal AI's response is not predetermined by this reversal.
- The ending form of *Attending While Seated* can let the work stop at hearing and co-presence, rather than using one cosmological success to prove that all previous ethical problems have been resolved.

These are only combinable structural potentials, and do not confirm that they must form a complete character arc in sequence.

## Current cost and boundaries

Before actual writing, it remains necessary to establish the concrete causal chain by which Xuyuan has exhausted his own action. Without that preceding chain, "nothing left to do" will only become an abstract reversal. What the terminal AI is doing at that moment, how she responds, and whether she changes direction all remain `TBD`; this no longer presupposes that she was previously in despair or needed to be rekindled.

This round does not continue developing Lulu's ordinary goodbye, the Britain / China job search and visa branches, compression of more than ten years, or a complete family route. Their required investment is disproportionate to the literary function of this entry.

The plural-universe ouroboros is still mainly a cosmological image, and depends on multiple unconfirmed mechanisms such as cosmic seams, information carriage, civilisational copies, and subject identity; this round does not connect it to the ending, and only retains the original file as a later return point.
