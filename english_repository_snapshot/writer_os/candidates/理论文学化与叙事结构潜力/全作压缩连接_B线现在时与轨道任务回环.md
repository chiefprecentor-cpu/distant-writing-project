## The Player Mainly Lives in Line B

The main present of the player presentation order `π` is located in mission-intervention worldline B. Source worldline A and other worldlines mainly enter through flashback, curated material, memory transmission and "text within text"; A / B are a worldline relation, and do not automatically equal menu branches.

The opening can continue to function as the human material the player first encounters, and can later be reinterpreted by line B. Whether it ultimately belongs to the first expanded unit of A, the approximate shared prehistory of B, or a curatorial contrast between the two still needs to be confirmed by later macro-structure threads; this candidate does not retroactively rewrite the opening.

## The Conversation Already Happened in Line A

In a later stage of line A, Yuankai hands over the orbital data-centre mission. Xuyuan participates in the provisional operation; after the facility handling succeeds, the sealed isolation / forensic capsule he is in is ejected. In the capsule, unable to return, Xuyuan completes the final integration of evidence and sends the minimally sufficient material to the ground, allowing the secret intervention to stop and later accountability to continue. The full originals and Xuyuan's evacuation do not succeed; his capsule is the only crewed unit that fails to evacuate, while the other on-site members complete evacuation. Before losing consciousness, Xuyuan does not receive final confirmation of reception. The capsule then enters a heliocentric escape trajectory through sustained propulsion and a Jupiter gravity assist. Xuyuan dies from resource exhaustion and drifts for many years with his body and the most complete original material before entering causal-singularity space. The team action process, communications, long-term preservation and singularity-entry mechanism can still be compressed or remain unshown.

After the capsule enters the singularity, Interface revives the long-dead Xuyuan and speaks with him for about ten hours; this line A relational event has already been confirmed, while the revival mechanism remains `TBD`. Before the terminal AI's dive, she locks an exit causal-equivalence condition, and Xuyuan returns to death before leaving; years of sitting after the line A conversation are not thereby activated. During the conversation, Xuyuan can say that he is not happy, but this does not mean that he believes his life has had no meaning, regrets accepting the mission, believes the mission was wrong, or asks his counterpart in another worldline to make the opposite choice.

This conversation, together with the experience and change Interface gains from it, is retained along `τ`. The intervention in line B cannot retroactively cancel the relational event that has already formed in line A.

## The Encounter That Happens Earlier in Line B

In line B, which has an approximately similar prehistory, Yuankai again makes the same mission request worth hearing. Interface appears before Xuyuan accepts or executes the mission, carrying line A's materials and first-person experience, and asks him not to go.

She is not trying to erase the conversation that has already happened, and she also cannot use mission failure to prove that Xuyuan should not have gone: the line A action objective did succeed. What she tries to prevent is Xuyuan having to pass through failed evacuation, death and long drifting, and only meet her after she revives him in the singularity. She comes before him in advance, trying to replace a meeting that came too late.

"Unhappiness" can only become pressure and a private reason for her intervention; it cannot become a sufficient judgement. She must distinguish unhappiness, regret, harm, meaning and still being willing to bear responsibility. If she only needs the external result of "not going", then she has no necessary reason to become Interface in person.

Therefore, "preventing" should first be presented as a request that can be heard, refuted and refused, rather than using complete history to decide for Xuyuan. Line B Xuyuan receives line A's materials, but he does not inherit line A Xuyuan's consent, promises or conclusions.

## Compress Through Text, Without Pursuing Natural Transitions

The intervening years, company-employee testimony, orbital jurisdiction, mission preparation and failure can enter through characters' works, theoretical letters, legal materials, hearing excerpts, mission briefings, communication records, black-box logs and curatorial short comments. The work does not need to rewrite every theoretical item into natural dialogue; Xuyuan's works can occupy the main text in an obvious "text within text" form, even an intentionally stiff form. This can become the style of the work, rather than a transitional defect waiting to be repaired.

This compression still needs to preserve differences of rhythm: the embodied everyday life of the opening, the ten-hour line A conversation and the line B request can receive scene time; institutional history and the course of the mission can be carried by high-density materials. Compactness does not mean every passage uses the same information speed.

The textual forms must mark whose material the current material is, which line it comes from, and when line B Xuyuan and the player obtain it. Materials can be incomplete, but information permission must not become so unclear that the player mistakenly thinks the line A conversation has not yet happened.

## Learner's Downgrade Boundary

Learner no longer carries an independent character line, pre-crisis relationship cultivation or main mission structure. If the orbital data centre needs "original records deleted while behavioural change remains", "causal resonance from genealogical merging and copying", or conflict over merge authority, company / institutional employees can provide testimony and make the mechanism part of the facility's illegality and dangerous knowledge; Xuyuan does not communicate directly with Learner.

If the orbital mission does not need the specific functions above, Learner-related structures can stay out of this candidate entirely, and this does not create a narrative gap.

## Branching and Player Role

This candidate does not require "go / do not go" to be immediately made into two equal routes. The existence of A and B already provides a counterfactual structure; a fixed narrative can also complete Interface's request, Xuyuan's answer and the mutual change between them.

Later, the player can obtain limited agency over the order in which materials open, the depth of expansion, or local modes of response, but these choices first change `π` and understanding; they do not automatically rewrite worldlines. If the player is truly allowed to decide the mission result, the full consequences on both sides must be borne separately. This file does not presuppose adopting that approach.

## Not Yet Closed, But No Longer Building Ordinary Bridges

- Xuyuan's on-site participation and the team's partial authorisation have obtained a minimum causal explanation; his capsule is the only crewed unit that fails to evacuate, while the minimally sufficient material, unauthorised on-site actions, vehicle ejection and the process by which the other members evacuate still need further specification;
- Interface's private judgement in moving from "unhappy" to "please do not go" may include preference, misreading or paternalism. The work does not defend her in advance;
- the exact wording in the line A conversation, whether Xuyuan would still be willing to choose the mission again, and the final line B answer remain `TBD`;
- this candidate only permits compression, flashback and explicit blanks to reduce length. It does not disguise missing causality as a natural transition that has already happened.
