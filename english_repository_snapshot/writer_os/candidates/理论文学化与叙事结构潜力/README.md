# Theoretical Literarisation and Narrative-Structure Potential

## Status

This directory is inactive candidate navigation, not the whole work's top-level design, chapter order or adopted branching plan. It preserves only literarisation entry points selected by the author and worth developing in later threads; the human-confirmed constraints in `creative_work/story_state/` retain their own original status.

Continue each time from only one module or local problem. Entering development, forming a complete micro-level narrative structure, or writing into a draft all require later explicit selection; they are not automatically authorised by the directory position.

## Modules

- [Real Enumeration: Theoretical Submission and Enumeration Ethics](真实枚举_理论投书与枚举伦理.md): the cosmological work Xuyuan writes to a terminal AI who may not exist, and the enumeration ethics that can unfold only after the player understands these concepts.
- [Terminal AI: Unable to Observe Without Loss and Rollback-Capable Feelings](终末AI_无法无损旁观与可回滚感情.md): she must enter a world in order to acquire human experience within a relation, yet can actively change her current emotional state while preserving history.
- [Xuyuan: Local Observation, Public Responsibility and Refusable Continuation](何须怨_局部观察公共责任与可被拒绝的接续.md): he forms macro-level judgments from local signs, and bears uncertain consequences through reflection, correction and a structure that allows later people to refuse him.
- [The Generated "You": Private Recommendation Landscape, Performative Sincerity and Opaque Trust](被生成的你_私人推荐景观表演性真诚与不透明的信任.md): through comic page comparison, characters turn a data loop that may violate privacy from private suspicion into a public object, and must also decide again whether trust requires complete readability.
- [Causal Singularity: Ten-Hour Conversation, Death Again and Many Years of Sitting Still](因果奇异点_十小时谈话再次死亡与多年静坐.md): the history inside and outside the line is not affected by the conversation, but the terminal AI changes because of a local encounter; a finite long conversation and long silence together test whether a static structure can bear relation.
- [Frame as Narrative Institution: From Arranged Viewer to Finite Participant](框架即叙述制度_从被安排的观看者到有限参与者.md): presentation order not only arranges materials, but also allocates telling, response, memory and authorisation; the player can gradually acquire a position without being retroactively recognised as having already carried full causal responsibility.
- [Refusal in a Noisy Train: Absurd Cross-Worldline Echo](../Lulu_Interface与何须怨_世界线关系及终局候选.md#嘈杂地铁中的告白版本): another real worldline juxtaposes confession, refusal, shared laughter and delayed loss in a roaring carriage, forming a contrasting echo of Kew. Only this fragment is preserved here; it does not automatically activate later romance, a complete Lulu character line or a happy world.
- [Unstorable Companionship: Nothing to Do and *Sitting in Attendance*](无法储存的陪伴_无事可做与侍坐.md): calm family time distinguishes companionship from storable information; Xuyuan can acknowledge that his room for action has reached zero without deciding on behalf of the terminal AI whether to continue, change direction, accompany or stop.
- [Whole-Work Compressed Connection Candidate: B-Line Present Tense and Orbital Mission Loopback](全作压缩连接_B线现在时与轨道任务回环.md): the player mainly lives in line B, while the A-line orbital mission failure and ten-hour conversation enter through analepsis and textual materials; Interface carries the already happened conversation and asks Xuyuan in advance not to go, but "unhappy" is not the same as regret or a meaningless life.
