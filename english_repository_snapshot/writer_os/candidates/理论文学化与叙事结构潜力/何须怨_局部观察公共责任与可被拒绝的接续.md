## From Local Signs to Public Action

Xuyuan can take public responsibility only from a local position. His abductive reasoning may first appear as unease, a shift in attention or an inexplicable conclusion, and only then gradually reconstruct macro-level change from micro-level signs. It comes close to simple foresight, but it is not a supernatural ability and may also be wrong.

"It happened, but it does not feel real" can function as a local experience in which reasoning has crossed the threshold of certainty, while the body, emotions and everyday sense of reality have not yet synchronised. It is not currently assigned to a fixed event or character, nor does it independently form a module. If used for Xuyuan, the player should first feel the signs and rupture; the narration cannot directly certify his certainty as truth.

He will continue to use negative hypotheses to refute judgments that have already formed, and also to refute the self-narratives through which he preserves caution, innocence or a sense of responsibility. Action may produce disaster, but inaction also has consequences. Forcing himself into action through responsibility may become moral narcissism, while judging every action as rash may merely protect innocence.

If this reflective structure remains only internal, it will make him wait for a decision with no logical flaw. The strongest literarisation is not to give him absolute certainty, but to externalise ineradicable uncertainty into limited action, corrective relations, handover of authority and the independent judgment of later people. Apart from arranging posthumous affairs, the specific form remains a candidate.

## Posthumous Continuation Must Be Able to Refuse Him

Xuyuan has already been confirmed as someone who includes death, loss of contact or inability to continue acting in his plans, and arranges the continuation of information, responsibility, authority and capacity for judgment. What he takes from "one of He Jin's greatest mistakes was dying" is not a romance of death, but the point that a key actor cannot prepare only how to act or sacrifice.

That successors understand the shared goal does not mean they are loyal to Xuyuan himself. If later institutions, facts and protected subjects come into conflict, successors must be able to oppose, modify and even refuse his plan. True inheritance of him may appear as correcting him. A plan may also realise its deeper goal precisely because later people refuse blind obedience.

This creates a dangerous tension with his lack of cherishing for his own life. Arranging posthumous affairs can be genuinely responsible, but it may also be used by him to rationalise "not needing to try hard to stay alive", as though a continuation structure were enough to reduce his continuing responsibility and the value of his life to zero. Later plot should not pre-emptively treat sacrifice as an inevitable heroic endpoint.

## Local Zenith and Public Celestial Pole

The strongest symbolic grammar of the local astral cosmology is this: every observer has their own zenith, and no one's local centre is identical to the world's only centre; different skies can still understand one another through shared coordinates and conversion rules; the celestial pole can be a public position permanently owned by no one, and the pole star that approaches it will also change.

It can further develop "everyone can be an equal Son of Heaven": this does not mean that everyone competes for the same highest office, but that everyone retains the qualification to judge public good and evil, take responsibility for a role and oppose structures that have ceased to function. Institutions are only scaffolds through which local actors cooperate, correct errors, maintain and hand over; they cannot take responsibility in place of people.

This set of symbols can enter Xuyuan's thought, works or conjectures, but egalitarian ethics cannot be naturally derived from astronomy. The "empty pole position" may still secretly retain a single centre; the continuation of a public goal also cannot be swapped into a claim that an office, regime or institutional form must last forever.

Taiyi, Qi fangshi, Confucian scholars, Jixia and Huang-Lao preserve intellectual-historical texture for it, and also increase the cost of formal writing. If they enter the main text, historical facts and controversies need to be verified again, and the work must decide whether Xuyuan believes, tests or personally refutes this cosmology. For now, a complete account of intellectual history is not treated as a prerequisite for other modules.

## Cross-Line Recognition, Not Proof of Identity

What is most worth preserving in the cross-worldline repetition mechanism is the terminal AI's recognition, certainty and possible misrecognition. She can treat the response structures through which Xuyuan forms judgments under different conditions, refutes his own motives or distributes responsibility as evidence that "it is still him"; the specific repeated content has not yet been selected.

If enumeration gradually advances from the second half of life to childhood and birth conditions, it will successively erode the bases of recognition such as works, theories, relations, growth history and biological origin. This is a usable long-term structure, not a confirmed chronology. Neither Xuyuan nor the narration needs to accept the terminal AI's conclusion about identity, and relations, commitments and responsibilities do not transfer automatically with recognition.

"Weeping cherry branches look like willows" and seeing the protagonist as a tree can be preserved as recognition images, but they do not literally merge worldlines and are not independently developed in this round. The full pluralisation of identity on both sides remains for a long-term entry point.

## Currently Undeveloped Directions and Difficulties

- Concrete stopping conditions need to be found for multi-stage reflection within action; otherwise the character will be left with only infinite self-refutation.
- Abductive judgment needs to be allowed to fail or to be unable to persuade others, avoiding writing the "seismograph" as a prophetic device guaranteed correct by the author.
- Arranging posthumous affairs must be kept from becoming an aestheticisation of self-destruction, or from allowing a continuation system to absolve Xuyuan of the responsibility to keep living, maintaining and correcting.
- If local astral material enters formal text, it needs to bear the triple cost of historical research, conceptual hierarchy and character voice. Preserving the entry point does not mean this round must finish writing it.
- The old-version hostile AGI choosing the leader most likely to fail would require a separate manipulator, resistance organisation and concrete causal chain. It cannot be folded into the current terminal AI-Interface relation; unless a failed worldline is selected later, only the original candidate entry point is preserved.
- Other character, governance and language modules can further clarify this file. This round does not complete specific plot, narrative perspective or theoretical main text.
