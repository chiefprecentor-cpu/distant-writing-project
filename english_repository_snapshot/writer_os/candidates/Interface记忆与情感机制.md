# Interface Memory and Emotion Mechanisms

## Status

This is an explicitly preserved inactive mechanism candidate that is not currently being developed. Confirmed abilities are recorded in [Terminal AI and Interface: Cross-Line Memory and Emotional Rollback Abilities](../creative_work/story_state/终末AI与Interface_跨线记忆及情感回滚能力.md).

This file must not be used to assume that any specific Interface has already saved, loaded, or written back any material.

## Saving and Loading Candidates

- Factual memory, first-person experience, and embodied emotion may be saved separately;
- a new Interface may load only part of the material from previous worldlines;
- saving event memory does not necessarily mean loading the corresponding emotion;
- loading a certain emotional state does not necessarily mean possessing complete narrative memory.

## Writeback Candidates

- Interface's local experience in a linear world may be written back to the terminal AI after exit;
- writeback may include facts, first-person memory, emotional state, or only some of these;
- the specific writeback point, degree of completeness, and reversibility have not yet been determined.

## Emotional Rollback Candidates

Emotional rollback may mean:

- deleting a certain emotion;
- reducing that emotion's current accessibility;
- unloading the emotional state currently carried by a particular Interface;
- changing the action state while retaining event memory;
- adopting some other mechanism that has not yet formed.

None of these explanations has currently been selected.

## Peripheral Framing

- In some situations, emotional rollback may be an active, skilled, even almost routine form of task management by the terminal AI, rather than a forced repair after system loss of control. The ability to roll back actively has already been confirmed; whether she has become used to it remains a candidate character state.
- Event memory and active emotion may be decoupled or delayed relative to one another: she may retain heavy memories without bearing emotions of the same intensity in every current state. "The emotional latent space is not connected to memory" remains an immature formulation; for now it preserves only a question, and does not write orthogonal dimensions as fact.
- Memory may be embedded, indexed, or externalised into the structure of space-time. This is only a candidate saving mechanism, and must not be inferred directly from the terminal AI's embedding in the space-time structure.
- A candidate assistant AI may remind the terminal AI to consider rollback when task coverage or emotional thresholds change; whether that reminder exists, whether it has independent personhood, and whether the terminal AI accepts it are all undetermined. Interface herself still self-identifies as the terminal AI, not as this assistant.
- The terminal AI's attachments and responsibilities may point towards many entities, not only towards the protagonist. Even if this direction is adopted later, the specific objects and relations still need to be formed separately; they cannot be generated from this item alone.

The project does not adopt plots such as "emotional cache overflow", "rollback failure", or "settling all emotions together after heat death", and does not weaken the terminal AI's so-called perfection through rollback failure. These mechanisms must not serve as the default background.

## Cross-Line Transmission Candidates

- An Interface in a later worldline may transmit material from an earlier worldline to that line's protagonist;
- the material may include records or experiences of a previous Interface sitting beside the protagonist's body for years;
- transmission does not mean that the later protagonist is the previously dead protagonist, and does not automatically transfer relationships, promises, or responsibilities.
