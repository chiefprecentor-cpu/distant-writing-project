# AI-Civilisation Data Dilemma: Synthetic Data and Real Experience

## Status

This is an explicitly preserved background-mechanism candidate that is not currently being developed. It can help explain why the AI civilisation still lacks sufficient human causal-level behavioural material, but it is not already confirmed technical history, and it cannot replace the smaller and more stable setting facts in [Global Foundation: Real Enumeration and Metatime](../creative_work/story_state/全局底座_真实枚举与元时间.md).

## Preserved Candidate Directions

- If training material increasingly comes from the outputs of existing models, a system may repeatedly amplify existing biases, errors and expressive habits, while reducing rare but important experiential patterns. Whether this happens, and to what degree, depends on data sources, filtering, validation, feedback and regrounding in real environments. It cannot be written as a law that "synthetic data inevitably collapses whenever it is used".
- A civilisation may possess an extremely large quantity of linguistic material while still lacking process material about how people form judgements, revise judgements and bear the consequences of action within concrete bodies, relationships, institutions and dangers. Data scale and causal coverage are not the same thing.
- Stream of consciousness, autoethnographic records and other first-person materials can expose life processes that are usually not preserved, but they are not transparent dumps of consciousness. For a person to undergo spontaneous consciousness while also analysing and transcribing it in real time already changes the process being recorded.
- Therefore, "rare human process data" can be one source of the AI civilisation's data insufficiency. At present, this is not confirmed as the only reason, and it is not confirmed that the terminal AI must depend on one particular literary form in order to understand humans.

## Conceptual Distinctions to Maintain

- Statistical entropy, probabilistic surprisal, rarity within a training distribution, artistic value and a person's irreplaceability are not the same quantity.
- A stretch of stream of consciousness does not necessarily have higher information value because it is irregular, and the fact that it can be imitated by a model does not mean the life-causality that produced it has already been provided.
- A unique person cannot be abbreviated as a "high-entropy sample". That wording would mispackage ethical irreplaceability as a technical metric.
- Synthetic data, multi-Agent discussion and higher sampling randomness do not automatically produce genuine causal understanding. At most, they change the search, expression or validation process.

## Boundary with the Interface Problem

This candidate can explain why historical corpora and offline simulation alone are insufficient, but it cannot by itself explain why the terminal AI needs personally to become Interface. The current setting already allows her to read rich psychological and bodily states from already formed worldlines. What she may obtain after entering is the update process, common knowledge, relational event and first-person experience that can only be generated by her question and presence, rather than simply an extra batch of missing text.

This layer of development appears in [Xuyuan and Interface: Abductive Intuition and the Purpose of Entering a World](../creative_work/development/主人公与Interface_溯因直觉镜像及进入世界的目的.md). For the more general boundaries of linguistic records, embodied situations and stream of consciousness, see [Linguistic Topology, Embodied Poetics and Stream of Consciousness](语言拓扑_具身诗意与意识流.md).

## Must Not Be Automatically Adopted

- Do not write "model collapse" as an inevitable plot in which a model can ultimately only repeat a single word.
- Do not write random sampling, strange texts or stream of consciousness as a data virus that can naturally destroy AI.
- Do not directly upgrade the synthetic-data dilemma into the terminal AI's complete reason for performing real enumeration; between data insufficiency and making real lives bear the cost of search, there are still institutional, technical and ethical choices.
- Causal conclusions and effect strength concerning real machine-learning research have not yet been sufficiently verified. If they enter technical explanation later, they need separate checking.
