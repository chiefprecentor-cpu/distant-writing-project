# Old Protagonist: Zhen, Willow and Failed-Leader Manipulation

## Status

This is an inactive candidate selectively preserved from an old version, and does not enter the current default character setting. It preserves a group of character and imagery sources from before "Xuyuan" formed, for reloading later if an old worldline, enemy AI or failed-leader plot is needed.

## Old Core

- The protagonist was once named "Zhen". The author used "a small tree, unable to bear the main beam" as the working image for this name.
- The old version once imagined that an enemy AGI force, through social engineering, pushed the potential leader most likely to lead humanity toward failure into the leadership position of the resistance movement.
- This means the protagonist's danger lies not in active betrayal, but in goodwill, limits of capacity, a manipulated environment and historical position jointly turning him into a condition for a bad ending.

## Later Imagery Associations

- The imagery of "willow" as weak, sensitive, changing with the environment and possibly becoming fuel participated in the naming evolution from "Zhen" to "Xuyuan".
- "Red Horse, Red Goat", "Nine Purple Li Fire" and Random Forest were once used to connect wood, fire, algorithms and the failed leader, but most concrete derivations have not been confirmed as current setting.
- Random Forest can continue as poetic vocabulary or as a model misused by a character, but it cannot be directly treated as an accurate machine-learning mechanism: random forests rely on sampling, feature randomisation and decorrelation among trees, and are not equivalent to blowing a group of easily controlled "weak trees" in the same direction.

## Clear Boundaries

- This old candidate does not confirm that the current protagonist will necessarily become the leader of the resistance.
- It does not confirm that the current terminal AI will manipulate the protagonist into failure, and cannot be directly merged into the present terminal AI-Interface relation.
- "Red Horse, Red Goat" and the "Five Phases" do not constitute predictive laws of the novel universe.
- This candidate preserves only the old character and imagery explicitly listed above; version material not written into this file does not constitute a recoverable setting.

If it is reactivated later, first specify which worldline it belongs to, and redetermine the manipulator, action goal, protagonist's degree of knowledge, and the concrete causal chain of failure.
