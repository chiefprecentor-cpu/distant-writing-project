## Positions of the Three Subjects

- Lulu forms a relationship history with Xuyuan inside a worldline along physical time `t`.
- The terminal AI can read complete already-formed worldlines along meta-time `τ`, including the relationship line between Xuyuan and Lulu.
- Interface is the terminal AI's local presence in finite non-initial worldlines, not an ordinary romance-route heroine parallel to and mutually exclusive with Lulu.
- The terminal AI seeing the outcome of a relationship does not mean Interface has already obtained Xuyuan's local experience at the time; informational completeness is also not the same as being able to decide how Xuyuan understands the information shown to him.

For related boundaries on subject abilities, see [Terminal AI and Interface: Cross-Line Memory and Emotional Rollback Abilities](../creative_work/story_state/终末AI与Interface_跨线记忆及情感回滚能力.md) and [Xuyuan and Interface: The Purpose of Entering the World](../creative_work/development/主人公与Interface_溯因直觉镜像及进入世界的目的.md).

## Do Not Conflate Worldlines and Branches

There are currently at least three mutually orthogonal problem axes:

1. **Relationship-history axis**: non-romantic relationship history in the player first-seen line / Lulu relationship histories whose earlier causality is already different;
2. **geographical axis**: UK development / China development;
3. **macro-responsibility axis**: undertaking / refusing, and the difference between fuel-like undertaking and balanced undertaking.

A single local player choice may change tone, understanding, or presentation path without automatically generating a new worldline; a worldline may also differ because of the terminal AI's historical intervention, rather than being produced by the current menu.

## The First Human Relationship History Seen by the Player

### Identity

This is the relationship history in the first worldline presented to the player, and also the player's initial human narrative baseline. It is not the initial worldline (the worldline where the terminal AI is born); there is no need to call it the "original failure line" inside the work.

### Current Event Skeleton

- Multiple system failures and chance encounters;
- sharing a table at a maid café, walking together after a transport disruption, and a small amount of online communication;
- Xuyuan invites Lulu to Kew, while the two sides understand the nature of the outing differently;
- this work does not offer a "do not confess" option;
- Xuyuan makes a full confession beneath the cherry tree;
- Lulu explains that she is already in a relationship;
- Xuyuan eventually stops pursuing her in an actional sense, but Lulu continues to exist in the same public world;
- whether he later stays in London, returns to China, undertakes an era-level responsibility, or falls into long-term depression is determined by independent causality.

## Earlier Relationship-History Divergence

The relationship difference is produced by an earlier causal history, while geographical position after graduation forms another independent axis. If Lulu has not entered the original relationship in another worldline, the divergence must happen before Xuyuan knows her, or at least earlier than a temporary choice before Kew.

### Two Candidate Intensities

1. **Collateral result of a broader historical change**

   The terminal AI changes an early historical condition for a civilisational aim unrelated to romance; Lulu not entering the original relationship is only a side effect.

2. **Deliberately choosing a world where the relationship did not occur**

   For Xuyuan's private possibility, the terminal AI chooses or causes a world where Lulu did not meet her original partner. If this is adopted, the work must treat it as a serious ethical conflict, not as romantic assistance.

Xuyuan may point out:

> "For you, that was an earlier parameter change."
>
> "For her, it would have been a life."

The specific historical event, the terminal AI's purpose, and the ontological status of Lulu's other relationship history inside the counterfactual remain `TBD`.

## Lulu Relationship-Line Candidates

An earlier difference in history only provides new conditions for acquaintance. Lulu can still refuse an overly early confession, and the later relationship must be opened by new facts and her active choice.

### Confession Version in a Noisy Underground Train

This line can use an aesthetic completely different from the player first-seen line:

- Player first-seen line: beneath an ideal cherry tree, the confession is serious, and the refusal immediately strikes because of an existing relationship;
- Lulu line: in a roaring Underground train, the two have to shout loudly, and Lulu refuses with "you are a very good person / but you are leaving soon". The situation is absurd enough that both laugh on the spot, and the loss arrives slowly after they part.

It is an independent confession version from another worldline, and does not change the fact that the player first-seen line completes the confession beneath the cherry tree.

If Xuyuan can later stay, staying by itself cannot automatically invalidate the refusal. Lulu must actively reopen the question, for example by asking whether his plans have really changed, and then deciding whether to permit the question to be asked again.

The "happy world" below is only a subsequent idea in this candidate that has not yet been activated: if a reciprocal relationship does in fact develop after this confession, it would belong to a whole separately developed worldline. The refusal and shared laughter on the train would still only be one fragment within it, not a rewrite in which Lulu accepts on the spot. The A-line ten hours currently selects only the confession, refusal, shared laughter, and delayed loss inside the carriage, and does not show or confirm the later happy relationship.

### Revisiting Kew

Another Lulu line can redistribute imagery left unused by the player first-seen Kew line: the Treetop Walkway, evolution slabs, names and hearts carved into railings, bamboo, or the bodhi tree.

If she chooses to go up high, that is a decision made after autonomously assessing the risk on that occasion, not love curing a fear of heights. A contrast can be formed between the heart made of petals in the player first-seen line, ready to scatter at any moment, and the heart carved into a railing in another line, trying to preserve itself permanently.

### Reciprocal Causality Still Missing

- Why Lulu later likes Xuyuan in that line;
- what she actively initiates, rather than only waiting for invitations;
- what choice her professional goals would force her to make that Xuyuan would dislike;
- after what new fact the two discuss the relationship again.

Kindness, humour, anti-elite mate preference, and learning to respect boundaries are only candidate materials, not route stats.

## Macro-Responsibility Axis

Relationship outcome and civilisational outcome also cannot be bound together. The current problem frame is:

| Private happiness state | Macro-level action | Candidate nature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unrealised | Undertake | May become fuel for the era, or may learn balance |
| Unrealised | Refuse | Refusal of paradise / refusal to improve a future without himself in it |
| Realised | Undertake | Integration of romance and public responsibility |
| Realised | Refuse | Closed garden, protecting only inner-circle happiness |

The so-called "utopia" may already be unable to arrive completely. Xuyuan's action may only decide whether to lessen disaster, preserve some future possibilities, hand over a solution, or complete a choice that cannot be transferred to someone else.

Therefore "refusing action" must not be written as Lulu's refusal causing the destruction of civilisation. Lulu is only the symbol Xuyuan uses to organise his reasons; final responsibility belongs to the decision he makes after long-term evolution.

## Interface-Presence Line with an Approximately Player-First-Seen Prehistory

### Identity Boundary

This line can have an almost identical human causal prehistory to the first presented line. It cannot be written as an in-place modification of initial history.

### Earlier Task-Prevention Framework (Refined by Active Development)

The following content is an earlier source for the current A/B task-intervention structure, and no longer serves independently as the active plot explanation. It first prevents Yuankai from bringing Xuyuan before Interface, and places a single task request into two similar but different non-initial worldlines:

1. In source line A, where there is no Interface, Yuankai passes to Xuyuan, who has already moved away from public action, a task or responsibility that cannot be answered on his behalf by a friend; Xuyuan goes to carry it out, and his life afterwards is not happy.
2. After the source line has fully formed, the terminal AI reads the task, its execution, and the later life, and arranges about twenty years of high-resolution material into roughly 120 time segments that can be transmitted and gradually understood.
3. In another non-initial intervention line B, Interface has as her current candidate purpose preventing Xuyuan from carrying out the same task, because she does not want to see again the unhappy life that follows the task.
4. In B, Yuankai remains responsible only for making the external request heard by Xuyuan; Interface is an independent intervener, is not brought by Yuankai, and does not borrow Yuankai to obtain legitimacy for herself.
5. Interface can show source line A in order to dissuade him, but completely reading one outcome is not the same as having exhausted Xuyuan's responsibility judgement in the new worldline. She can choose material, but cannot decide how he understands it; he may still form, from an unhappy outcome, the reason that he "should carry it out".
6. Whether Interface can successfully prevent him, whether Xuyuan still goes to carry out the task, whether the task is worth it, and how much weight personal happiness should hold are all currently undecided.

The skeleton here, including the A/B separation, Yuankai not obtaining legitimacy for Interface, and Interface only being able to choose material rather than decide how Xuyuan understands it, has been absorbed into active development. For the current versions of the source-line task outcome, death, and ten-hour conversation, defer to [Xuyuan and Interface](../creative_work/development/主人公与Interface_溯因直觉镜像及进入世界的目的.md) and the [player first-seen line](../creative_work/presentation/玩家首见线_策展暗示与任务干预世界线.md); the older outcome wording in the six steps above must not override them.

### Earlier Version of Accompanied Death

The following process remains preserved as an earlier alternative, and does not become the default at the same time as the task-prevention version:

1. In an approximate prehistory without Interface, after the Lulu relationship is not realised, Xuyuan refuses an important action, remains depressed for a long time, and dies in low spirits.
2. In another non-initial line, Interface, for private reasons, does not want to only watch him die in low spirits again.
3. At first, she does not try to push him back towards a public mission, but wants to change the ending into this: she is present to accompany him, and, if the relationship between the two sides and the institutional conditions allow it, assists his euthanasia.
4. To help him say farewell, she selectively provides information from another worldline in which "he really once could have been happy"; the happy Lulu line after the noisy-train confession can become the strongest fragment in this.
5. Yet because Xuyuan is moved by receiving this worldline, he believes there is still a future worth fighting for in the present.
6. His friend's encouragement becomes the final catalyst, but "then I should still go" is decided by Xuyuan himself.
7. Interface can choose what to show, but cannot decide how he understands it; her failure is not insufficient computing power, but the failure to exhaust the meanings a person can generate when facing possibility.

### Afterlife Joke and Poincaré Recurrence

Before taking action, Xuyuan can say:

> "Then I should still go. If I don't, maybe I won't even have a chance to see her again in another life. I can't exactly rely on something as insubstantial as Poincaré recurrence, can I?"
>
> His friend was silent for several seconds.
>
> "You're simping on a cosmological scale now."

Then he makes an agreement with Interface to meet again after the next Poincaré recurrence, even though by then it may not still be this self.

Poincaré recurrence can also form two elegant reversals here:

- Before action: the present choice cannot be entrusted to the insubstantial guarantee of Poincaré recurrence, so action must happen now.
- At farewell: knowing clearly that next time it may not still be the same "self", he still agrees with Interface to meet again in the next recurrence.

Therefore it is not an afterlife setting, but an agreement through which both sides express attachment even though neither is certain it can be fulfilled.

This is not a scientific guarantee or an afterlife mechanism confirmed by the work, only an agreement through which two people express attachment while knowing it is almost impossible to fulfil. Poincaré recurrence, cosmic replay, and subject identity remain unresolved questions; see [Plural-Universe Ouroboros and Civilisational Continuation](宇宙终局_复数宇宙衔尾蛇与文明延续.md).

### The Ending Is Still Undecided

- In the task-prevention version, whether Interface succeeds, whether Xuyuan still carries out the task, and how he evaluates the relation between "unhappiness" and "still ought to undertake";
- whether Xuyuan still moves towards the euthanasia Interface originally prepared after completing the task;
- whether he completes only one limited task and then chooses life again;
- whether Interface changes her relational aim because she misjudged his response;
- whether this line first shows the death version, the Interface version, or the counterfactual happy fragment in `π`;
- the consent, ability, and situational conditions for euthanasia.

Before these questions are decided, "Interface accompanies him in death" or "Xuyuan is saved by the happy world" must not be written as an adopted ending.

## Ethical Conflict Between Interface and Relationship History

The terminal AI can raise the probability of encounters, choose records to show, or form non-initial worldlines, but she cannot:

- transplant consent and love from another line onto the current Lulu;
- use her possession of complete outcomes to obtain relationship eligibility for Xuyuan;
- believe that showing Xuyuan a "happy version" allows her to control his production of meaning;
- erase Lulu's independent causality because of private hope without bearing narrative interrogation.

This conflict can form a scale mirror with the suspension-bridge effect at Kew: Xuyuan once believed that using a situation to raise the probability of romantic feeling counted as cheating; Interface faces a stronger and less visible capacity to arrange history.

## Player Presentation Candidates

- The player does not need to know at the start that a happy Lulu version or an Interface-accompaniment version exists later.
- The current stronger direction is not to place a short terminal-AI prologue before the whole human plot, but to hard cut, after the player has seen the first time segment from the collision through Hyde Park, to a curatorial-hint shot not seen by Xuyuan in that line. This shot only exposes the existence of some subject arranging the material; it does not complete a mechanism explanation, and does not mean Interface was in the park at the time.
- When the player later sees the noisy-train confession, they may first experience it as a real worldline, and later discover in the Interface line that it was selectively curated as comforting material.
- The same fragment changes meaning because its position in `π` changes, but the record itself does not thereby become a false simulation.
- Whether the player participates in the terminal AI's choices, only watches her curation, or only locally influences Xuyuan's expression remains suspended.

## First-Draft Scale Problem

At present, all combinations should not be produced in equal amounts. The minimum viable structure may need only:

1. the Kew refusal in the player first-seen line and one later UK or China development;
2. a Lulu counterfactual with a different earlier relationship history, at least forming the noisy-train confession;
3. a later action line with an approximately player-first-seen prehistory but Interface present.

This is candidate compression, and the actual scope of the first draft is still unconfirmed. Other combinations of geography and macro responsibility may only preserve interfaces, or become a sequel.

## Must Not Be Automatically Inferred

- Non-initial Lulu lacking the original partner does not mean someone has taken an actual experience away from her subjective memory; the specific worldline mechanism has not yet been adjudicated.
- The terminal AI seeing the entire relationship history does not mean she understands or possesses that relationship.
- Interface's private motive does not automatically take priority over the subjectivity of Lulu, Xuyuan, or anyone else.
- Xuyuan choosing action does not prove that counterfactual happiness will necessarily be realised in the future.
- The Poincaré recurrence agreement does not solve cross-universe subject identity.
