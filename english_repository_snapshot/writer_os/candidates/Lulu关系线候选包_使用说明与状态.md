# Lulu Relationship-Line Candidate Package: Usage Instructions and Status

## Status

This is an entry point for a prewriting result the author has chosen to preserve. It only navigates the character, plot-block, and worldline candidates that can currently continue to be developed.

This file mainly navigates inactive materials in `candidates/`, while also pointing to the opening draft and continuity checkpoint that have already formed, so that candidates can avoid existing continuity. Only materials actually located in `candidates/` are, by default, not loaded, not adopted, and not assigned to worldlines; linking to a draft or story state does not activate adjacent candidates together.

If a candidate is adopted by the author later, it should enter `creative_work/development/`, `presentation/`, `story_state/`, or `drafts/` according to its role at that time, and link back to the relevant candidate; do not promote the entire candidate package to canon all at once.

## Current Working Model

- `Lulu` is the character's official name.
- Xuyuan is the male protagonist the player follows first and the main human perspective character.
- Lulu is not an ordinary galgame heroine placed alongside Interface. She forms a relationship with Xuyuan along physical time `t`; the terminal AI can read the relevant worldlines along `τ`; Interface is physically present only in limited non-initial worldlines.
- The Kew confession in the worldline first encountered by the player cannot be cancelled through the menu of this work; "not confessing" is only preserved as a possible independently produced sequel IF.
- In the player's first-seen line, the confession takes place under the cherry tree, and the reason for rejection is that Lulu is already in a relationship.
- Romantic differences arise from earlier relationship history/historical causal differences; the later UK/China geographic choice is another independent axis.
- The early relationship history is composed of system failure, public activities, sharing a table at the maid cafe, transport interruption, and a short walk.
- Kew is Lulu and Xuyuan's first long period alone together. She genuinely wants to see cherry blossoms, Xuyuan formally invites her only once, and when she agrees she has already explained that she has another arrangement later.
- Line A's ten hours has already selected a noisy-carriage confession, Lulu's rejection, their shared laughter, and the loss brought by delayed arrival in another independent worldline. This selection does not show later mutuality or a "happy world", and does not rewrite the Kew rejection in the opening.
- The main text's target language is English. Existing Chinese dialogue preserves only semantics and beats; formal English is created directly, without first assuming a Japanese original and then translating it.
