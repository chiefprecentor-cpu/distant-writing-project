## Plot Block One: Language Shutdown and Walking West

### Return Journey, Short Talk, and Walking West

- After the rejection, Xuyuan's psychological access first closes down; the return journey, the conversation with Yuankai, and most of the walk west are presented only from the outside. The magnolia conversation may happen that same day, or on another daytime later on; the omitted time in between does not automatically restore psychological access.
- Yuankai clearly points out that Lulu has already said she was happy to see the cherry blossoms, so the goal of "letting her happily see flowers once" has been achieved. Xuyuan says he is uncertain whether she truly meant it. This disagreement is not treated as an object of discussion about abductive intuition; a later debate is triggered separately when Xuyuan uses `abductive reasoning/feeling` to support a prediction on the scale of an entire life.
- The magnolia beside the campus chapel currently serves as the location for Xuyuan and Yuankai's short conversation: Xuyuan sits under the flowers, replacing the still-closed interior description with checking the route, reporting the direction, and standing up, then starts walking west. The unfinished drink and other campus white flowers are still not required beats.

### Another Cherry Tree in Hyde Park

Xuyuan replaces language he cannot yet organise with bodily movement and walks west to Hyde Park. In the current city continuity, two brief stops are selected: beside the water in St James's Park, red-and-white braided cords and small hanging ornaments are tied among the thick branches of a nearly white cherry tree, with the water and pedestrians on the far side behind it and a pigeon on the ground; between Green Park and Hyde Park, a black ring-shaped memorial object sits on a low stone plinth, containing red and white flowers, small crosses, commemorative objects, and black-and-white portraits inside the ring, with grass, bare branches, and the road beyond the rope barrier. Both places only show Xuyuan stopping from the outside and do not open his psychology; Green Park no longer uses a third sightseeing image. The compositions have already been checked against real locations provided by the author, but the meaning of the braided cords, the formal name of the second place, the commemorated subject, and the exact location still need verification. The main text should not name anything beyond the visible image for now.

After he has walked to exhaustion, he sees a larger cherry tree elsewhere. His first reaction is not poetic, but a trivial counterfactual:

> If I had known there was one here, we would not have needed to walk so far in Kew.

The sentence hurts because it is practical, useless, and too late. The new tree cannot replace that day at Kew, and it cannot change the answer.

### Phone Call and "I Want to Be Buried Here" Candidate

The scene currently selects a video call between Xuyuan and his mother; his father can still be beside his mother or outside the frame. Xuyuan first shows his mother the nearby park and the cherry blossoms, then for the first time talks about Lulu, whom his parents had not known about before. His mother can take the name `Lulu` and the flowers in the camera frame as an opening for a small "like a flower fairy" joke, then confirm with surprise that this is the first time Xuyuan has confessed his feelings. Xuyuan tries to maintain a normal tone, moving gradually from "I want to go home" to "I want to be buried here someday", and only then does his mother's tone become slightly sad.

"I want to be buried here" remains a strong candidate, not an after-death plan Xuyuan has already made. The mother's comfort can use one sentence that lightly hints at Xuyuan's past by showing long familiarity, but should not fill in specific events while the old history is still undecided. The call closes with Xuyuan admitting that he is extremely tired; afterwards he lies down on a nearby bench and falls asleep, is briefly woken by the panting sound of a passing golden retriever, then sleeps again. After a black screen, it cuts to the curation terminal he cannot see.

## Plot Block Two: An Ordinary Goodbye After Coincidences Stop

### Recommended Position

Three to seven days after the confession, or vaguely "less than a week". Because the activity itself is worth attending, Xuyuan goes to a public lecture, film screening, or small discussion that Lulu recommended at Kew Gardens; he also admits that part of him wants to test what will happen when he sees Lulu.

The previous encounters that seemed like fate stop after the rejection. This meeting comes from Xuyuan's decision to attend an activity that is worth attending in itself; the two of them still live in adjacent campus and interest networks.

### Scene Core

Xuyuan wants to know:

> When I see her now, will I still be happy simply because she is there?

The answer can be "yes", but for the first time he distinguishes:

> The feeling has not disappeared immediately;
> the pursuit has ended.

### Minimum Beats

- The two of them only exchange ordinary greetings; they do not avoid each other, and they do not become additionally close.
- Xuyuan feels happy before he thinks, then does not infer hope from that happiness.
- He sits with friends or other people and does not keep watching Lulu.
- After the activity ends, Lulu leaves, and Xuyuan does not follow her.
- This happiness is like ripples on water: it exists for a while, then naturally becomes still.

This is the first time Xuyuan achieves a benign balance by himself: he is still happy to see Lulu, while also being able to stop pursuing her and continue attending to the activity in front of him.

A shorter branch can have Xuyuan not attend the activity and then not see Lulu again for several weeks, preserving the poetic sense that "after the answer appears, fate stops like a prank"; whether to produce it remains `TBD`.

## Plot Block Three: The Second Hidden Variable and Homesickness

### Information Brought by a Friend

While drinking or in ordinary conversation, a friend tells Xuyuan: graduation does not necessarily mean leaving immediately and permanently; work or project paths may still exist.

This is the second hidden variable: again, he has not even checked the most basic real-world options.

The friend can say:

> "Find something you would be willing to come back for even if she is not in London."

Xuyuan needs to separate "I can stay" from "she will choose me again".

### The First Strong Homesickness

The rejection breaks through the time anxiety he had previously organised only around Lulu, making him suddenly see that the time of his parents and grandparents also cannot be stored:

> Companionship cannot be stored.
> The days not spent together this year will not be made up just because I live with them for a few extra months next year.

His grandparents are still healthy; the time pressure comes from the everyday passage of time itself. The same "two years" carries different experiential weight for people at different ages; even if everyone is still healthy a year later, "this year's companionship" will already be impossible to recover.

This homesickness may be a retreat to family for comfort after heartbreak, but more than that, it is the reappearance of a long-deferred question after the structure of his attention has been shaken.

## Family Relationship Candidate

- His parents can be very open-minded. During his undergraduate years there had been intense conflict, but later they gradually reconciled.
- His parents may actively propose ageing collectively with friends and not require Xuyuan to sacrifice his life; Xuyuan, conversely, finds it hard to accept his own long-term absence.
- They may also be willing to fund him while he continues to look for opportunities in London. The conflict is not that his parents obstruct him, but whether he is willing to consume the family's risk reserve.
- His family can question whether, when he calculates "the overall wellbeing of the family", he has included his own future in it.
- Xuyuan gradually discovers that his family will not simply wait in place for him; they also have their own arrangements, judgments, and expectations for his life.

One gentle scene is that after Xuyuan goes home and lives there for a period, his grandparents or parents instead urge him to do what he truly wants to do. Leaving does not automatically constitute debt; staying does not automatically constitute care.

## Economic Conditions and Family Risk

Xuyuan's rule candidate is:

> If London cannot provide a job sufficient for independent living, he will not go back.

He is firmly opposed to using his parents' money to purchase the possibility of "remaining geographically close to Lulu" when there is no job, deadline, or independent reason. The cost of living in London therefore pulls grand ideals back to rent, budgets, and the family's retirement safety cushion.

This judgment is reasonable, but it also contains a desire for control: he easily defines the family's overall wellbeing on everyone else's behalf. The family conflict can focus on:

> "London is burning the future into rent every month."
>
> "That is also your future."
>
> "It is also your retirement money."
>
> "When you calculate the family's overall wellbeing, did you include yourself?"

He can accept short-term support with a goal, budget, and deadline, but accepting it itself makes him uneasy.

## Geographic Choice Axis

### First Layer: Job-Search Preparation Resources

The first-layer player option in Hyde Park currently makes an extreme investment between two sets of job-search preparation that cannot run in parallel, rather than directly guaranteeing the eventual geographic outcome:

- completely abandon the London direction and invest all job-search time and energy in domestic autumn recruitment;
- completely abandon domestic autumn-recruitment preparation and invest all job-search time and energy in the London job search.

Both options first return to China and live with family for at least several months. Before the menu appears, it must be clear that time and energy do not allow preparing in both directions at once, and that there is no middle option of "main focus/also take care of the other". The choice is the current action priority, not asking the player to decide job opportunities, visas, and historical results with a single sentence.

### Fighting for Two to Three Years in London After Going Home

After accompanying his family for a period, Xuyuan tries to return to the UK. The reasons for returning include career, observation, and problems of the age; even if Lulu no longer offers hope, London may still have independent value. This path can currently be limited to an age-window of roughly two to three years, rather than staying indefinitely after graduation without first returning home.

He expects that he may spend a key historical turning point outside his hometown, and take on the higher risk of turmoil or disorder in London; the corresponding benefit is treating London as a high-density sample for observing international cities, institutions, and technological change. The costs also include possibly missing eligibility or windows for domestic autumn recruitment, reducing participation in family and friend activities, and lowering the frequency with which he continuously obtains information about changes of the age from domestic relationships. Concrete recruitment systems, visas, and timelines need to be verified after the plot is settled.

After he fully commits to the London job search, the line will divide again according to actual outcomes: if the position, term, capacity for independent living, or work content does not reach the return threshold Xuyuan sets, he stays in China; if it reaches the threshold, only then does he return to London. Which conditions compose the threshold, and whether the player first selects the threshold and then watches the result, or whether earlier actions cumulatively form the result, still need design.

Returning to London is also not a permanent lock. Within two to three years, family changes, personal shocks, or events of the age can prompt him to return to China again, forming another layer of short branch or a later convergence with the China posthuman line; the specific shock cannot occur merely to force the route back to the main line.

### No Suitable London Job, but Domestic Opportunities Are Obtained

Xuyuan stays in China to develop. This is not second best. If there is no major event of the age, this path can correspond to a relatively safe personal life; at the same time, the China line can come closer to large-scale technological deployment, governance practice, and conflicts among family and national/civilisational responsibility.

### Hyde Park Risk Calendar Candidate

When Xuyuan enumerates the futures above in Hyde Park, he can reveal a partly superstitious tendency that is not fully governed by evidence: he places the "Red Horse Red Goat calamity" alongside the beginning of the "Nine Purple Li Fire period" as a calendrical signal with emotional weight, then combines this with his own technical judgment and believes AGI is very likely to be achieved before 2028. Therefore, in his mind, two or three years of London life is not ordinary overseas residence, but may cover a window of civilisational turning point.

This only confirms how the character may estimate risk. It does not confirm the validity of numerological cosmology, the year of AGI realisation, or future unrest. The shape of his thinking resembles the protagonist's "enumerating futures" in that he simultaneously preserves conditional branches, low-probability high-impact events, action costs, and missing information, rather than treating one prophecy as the sole future; for the relevant confirmed theory, see [protagonist theory: enumerating futures and multi-source discovery](../creative_work/story_state/主人公理论_枚举未来与多来源发现.md).

"I will not like anyone else again" is not used in this line as a declaration to Lulu after the Kew rejection, but becomes Xuyuan's analytical starting point spoken to Yuankai under the chapel magnolia. Its precise meaning is not that he will never like anyone again afterwards, but that he makes the extreme judgment that he will never again, in his lifetime, meet another girl he likes to the same degree. Yuankai questions Xuyuan's description of `abductive reasoning/feeling` as if it were a special ability, and points out that an accurate feeling in the past does not guarantee calibration when the sample is extremely small, he has just been rejected, and his emotions are unstable. There is no need to extrapolate one rejection into a life proposition on that day.

Xuyuan can still insist on this extreme intuition, while separately linking the urgency of action over the next few years to changes of the age: even if the life proposition might be wrong, if he cannot form the personal happiness he wants during these years, the time to seek and maintain intimate relationships may later become a luxury, and the strong attractor of changes of the age may also occupy most future attention and action. Yuankai questions whether this ability is still calibrated when the shock has not yet subsided; the conversation does not have to occur on the day of the rejection. This is not a lifelong prophecy confirmed by the work. It can be broken by new relationship facts in a later transhuman line, but the specific retrieval has not yet been adopted. After psychological recovery in Hyde Park, this is no longer fully repeated; only the one-time opportunity, the narrowing window, and his feeling that he cannot distinguish whether he is predicting or preparing to hand himself over to the age are retained, before entering the job-search resource menu.

The core of this line is that he uses grand language to hide his unwillingness to leave, and income only turns this self-deception into the real condition of long-term pressure. Whether to produce it has not yet been decided.

## Retrieval of Calm Days

An ordinary goodbye, cooking for family, drinking with friends, walking, and noticing white flowers can gradually form:

> Life is not only loving someone or hating someone very intensely.
> There are also many calm days, occasionally stirred by small ripples.
> I want to cherish those days too, in the future.

This both acknowledges Lulu's irreplaceability and does not require Xuyuan to withdraw from the mission of the age. It only turns "calm" from a blank left by the absence of grand narrative into a positive capacity for life.
