# Cosmic Endgame: Multiple-Universe Ouroboros and Civilisational Continuation

## Status

This is a cosmic-endgame candidate that the author once proposed and is not currently developing. It has not yet been joined to the terminal AI's established task, the worldlines serially formed along `τ`, or the ending of the main work, and it is not a cosmological truth the novel has already adopted.

## Core Image Proposed by the Author

- One can imagine more than one universe: each universe has its own beginning and end, multiple universes connect in sequence, and the end point of the final universe connects again with the starting point of the first universe to form an ouroboros.
- The core motive of this image is not to make the universe copy itself for abstract "reproduction", but to let a civilisation living in the late stage of one universe continue in the next universe.
- "Continuation" does not necessarily require civilisation to send complete information directly through the seam between universes. One candidate direction is: the previous civilisation leaves some kind of causal support condition, allowing later matter, computers or civilisation to reconstruct, awaken or carry its copy again.
- If the previous civilisation is mainly composed of AI, the problem of crossing the seam may appear as the re-establishment of state, structure, startup conditions and self-continuity, without first assuming that it can control all initial parameters of the next Big Bang.

## Distinction from the Existing Cosmic Structure

- The "multiple universes" here are not the multiple current worldlines that the terminal AI enumerates in sequence along `τ`; whether any mechanism connects the two remains `TBD`.
- It is also not the new multiverse endgame in [Serial Enumeration Ethics and the New Multiverse](串行枚举伦理与新多元宇宙.md), where "many parallel worlds exist simultaneously". One is a candidate in which universe generations connect head-to-tail, while the other is a candidate in which parallel worlds coexist; they must not be merged merely because both use "multiverse".
- The terminal AI's currently confirmed task remains only to find a worldline in which civilisation crosses the end of time; whether this is ultimately completed through this ouroboros structure has not been decided.

## Scientific and Philosophical Boundaries

- Heat death and Big Crunch are not the same endgame and must not be mixed into a single process. Whether different universes have the same endgame is also unconfirmed.
- This candidate does not claim that the real universe must oscillate periodically, and does not claim that each new universe will automatically clear entropy, preserve information or replay the previous universe.
- Whether identical initial parameters produce a completely identical universe, how chaos affects replay, whether Poincare recurrence can apply, and whether digital or physical reconstruction preserves subject identity are all unresolved questions.
- A civilisation becoming the creation condition or first cause of a successor universe does not automatically make it bear "original sin". Creation may generate questions of responsibility and power, but "first cause = guilty" is not a logic this project accepts.
- The continuation of civilisation, the continuation of information, the continuation of functional copies and the continuation of first-person subjects need to be separated; this candidate currently has not chosen an answer.

## Exclusion Boundary

This file preserves only the questions of universe generations and civilisational continuation explicitly listed above. It does not adopt specific character anchors, storage mechanisms, fixed reincarnation routes or concrete schemes such as "using pain as the foundation of a new universe" that are not included in this candidate, and it does not treat them as problems that must be solved later.
