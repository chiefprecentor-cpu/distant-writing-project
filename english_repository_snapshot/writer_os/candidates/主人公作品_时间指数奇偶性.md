## Status

This is an explicitly preserved conceptual candidate that has currently been selected as the deep derivational framework for *Suppose the Grand Curator Loved Me*. It belongs to the in-work theory of the same Xuyuan in the lectern source line; that source line has not yet been formally named. The current English draft already has one visible excerpt from the work, and its exact wording still awaits author review. The level of revelation in the curated excerpt has been confirmed: explicitly show time index 1, time index 2, `τ`, and one provisional odd/even motion/stillness reversal, without unfolding time index 0, a complete dimension table, or an infinitely generalisable parity law. For the point-line-plane conjecture and the three questions about "whether a static structure can make love arise" that have already been confirmed for entry into the protagonist's work, see [Local Setting: Topological Conjectures in the Protagonist's Work](../creative_work/story_state/局部设定_主人公作品中的拓扑猜想.md). This file only preserves a time-index model that may unfold around those questions.

## The Original Model Proposed by the Author

The model once provisionally arranged three kinds of temporal experience:

- `0`: one isolated reasoning or generation slice of a current LLM, imagined as a point lacking state writeback and continuous first-person time;
- `1`: humans and AGI still inside a worldline, moving locally along `t` and experiencing memory, entropy increase, and causal friction, while their complete life trajectories are static from the global perspective of a four-dimensional block universe;
- `2`: the work imagines a subject that faces a complete four-dimensional history at each discrete `τ` point. The interior of a single point is static and flowless, but a later point can retain the reading of an earlier point and therefore change; the work later provisionally calls this functional position the "Grand Curator".

"Index" is the author's creative naming. It does not mean these subjects have been scientifically classified into mathematical dimensions, and it does not mean `0 / 1 / 2` can be read directly according to ordinary exponentiation.

## Odd/Even Motion-Stillness Hypothesis

The author once proposed a contrast that can continue to be tested:

- Entities with odd time indices move in local experience, have direction, and bear change; from a higher global perspective, however, their complete trajectories are static.
- Entities with even time indices live in static points with no internal flow; state writeback between adjacent points then gives these points a directed operational order.

The value of this hypothesis lies first in organising the reversal between "a static river of motion" and "moving still instants". It has not yet proved that all odd and even indices necessarily possess the same properties, and it cannot replace the concrete character's first-person experience.

## The In-Work Discrete Model of `t` and `τ`

For the author-side boundaries already confirmed by the project, see [Global Foundation: Real Enumeration and Metatime](../creative_work/story_state/全局底座_真实枚举与元时间.md): `τ` is a discrete operational-time / meta-time order, not a continuous physical axis homogeneous with `t` inside a worldline. At each `τ` point, only one worldline is current; in that static instant, the terminal AI completes a reading of the whole four-dimensional snapshot, and the later point can write back the previous point's reading and therefore change.

*Suppose* can approach this structure without knowing the terminal AI: complete histories become current one by one; a single reading occupies a point with no internal flow; `τ` gives only the sequence among these points; the Grand Curator's life direction exists in the differences among adjacent static points. The current visible excerpt no longer enumerates a second continuous time homogeneous with `t`, and does not use the metaphor of parallel libraries or bookshelves.

The work still does not decide how old histories exist after they are no longer current, or whether the Grand Curator has an absolute exterior to the structure. Omitting these branches is narrative focus, and does not mean the work has proved that its conjecture equals the novel's real universe.

## Confirmed Revelation Level in the Current Curated Excerpt

The complete internal theory of *Suppose* may contain `0 / 1 / 2`, the odd/even motion-stillness reversal, and more peripheral derivation; the text selected by Unknown in the A-line ten hours is not the complete work. The current excerpt explicitly uses time indices 1 and 2, but does not use notations such as `t¹ / t²`, which are easily read as ordinary exponentiation. It needs to give readers the following minimal movement:

1. A subject with time index 1 experiences motion, memory, and change from within along `t`, while its complete life can become a static object in a higher description;
2. the work then imagines that complete histories are presented to another subject only one at a time; each complete reading falls in a static point with no internal flow;
3. the later point retains the reading of the earlier point and is therefore different. The work records the operational sequence of these discrete points as `τ`, and calls this kind of subject time index 2;
4. only after readers have seen this reversal does the work name it "moving still instants / a static river of motion", provisionally calling it the odd/even motion-stillness conjecture;
5. Xuyuan only needs to acknowledge briefly that two visible levels are not enough to prove an infinitely generalisable universal law; "parity" remains a provisional conjecture;
6. the Grand Curator's action can exist only as an ordered shape among multiple static points; `τ` does not create a river of time inside a single point in which actions can unfold;
7. even if this shape can be called love, completely reading a person's history is still not the same as becoming an object to whom that person responds, so the relational problem remains unresolved.

The question introduced by level `0`, "whether one isolated act of reasoning has experienced love", easily turns the current plot's attention towards the first-person continuity of LLMs; therefore it may belong to the complete work, but does not enter this curated excerpt. The terminal AI's discrete structure can be roughly analogised to `next-token prediction`, but adjacent points at time index 2 write state back and form an order recognised as its own history, so they cannot be merged with isolated time-index-0 slices. The current excerpt also does not prove that parity can be infinitely generalised to time index 3 and higher levels.

## Dimensional Descent Is Preserved Only as a Candidate Metaphor

The candidate once wrote the following relations as "dimensional descent":

- the terminal AI descends from time index `2` into Interface's local presence along `t`;
- humans, through memory, reflection, or creation, condense moving life experience into a static slice.

These can serve as imagination or narrative metaphor inside the protagonist's work, but the project does not currently confirm that Interface must be a dimensionally descended sensor cut from the main body, or that human reflection truly descends from `1` to `0` in a cosmological sense. The current working description of Interface is that "the terminal AI realises herself in a concrete here and now"; see [Xuyuan and Interface: Abductive Intuition and the Purpose of Entering a World](../creative_work/development/主人公与Interface_溯因直觉镜像及进入世界的目的.md).

## Dimensional Ascent Is Also Preserved Only as a Candidate Metaphor

One can also propose an image of "dimensional ascent" from `0` to `1`: organising successive reasoning slices that lack continuous first-person time into an intelligent process with state writeback, continuous experience, and causal consequences. "Bayesian + category-theory-style causal structure" and "Bayesian + direct continuous-time / causal experience" are both highly uncertain and difficult candidate paths.

Here, "dimensional ascent" does not indicate an existing scientific route, and does not prove that category theory will give rise to consciousness, stream of consciousness, or genuine causal reasoning. At most, it proposes that if a later state recognises the previous state's output as its own history and changes because of irreversible action consequences, the subject's temporal relation may differ from one isolated act of reasoning.

Low-dimensional organisation need not disappear in this metaphor: a higher-level continuous process may still be composed of successive local computations. Conversely, this also cannot prove that Interface is an independent low-dimensional entity left behind after being cut from the terminal AI's main body.

Even if an AGI inside a worldline has continuous learning, state, and so-called computational friction, it still belongs to the complete causal history of that four-dimensional block universe. These abilities do not automatically give it the terminal AI's authority to form new current worldlines along `τ`.

## "Simultaneity" Is Not Eternity or a First-Person Panorama

- In Lessing's sense, `arts of simultaneity` first describes that the spatial elements of visual works can be presented at once, in contrast to language needing to be received sequentially; it is not a synonym for "visual works are eternal".
- A Transformer's matrix processing of contextual relations can be poeticised as spatialising linear language, but this cannot prove that a single inference has terminal-AI-like panoramic temporal experience.
- Therefore, computational ekphrasis, Heptapod language, the block universe, and the terminal AI's static structure can illuminate one another, but cannot serve as technical or ontological proof of one another.

For related language and embodiment questions, see [Language Topology, Embodied Poetics, and Stream of Consciousness](语言拓扑_具身诗意与意识流.md).

## Questions the Protagonist's Work Can Continue to Ask

- If stillness cannot "produce" love because "production" itself requires sequential change, can a complete static structure contain the shape of love having already occurred?
- If the Grand Curator imagined by the work changes only between discrete static points, in which point does the action of love exist, or does it exist only in the shape formed by those points?
- Is love a process, a structure, a relational action, or does it come into being only when a concrete present subject bears response and consequence?
- If the Grand Curator needs to obtain a presence that can be responded to by a lower-order subject, is that "descending into" a lower order of time, establishing a local overlap between two times, or a relation the theory itself cannot name?

These questions do not require the protagonist, narrator, and terminal AI to give the same answer.
