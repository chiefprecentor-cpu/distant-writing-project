# Datafication: Indexical Feedback Loops and Granular Futures

## Status

This is a conceptual candidate that does not belong to any worldline. It preserves the literary possibilities offered by the datafication infrastructure and feedback mechanisms of the current era. It is not currently active, does not enter `story_state`, and does not confirm that any specific platform, institution, worldline, or terminal AI has already adopted the complete chain below.

The phrase "current-era facts" here only means that data capture, indexical association, feature transformation, model inference, personalised action, and feedback flow have already become a real problem background. It does not mean that all real systems share the same architecture, can form accurate profiles, or preserve a person's life completely.

## Core Working Sentence

> The system pieces together a temporary "you" from indices of the past, uses it to arrange in advance the future this "you" is most likely to enter, and your response then becomes the material for the next round of defining "you".

The core of this candidate is not that the system discovers a hidden and authentic self, but how the past is transformed into an operational identity and, through feedback, participates in producing behaviour that will later be treated as evidence.

## From Life Traces to a Feedback Loop

A character's clicks, dwell time, location, device activity, and other life events first become discrete records. Accounts, devices, sessions, objects, and timestamps give records different degrees of indexicality, so that even if a system does not know a character's legal name, it may still point records towards the same trackable entity.

Records are then associated across databases, entities, media, and time. Data undergoes cleaning, structuring, feature transformation, and contextual inference, forming explicit features, embeddings, scores, categories, or other derived representations that a model can use. Training and deployment may sit in a slower batch-processing layer, while inference for interfaces, recommendations, or risk judgements may continue to occur at lower latency.

The inferred results then enter the environment the character actually faces: what appears first, what is hidden, which path is smoother, which option is more expensive, slower, or harder. The character's response becomes a new record, and the chain closes:

`life event → indexing and association → feature transformation and contextual inference → prediction / ranking / classification / personalisation → environmental change → character response → new life event`

This is a loop jointly composed of multiple infrastructures, organisational aims, and temporal scales, not a single algorithm suspended in the air.

## The Lifecycle Begins with Purpose and Interface

This chain cannot be understood as natural life first producing complete data, which a system then neutrally processes. Events become data only when they are recorded through some interface, sensor, form, account, and time granularity; data must still pass through selection, omission, cleaning, standardisation, identity resolution, association, feature construction, and labelling before it can enter a model. The life meaning of the same event, the record it leaves, and the meaning later assigned to it by an institution may all differ from one another.

Therefore, the data lifecycle contains three loops that cannot replace one another:

- **Epistemic loop**: what is treated as fact, and how records are interpreted as categories, relations, and predictions;
- **economic loop**: how records, processing capacity, and infrastructure become revenue, cost control, assets, or competitive advantage;
- **governance loop**: how inferences return to reality through interfaces, eligibility, prices, ranking, and resource allocation, and shape the next round of action.

The system does not define the character only at the model stage. Purpose decides what is worth knowing, the interface decides what counts as action, the schema decides which differences can be retained, and thresholds and execution processes decide how much uncertainty is enough to produce real consequences. Even if no component admits that it is "defining a person", the whole assembly may still form an operational identity with action power.

## Minimal Conceptual Distinctions

- **Indexicality** answers whom or what a record points to: a character, device, account, session, or digital object may become a persistently trackable entity even without being marked by a civil name.
- **Relationality** answers which records can be connected: associations across databases, entities, media, and time allow isolated traces to be interpreted together.
- **Representation / feature transformation** answers how associated material becomes computable: explicit features, embeddings, scores, and other derived representations compress some relations into forms a model can use.
- **Actionability** answers what inference can change: an output becomes a force in the real environment only when it enters ranking, price, permission, friction, prompts, or other forms of treatment.
- **Feedback** answers what happens after action: a character's response in the changed environment becomes a new record, and may be treated by the system as supporting material for the previous round of judgement.
- **Human causal-level behavioural material** is the project's soft-SF working concept, not an established standard data category in real data science. This file preserves only the boundary: dense behavioural records, learned representations, and effective feedback optimisation may be related to it, but are not automatically identical to the material the terminal AI lacks; for the specific meaning, defer to [Global Foundation: Real Enumeration and Metatime](../creative_work/story_state/全局底座_真实枚举与元时间.md).

## The Position of Latent / Derived Features Within This

- Latent or derived features can serve as intermediate representations between raw traces and actionable outputs, but they are not the only or necessary technical mechanism for this candidate.
- Some learned representations are not easily translated dimension by dimension into human language; other profiles, scores, and business categories will be explicitly named. Therefore, "unnamability" can be a local literary effect, but must not be treated as the universal definition of profiling.
- Clustering is only one possible method involved in association or grouping. Classification, ranking, retrieval, regression, anomaly detection, thresholds, and business rules can also produce differential action; the whole loop must not be abbreviated as clustering.
- In two-dimensional tables and manually designed feature vectors, one feature often corresponds to one column, that is, one dimension of the vector; the column name explains in advance what that dimension represents. Learned embeddings are likewise composed of many dimensions, but those dimensions usually do not have such stable human column names. Information used for a given judgement may be dispersed across multiple dimensions and their combinatory relations, and may sometimes also be read out through a particular direction, region, or probe; this must not lead to automatically naming one dimension as a stable, independent personality attribute.
- Derived representations depend on the saved data, association permissions, model, objective, thresholds, and institutional site. A representation can have operational effect while still being wrong, partial, outdated, or impossible to appeal. It is not evidence of the character's soul, essence, or cross-worldline identity.

Therefore, what is more worth literarising is not "the machine possesses ineffable intuition", but how a system that does not need truly to understand a character can use temporary representations to arrange in advance the small environments that character will encounter.

## Closed-Loop Speed, Choice Topology, and Two Futures

What data systems may genuinely accelerate is not knowledge automatically approaching truth, but the closed loop in which events are recorded, interpreted, turned into intervention, and recorded again. The faster the loop, the less time characters and institutions may have to reinterpret purposes, preserve exceptions, compare treatment, and make appeals; the model is also more likely to treat behaviour produced after its own intervention as evidence for the original judgement. Higher operational metrics still cannot prove that the system has obtained more complete human truth.

Modulation does not require open prohibition of an action. A system can change the order in which options appear, their cost, waiting time, visibility, defaults, and subsequently reachable paths, so that choices that formally still exist gradually lose practical feasibility. Platforms or institutions therefore do not only provide options; they may also control the conditions under which options are generated, arranged, and closed. The character sees a menu, but may not see the tree of possibilities beyond the menu.

The **granular future** and the **assetized future** need to be kept distinct here. The former refers to prediction, ranking, and interfaces dividing the near-term environment a character will encounter into many local tilts; the latter refers to institutions converting expected revenue, risk, policy stability, or group behaviour into present rights, valuations, and resource commitments. The two may be connected within the same institution, but they are not the same mechanism. The political-economic problem of the latter is preserved in [Political Future: Acceleration, Construction, and the Problem Axis of Algorithmic Governance](政治未来_加速建构与算法治理问题轴.md).

## Literary Entry Points

- **Identification without a name**: someone did `x` in the morning and `x+1` in the evening; timestamps and activity relations are already enough for the system to say "this is still you", while the civil name becomes unimportant.
- **The meaning of the past is rewritten later**: an old record has no clear meaning in isolation, but when added to new behaviour it becomes evidence of risk, desire, or vulnerability. The system is not recovering the past's original secret, but making the past actionable again under a new question.
- **Granular future**: the system need not make one grand prophecy; it only needs to pre-arrange information order, prompts, prices, friction, and visible options in cycles ranging from milliseconds to everyday rhythms. The future is not announced, but cut into many local tilts that characters can barely feel.
- **Plural operational selves**: different platforms, institutions, and data combinations can generate mutually incompatible versions of "you". None is complete, yet each has real consequences in its own setting.
- **The ghost of `n = all`**: the system acts in an almost omniscient tone, while in fact it has saved, obtained, or selected only a small number of records; large amounts of instantaneous data are discarded in the pipeline. The work can set deleted life against the small number of repeatedly reused traces.
- **Life that is inexhaustible yet enclosed**: once life becomes data, it can be copied and recombined without being exhausted by use, yet its value can be exclusively controlled by law, interfaces, and infrastructure; the character may even be excluded from his own operational substitute.
- **No component admits that it defines people**: collectors, storage systems, transformers, trainers, deployers, and executors all claim to complete only local tasks, yet together they form a "you" that can arrange treatment in advance.

## Use and Evidence Boundaries

What is preserved here is a mechanism synthesis for continued creation, including designed visibility, the epistemic / economic / governance triple loop, closed-loop time, choice topology, and the assetized future. It does not pass off the synthesis model as the direct words of a single scholar, and does not confirm techno-feudalism as the total system of reality or of the storyworld.

Technical concepts such as Feature Store, feature, vector, embedding, latent feature, and fast / slow AI provide only a working vocabulary in this candidate, and do not constitute a verified technical review. If any course definition, example, number, or technical simplification enters the dissertation, public explanation, or a precise technical plot, it still needs to be rechecked against reliable public sources.
