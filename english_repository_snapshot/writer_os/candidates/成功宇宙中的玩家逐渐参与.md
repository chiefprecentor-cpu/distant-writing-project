# Gradual Player Participation in a Successful Universe

## Status

This is an explicitly retained inactive candidate that is not currently being developed. It does not change the current presentation principle for the player's camera position, and does not mean the player already has in-work ontology or causal-engineering authority.

## Candidate Direction

- At first, the player only watches the content and arranges their own understanding in `π`.
- The work may gradually let the terminal AI, Interface or some receiving position notice that there is also a viewing or responding position in the camera that is not fully occupied by the current characters.
- Later stages can separately explore being perceived, being able to communicate, being remembered, receiving authorisation and possessing limited causal authority.
- In a plot where civilization successfully continues, the player may gain a sense of participation.
- This participation can involve response, authorisation, value boundaries or limited intervention, and need not equal foreknowing and directly choosing the complete result.

## Retained Design Rationale

The plot of a universe where continuation succeeds needs to let the player feel that they are not always merely passively receiving a prewritten ending, but this sense of participation does not require the work to confirm the player's ontological position now. The player can first watch, arrange and revise their own understanding through `π`, and only later gradually approach the possibilities of response, being remembered, participating in authorisation or exerting limited influence.

"The player participates in the successful universe" and "the successful universe is caused by the player" remain two different propositions. Gradually establishing a participatory position can avoid retroactively recognising the player, who previously only watched, as an actor with full causal responsibility only at the ending.

## Still Undecided

- whether the perceived position is a residual viewpoint inside the protagonist's experience, a receiving end in a transmission structure, a real meta-time position or some other form;
- whether the terminal AI knows that real players exist;
- whether player input enters the worldline-formation process in `τ`;
- whether a sense of participation requires causal engineering;
- whether the successful universe forms after player participation, or whether the player re-experiences already established events inside an already successful universe.
