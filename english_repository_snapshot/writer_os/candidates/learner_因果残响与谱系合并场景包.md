# Learner: Causal Residue and Lineage-Merger Scene Package

## Status

This is an inactive institutional-scene candidate. The author currently does not develop the continuously learning learner into a directly appearing character, and does not arrange for Xuyuan to communicate with it; this file only preserves behavioural changes that remain after record deletion, the deadline of lineage merger / rollback, and the rights conflicts around them.

The earlier direction in which the learner first formed a character relationship and then carried a later main task through its death or rollback is no longer adopted. If related mechanisms enter the work, they should be embedded in a larger legal, organisational or dangerous-knowledge conflict, with companies, partner institutions, public-service personnel and affected people carrying the character scenes; preserving this file does not mean adopting any organisation, technology, worldline or ending.

For the broader industrial and rights background, see [Future AI Route: Deployment Learning and Model Assetisation Regime](../creative_work/development/未来AI线路_部署学习与模型资产化regime.md); if combined with informant clearance, see [Dangerous Knowledge Clearance: Ghost System and Carrier Network](危险知识清除_幽灵系统与承载网络.md).

## Available Organisational Scene

A regional consortium somewhere between adult education, local public services and knowledge infrastructure can maintain several local learners that continuously learn from real deployment. They do not merely receive one-off training data, but learn exceptions, tone, local knowledge and mutually conflicting needs in schools, libraries, transition training, community services or the public sector.

Three landing points each have different centres of gravity:

- adult education / transition training is best suited to establishing everyday relations among teachers, learners and the learner, and can also easily connect to employment changes from automation;
- local integrated public services most easily produce public consequences around housing, welfare, migration and community resources, but the characters may be written as institutional cases;
- continuity and privacy conflicts in healthcare or care coordination are the sharpest, and would also significantly raise the fact-checking and emotional load.

The organisation name, administrative level and location are all undetermined. Xuyuan can be one participant, but cannot become the only trainer or saviour engineer.

## Human Scene Rather Than Learner Character Line

If Xuyuan encounters this problem, he can speak with employees of the company / partner institution that deploys, maintains or supervises the system. The system itself remains offstage; the player judges what exactly has remained from behavioural differences, audit gaps, employee memories and conflicting institutional explanations.

Employees cannot merely take turns speaking theory. Some may value local knowledge and service continuity, some are responsible for deletion commitments, system safety or corporate risk, and some fear that behavioural residue will re-identify human participants. Later merger cannot rewrite all previous practical help into a conspiracy.

## Trigger Chain: Records Deleted, Change Remains

1. A human participant withdraws consent under the rules then in force; the original conversations, personal file and retrievable samples are deleted.
2. An audit confirms that the learner cannot reproduce the original content, and the supplier announces on this basis that deletion is complete.
3. In a new situation that is similar but not identical, the learner makes a judgement that it would very likely only have formed after experiencing that relationship.
4. Isolated snapshots, control tasks and update lineages can only gradually confirm the existence of changes that cannot be explained by current records; they cannot recover an unambiguous complete past.
5. More aggressive reconstruction can infer limited fragments from the boundaries of change, while also potentially re-identifying human participants.
6. An imminent lineage merger, migration or rollback creates a deadline: merger may copy this change to large numbers of instances, while rollback may delete part of the learner's own formation history.

## Rights That Cannot Be Compressed into One Button

- Human participants can refuse to be carried, inferred or copied across instances without limit;
- Employees, users or other representatives can argue that the formation history of a continuously learning system should not be arbitrarily rewritten in the name of protecting humans; whether the system itself has independent rights still requires separate argument;
- Public institutions must consider the harm to dependants if a service stops;
- Technical teams can sincerely believe that unified merger benefits safety, maintenance and disaster recovery;
- The supplier has asset interests, but this does not make all of its safety claims false;
- Complete lineage logs can prove that the learner has undergone improper rollback, and also create dense cognitive monitoring.

Specific design should at least distinguish memory, bounded use, cross-instance copying / merger, and transaction / benefit; "preserve / delete" must not answer all four rights at once.

## Visible Task Actions

The character does not need to preside over a complete institution. Within a local procedure with a deadline, he can complete several actions:

- confirm which gaps can already be proven, and which are only underdetermined inferences;
- decide the order in which different testimonies are seen, and acknowledge that order changes understanding;
- let some evidence prove only that change exists, without exposing a complete life;
- distribute verification fragments to witnesses who are independent of one another, so that no single party can reproduce a dangerous capability alone;
- propose a time-limited arrangement among merger, global rollback, protected local fork, bounded use and delay;
- write review, succession and exit in from the first day, so that a temporary curator does not become a permanent gatekeeper.

If Xuyuan enters, he should occupy only a limited position of evidence organisation, responsibility judgement or a response that cannot be answered by proxy. Technical implementation, legal process and public judgement remain carried by different characters.

## Kew Memory Crosstalk

When judging whether reconstructed material can be treated as complete memory, Xuyuan can bring his memory of seeing a bumblebee under the cherry tree at Kew into Virginia Woolf's *Kew Gardens*, mistakenly believing that the short story also wrote about a bee. After checking, he finds that the relevant insects are a drone and a dragonfly; he even briefly tries to look in his own afternoon for a dragonfly he had not seen.

This error does not prove human memory false, and does not grant reconstruction technology absolute authority. It only shows how text, actual scene and later reading jointly form a memory that is still real, yet cannot be organised into a single source. `drone` can create a weak echo with later automated war, but must not turn the early Kew dialogue into a code for later setting.
