## Character Position

- Lulu is a character living in the physical world along linear time `t`, and forms the main interpersonal relationship of the traditional romance line with Xuyuan.
- She is not an ordinary romanceable heroine placed in parallel with, and mutually exclusive with, Interface. The terminal AI reads the relationship history between Xuyuan and Lulu along `τ`; Interface only enters physical time in limited worldlines.
- Lulu's rejection, love, commitment, consent, knowledge, and familiarity with Xuyuan are all local worldline facts and cannot be transferred from another line.
- Even if the Lulu in the first line seen by the player rejects Xuyuan, she does not disappear from the public world. She still has her own studies, actions, relationships, and future.

## Current Local Core

This set of traits only needs to support the early acquaintance and Kew chapter. It is not equivalent to a complete character biography:

- She has actively expressed wanting to see cherry blossoms, and Xuyuan then issues one invitation.
- When they first meet, she understands Xuyuan as an ordinary friend who is kind, easy to get along with, and occasionally absurd when he speaks.
- She is often easygoing about low-cost choices, even slightly excessive in yielding decision-making power; once she forms a judgment she truly values, she may also be more stubborn than others expect.
- Her personality is sunny, but her social energy is limited; when there are many people or an activity lasts too long, she will quiet down or find a place to rest.
- She treats people gently.
- She easily notices small facts in front of her, bodily states, time, and concrete objects, thereby pulling Xuyuan back from grand inner drama to the scene.

## Education and Interest Candidates

### Name

- `Lulu` is the character's official name.
- English main text uses `Lulu`; Chinese-language text may use the phonetic Chinese form of the name, and Japanese may write `ルル`.
- "Lunlun" can become a family nickname after Xuyuan's family mishears it, appearing only in a small amount of family comedy.

### Education Structure

- The current candidate is that she is a second-year student in a four-year joint-training programme at KCL, mainly in War Studies and jointly with another department. The main text does not need to explain the full academic administration system.
- This structure gives her a relatively long remaining campus time; the concrete departmental system will only continue to be designed when the plot needs it.

### Professional Problem Axis

She can be concerned with two boundaries before and after war at once:

- nuclear deterrence, arms control, and strategic stability: how to prevent the world from crossing a catastrophic threshold;
- post-war reconstruction and the recovery of civilian life: once the threshold has been crossed, who puts schools, transport, housing, and ordinary life back together.

Together, these two directions point to the question she truly cares about: how ordinary life can avoid being destroyed by war, and how it can begin again after disaster. She can temporarily summarise it as: "One studies how to try not to let the world break; the other studies what to do after it breaks." This is only a direction, not final dialogue.

## Voice Base

The recommended formula is:

> ordinary adult spoken language
> + a small amount of moe-style reaction
> + reality calibration
> + threshold-type assertiveness

### Basic Rules

- The main text writes natural English directly and does not simulate a nonexistent Japanese original.
- Her moe quality comes from reaction rhythm, objects of attention, and Xuyuan's way of seeing her, not from baby talk, referring to herself by name, a large number of Japanese sentence particles, or fixed catchphrases.
- When relaxed, she can use short sentences, pick up a joke, or repeat the strangest part of what Xuyuan has said; when serious, tired, rejecting someone, or in conflict, the surface moe layer decreases.
- She does not have to catch every joke; she can also say "I don't know", change wording midway, make a not very funny joke, or misunderstand Xuyuan.

## Principles for Presenting Moe Quality

Lulu's moe quality mainly comes from Xuyuan's attention and narrative selection. Ordinary actions can gain excessive aesthetic salience in his perspective:

> She only stepped slightly aside, letting the people getting off pass first.
> He did not know why he found that little movement cute.

This sentence expresses Xuyuan's attention, not the work claiming that the action has an objective and universal "moe" quality. If the same action were done by someone else, he might not remember it at all.

When Lulu is present, Xuyuan's narration can shift from concepts to light, distance, smell, sound, and small things in front of him; when she is about to leave or the relationship is threatened, grand reasoning floods back in. This shift shows how attraction happens, and also grounds their time together in concrete scenes.

## Agency and Flaws

### Active Causality Already Sufficient for Chapter One

- What she accepts is a daytime, public, clearly purposed friend outing, and when she accepts she explains how long she can stay.
- She can actively propose resting, choose a small object to look at, decide not to take a certain path, or leave according to her own schedule.

### First Developable Flaw

The current most promising option is a set of asymmetries:

> excessively easygoing on low-cost choices;
> once she forms a judgment, harder to update than others expect.

It can produce mistakes in her major, teamwork, or route choice, without needing to become a romantic quarrel immediately.

### Later Development Questions

- Her long-term desires and action line independent of Xuyuan;
- what she will actively strive for, clearly refuse, and be willing to bear costs for;
- why she actively reopens the relationship question in a certain worldline;
- why she may choose Xuyuan, not only why she is willing to go to Kew with him;
- what strengths she sees in Xuyuan, and which aspects of him she has no patience for;
- how she would make an erroneous judgment that has nothing to do with Xuyuan but brings real consequences.

Candidate material for "why she might choose him" includes: kindness, absurd humour, not filtering people by academic credentials, being able to correct projection after failure, and gradually learning to respect a single "no need".
