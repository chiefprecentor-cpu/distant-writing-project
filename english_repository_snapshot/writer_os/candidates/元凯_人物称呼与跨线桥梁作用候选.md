# Yuankai: Character, Form of Address and Cross-Line Bridge-Role Candidate

## Status and Source Scope

This file gathers the character base that Yuankai can reuse across scenes and worldlines. The specific language and staging of the opening follow the [near-final English draft](../creative_work/drafts/开幕_流水冷开场_相撞与讲座.md) and its [continuity checkpoint](../creative_work/story_state/开幕_相识至HydePark与终端硬切.md); for Xuyuan's relationship-cognition base, see [Xuyuan: Relationship Cognition, Forms of Care and Happiness Structure](男主角何须怨_关系认知照顾方式与幸福结构候选.md), and for the macro responsibility line, see [Lulu, Interface and Xuyuan: Worldline Relations and Ending](Lulu_Interface与何须怨_世界线关系及终局候选.md).

The name and current opening usage have already been adopted. How the two first met, the specific later request, and the number of appearances in each worldline remain candidates or `TBD`. This file does not automatically promote those candidates into facts for all worldlines.

## Form of Address

- The current adopted modern everyday form of address for the character is `Yuankai`. Its first formal appearance in the opening is written as `Yuankai, Xuyuan’s friend, ...`.
- `Yuankai` is the ancient-style courtesy name the character has adopted for himself; his legal name, surname and nationality remain `TBD`.
- Xuyuan and the narration directly call him `Yuankai`. In a non-Chinese context in London, others can naturally treat it as an ordinary given name. The main text does not require him to explain the naming system, and it does not need to arrange a reveal.
- Du Yu's courtesy name was Yuankai, and he was called "Du Wuku" because of his broad and versatile learning. Strictly speaking, the original *Zuo Zhuan* text refers collectively to the "Eight Yuan and Eight Kai", but Yuankai / Yuan-kai is the common form, and Du Yu's courtesy name is also written Yuankai. This self-chosen form of address carries performative desire and active historical self-positioning; the work does not guarantee, through the name, that he necessarily deserves the reference.

## Education and Voice Base

- He attends the LSE `MPhil/PhD Philosophy`, with research interests in philosophy of the social sciences; his specific dissertation topic remains `TBD`.
- His ability to understand and handle complex problems is strong, and his range of knowledge, judgement, capacity for action and persistence are all high-level, but these traits are not generated automatically by his doctoral status.
- He is familiar with Simplified-Chinese ACGN internet memes and can respond to the niche science-fiction, media and thought discussions Xuyuan throws out; their dialogue presents shared reasoning, rather than Yuankai explaining things one-way to the player.
- His comic mode includes compressing Xuyuan's long explanations into dry short sentences, and deliberately splicing incompatible theories together to create "conceptual accidents". The jokes are built on his understanding of the conflict, but they do not make him an always-correct authorial mouthpiece.
- The opening only needs to show part of his ability: handling actual communication in an unfamiliar situation, actively moving seats, not over-publicly joining in the teasing, and pointing out, in serious discussion, the way Xuyuan selects sets of events.

## Relationship with Xuyuan

The current candidate for how they met is: first, they argued for a long time on an unnamed Chinese-language website about the future of AI or the future of human society, and later discovered that they were both in London. The relationship formed not because one side conceded defeat, but because both were willing to answer the questions the other was actually asking, and to follow disagreement through to its consequences for action. The specific website, propositions, person who sent the private message and first offline meeting all remain `TBD`, and they also do not enter the maid café small talk.

Most of the time, they simply talk occasionally online or offline. Yuankai is not Xuyuan's full-time caretaker, and he is not an institutional messenger who only transports missions when the plot needs him. Ordinary argument, meals and inconsequential conversation must continue to exist.

## Nodal Bridge Role

- At key nodes in a small number of worldlines, a withdrawn and dispirited Xuyuan receives an external request or responsibility related to him personally, one that Yuankai cannot answer on his behalf.
- Yuankai can re-establish contact, hand over information, prove that a request deserves to be heard out, or translate institutional language into the question Xuyuan would actually respond to.
- He cannot decide for Xuyuan, and he cannot use Lulu, friendship or the consequences of disaster as emotional blackmail. The final acceptance, refusal and reasons still belong to Xuyuan.
- "No one else can do it" must come from Xuyuan's specific permissions, existing actions, relationships or knowledge position; he should not be written as a chosen genius uniquely able to understand the problem.
- Yuankai's doctoral status makes it possible for him still to be in London after Xuyuan completes his master's degree. This only increases the opportunity for natural appearances in some worldlines; it does not guarantee continuous contact.

## "Yuankai Comes to Raise the Person" Candidate

A friend bringing Xuyuan, who has withdrawn from history, back into an external request can invert the allusion to Shun raising Yuankai: in the original, Shun raises and employs Yuankai; in the story, "Yuankai comes to raise someone". Beneath the slight internal joke, it corresponds to a serious structure: someone who has already committed himself to history comes back and brings someone who has withdrawn from history into it again.

Currently preserved:

> "According to the allusion, someone should have come to raise me. How did this become me coming to raise you?"

and:

> "Shun raised Yuankai. Yuankai raises you. The rites have collapsed and the music is in ruins."

The Chinese verb being used here simultaneously carries the senses of appointing or employing, lifting up, and bringing someone back into action. The specific English target text should be designed after the concrete reunion scene is established, rather than preemptively literalised in a way that loses the allusion or the joke.

## Must Not Be Automatically Derived

- The self-chosen name "Yuankai" does not prove that he is actually equivalent to Du Yu, a worthy minister or a saviour-type figure.
- High ability does not mean every judgement is correct, and it does not exempt him from having clear blind spots and his own life goals.
- The bridge role is not a permanent duty of care; friendship is also not a maintenance position that the age has configured for Xuyuan.
- A request, encouragement, knowledge or degree of intimacy in one worldline cannot automatically transfer to another worldline.
