The current English draft has continued from `Because he wrote about one.` into the reconstructed visible excerpt, and has attributed the work to the same Xuyuan in the lecture-hall source line; the excerpt's exact English remains a draft awaiting author review, and does not automatically become confirmed source text merely because it has been implemented. The English title has been confirmed as *Suppose the Grand Curator Loved Me*, and the subtitle as *Love in the Fourth Dimension*. This file remains in `candidates/` because the complete work and its internal theory of time have not yet been finalised, not because its position in the ten hours or its current presentation vehicle is still undecided.

This work appears after the first lecture-hall transmission and the return to the capsule, and before the terminal AI initial / birth record; Interface's early naming line first retrieves the name from the work, the lecture-hall transmission only lets A-line Xuyuan see the person she means, and this work then explains where the name came from.

## Preserved Candidate Core

- Xuyuan in the lecture-hall source line wrote a work titled *Suppose the Grand Curator Loved Me*, with the subtitle *Love in the Fourth Dimension*, discussing whether love can exist in a four-dimensional structure.
- When Interface introduces herself, she may say verbatim:

  > "My name? The person on the lecture platform would probably call me the 'Grand Curator'."

- "Grand Curator" preserves the relations of enumeration, selection, preservation and organisation indicated by `Grand Curator`; in Chinese it should not be downgraded into the ordinary institutional position of "curator". Japanese and other later localisation forms remain TBD; translations not written into this file do not constitute selected options.

## Candidate Function of the Naming Loop

This design can let the protagonist first provide a name in his work for an imagined higher-dimensional being, and later let Interface retrieve that name from his work. It can simultaneously produce:

- an echo between the literary conjecture the protagonist proposes while unaware of the real structure and the later actual presence of the terminal AI;
- a side of the terminal AI as academic inheritor, reader, or hidden "academic fan";
- the uncertainty preserved by the word "probably": she can know what the protagonist would call her without confirming on behalf of the work that the protagonist's hypothesis has already become true;
- a reference by "the person on the lecture platform" to a specific slice, rather than an abstract omniscient self-account.

The initial worldline is only the origin of the terminal AI's interest in the protagonist, not the origin of emotion. Therefore, this naming loop must not be automatically written as "he created her name, so she must love him", nor can it prove that protagonists in different worldlines all inherit the same relationship.

## Confirmed Ten-Hour Vehicle

This work does not appear as a fifth full out-of-capsule transmission, and it does not cut into the scene of the author writing it at the time. It enters through an in-capsule "curation / reading log + original-work excerpt with clear source attribution" in line A:

- Xuyuan first asks what the other self wrote, and requests to see the part sufficient for understanding the name "Grand Curator"; Unknown does not play the next material unrequested.
- Unknown can explain that the work is long and that the name depends on the preceding argument; Xuyuan then asks her to show only a sufficient excerpt needed to understand the name. This brings the finitude of the ten hours back into the dialogue, and makes "which content to excerpt" a visible choice by Interface.
- The space capsule is not replaced by another source line. The shot can darken the environment or let text fill the frame, but A-line Xuyuan's body, remaining time and Unknown's presence are still uninterrupted.
- The dead in-capsule communication screen stays black; the screen does not suddenly recover into Interface's playback device. The log text is the curatorial layer of the transmission, not the interface of an in-capsule prop.
- The minimal source header only needs to let the reader distinguish author and work; it does not need to put every archive field from the other worldline into the main text.

This vehicle preserves three layers of ownership:

1. The original text and its argument belong to the same Xuyuan in the lecture-hall source line;
2. What to excerpt, how to arrange it, and where to stop belong to the terminal AI / Unknown's curation;
3. Reading it at this moment and forming a new response belong to A-line Xuyuan.

The vehicle itself also reenacts the work's problem: a set of static words can preserve the complete shape of a thought, but Xuyuan must read it section by section along his own linear time and give a response that is not present in the old work. This does not need to be summarised by the narration.

## The Work's Internal Time Argument

Time-index parity is now the deep argumentative skeleton of this work, not an added display of setting. A person with time index 1 experiences a moving river of life along `t`, while the complete four-dimensional history can be grasped as a static shape as a whole. The work then imagines time index 2: only one complete history appears at a time, each complete reading occurs at a static point with no internal flow, and the later point preserves the previous reading and is therefore different; `τ` only names the discrete operational sequence of these points.

The current excerpt no longer enumerates ontological branches such as homogeneous second time, parallel libraries or an absolute outside, and no longer uses the bookshelf metaphor. It compresses the reversal into one unexplained crossed naming: `a motionless instant in motion; a moving river held still`. The work thereby approaches the author-side real foundation, but the narration or Unknown does not announce that the two are completely identical; questions such as how old histories exist after they are no longer current remain outside this passage.

The "action" of the Grand Curator is also rewritten accordingly: a single `τ` point does not provide a process in which action unfolds; no single point contains "return"; the ordered plurality of static points itself constitutes return. `τ` only arranges her stillness; it does not turn it into the river humans experience along `t`. The work then asks whether a shape that still bends the later point after informational value has been exhausted can be called love, and preserves the counterargument that unrequited love does not require response as a condition of existence.

The work no longer argues that the Grand Curator should enter a particular person's `t`, and it does not explain in advance what new relational facts entry would create. `stand before a particular person` appears only in the third question as an unanswered difficulty, and does not become an action instruction written for the later Interface. For the specific theoretical boundary, see [Time-Index Parity](主人公作品_时间指数奇偶性.md).

## Implemented Presentation Rhythm and Review Baseline

This block immediately follows a relatively long lecture-hall speech, so it should not repeat the voice shape of "another person orally delivering a complete theory without interruption". The overall implemented rhythm is:

> Dry questions and answers after returning to the capsule -> emotional sting of the main title and subtitle and Unknown's quiet laugh -> static as a whole yet internally flowing in `t` -> static at a single point yet directional between points in discrete `τ` -> unexplained crossed naming -> space capsule and bodily breath break -> return as the shape of multiple static points -> deflection after informational value is exhausted and the unrequited-love counterargument -> the three questions land -> asking what exactly the work came close to in her

The current draft presents the work text as one continuous excerpt. Interface's curation appears in her selection of the beginning and ending points; the text does not rely on skipped chapter numbers, fragmentary collage or repeated lists of ontological models to prove that she is curating.

### Preventing the Work from Becoming Defensive Argument Again

- The current excerpt preserves only one time model; it does not itemise counter-models, disclaimers and ontological boundaries one by one to prove that the author knows other possibilities.
- Only two key forms of resistance remain: two layers are not enough to prove a parity law; method or fixation can also produce repetition. The former limits theoretical extrapolation, while the latter keeps love as a question.
- The unrequited-love passage remains because it genuinely changes the meaning of the third question: response cannot be substituted for proof that love exists.
- All explanation of what relational facts would arise after Interface enters `t` is left to the later plot. The work only proposes `stand before`, and does not arrange an answer for her.
- The bodily hinge continues to show that reading consumes the finite time of A-line Xuyuan; it is not a general decoration between theoretical passages.

### What the Reader Needs to Take Away

On a first reading, the only things that must be securely obtained are:

- another Xuyuan wrote this work, and Unknown selects only one passage from it;
- humans live along `t` in a moving process, while a complete four-dimensional life forms a static shape;
- at each discrete `τ` point, the Grand Curator completely reads one current history; a single point has no flow, while writeback from point to point forms direction;
- human love can enter a complete shape as an action occurring along `t`; if the Grand Curator's love exists, it can only be pursued as a question through deflection constituted by multiple static points;
- therefore "Grand Curator" is only an imagined position constructed by the work, and not yet real identification proof for Unknown.

What can be left for rereading, and does not constitute a first-reading exam, is:

- the definition of time index 0, the full relation among `0/1/2` in the complete theory, and whether the parity rule can be extended to higher orders;
- which conjectures in the work become structurally isomorphic with actions later shown by the terminal AI;
- why Unknown extracts precisely the part leading toward the relational question, rather than other cosmological derivations in the work;
- what relation the `me` in the title maintains with the lecture-hall source line, A-line Xuyuan and later B-line Xuyuan.

The work uses a discrete `τ` conjecture close to the real foundation, but the player does not need to decide on that basis that it has already obtained cosmological proof.

### Reducing the Load of Terms, Image and Voice

- The only new symbols used are `t` and `τ`; this excerpt does not add `π`, new functions, dimension tables or formulas for eight combinations.
- Keep the main nouns as stable as possible. When discussing complete history, mainly use `history`, without rotating through `worldline`, `timeline`, `snapshot` and `block` in adjacent sentences to show variation; when discussing a concrete person, return to `a life` or `a person`.
- Do not add a flowchart or token diagram for the discrete points; `next-token prediction` is only an author-side approximate understanding, and does not enter the work's main text.
- Keep the dead quiet inside the capsule throughout the passage; do not add data-stream sound effects, typing sounds or music swelling before the final question. Xuyuan's breathing and necessary small movements are already enough to keep the text embodied.
- Do not let the communication screen carry the text, and do not make the layout of the reading log so refined that it resembles a newly established software product. The source header and section breaks are enough to make ownership clear.

The author has confirmed that this excerpt has no need to control length according to a preset word count or a proportional relation to the length of the lecture-hall speech. Its length is determined by what is needed to complete the argument through motion / stillness, generating / containing, the action of love, change in `τ`, the unrequited-love counterargument and the relational question. Removing logical repetition and AI fingerprint remains literary review, not a word-cutting task.

### Work Voice and Character Voice

- The work's main text uses third person or general exposition, and does not address the imagined Grand Curator directly as `you`. Apart from the `me` in the title, the current draft has no direct first person, so the work does not read like a letter already precisely addressed to the terminal AI herself.
- This is a written work, so it does not need to retain the slightly stiff oral voice of the lecture platform, nor does it need to be deliberately clumsy to show Xuyuan searching for language. The current draft uses a more coherent philosophical prose voice; the theory's self-limitation is still carried by the argument, not by each paragraph closing perfectly as "definition - bifurcation - balanced-sentence conclusion".
- One or two traces of a human author's naming hesitation or self-correction can remain, such as admitting that "index" is only a temporary and not fully defensible name. Such traces show thought searching for language; it is unnecessary to attach full disclaimers to every term.
- Syntax can land on a short correction after one longer inferential sentence, but this long-short contrast should not repeat mechanically in every paragraph. The final three questions are the most obvious syntactic loop in the whole passage.
- Unknown does not interrupt, translate or confirm whether the work has guessed her correctly inside the work's main text. Her dialogue no longer uses "emotionless human-machine" as its default template: genuinely binary facts can be answered briefly, while matters involving her own reading, choice or response may allow fuller, more concrete and even humorous answers. The quiet laugh around the title is not her "learning emotion for the first time", but an aesthetic response that already belongs to her.
- After returning to the capsule, A-line Xuyuan does not deliver an abbreviated thesis to prove that he has understood. He only needs to inspect the most recent pragmatic result: that person named a position, while Unknown has taken it as her own form of address. This is enough to generate the next question.

### Confirmed English Language Choices

- The English title is *Suppose the Grand Curator Loved Me*. `loved` primarily carries the modal distance created by the English hypothetical mood; it does not mean "she once loved me but no longer does". The title first places "she loves me" before the reader as a relational hypothesis; the main text then only explains why this question can be asked, and does not elevate the Grand Curator's existence, subjectivity or love into fact.
- The English subtitle is *Love in the Fourth Dimension*. Its old-fashioned science-fiction / thought-experiment tone creates a gap with the private hypothesis of the main title; `the Fourth Dimension` points to time inside a worldline, and does not claim that `τ` is a homogeneous fifth physical dimension.
- The three questions preserve `it` in English, and do not switch early to `the Curator` in the third question. `it` initially continues to point to the complete static structure; predicates such as "contain love", "stand before someone" and "bear the relationship" then gradually make demands of subjectivity on it. This makes subjectivity the question being tested by the third question, not a premise already supplied by the pronoun. The formal English needs to keep the antecedent stable, so that this intentional drift does not collapse into simple referential confusion.

## Things Not Completed in This Block

- Do not confirm that the work has guessed the novel's real cosmological structure correctly;
- Do not confirm that Unknown is the "Grand Curator" or the Grand Curator's only realisation;
- Do not confirm that she has already fallen in love with Xuyuan;
- Do not let Xuyuan automatically claim the `me` in the title as himself in either line A or line B;
- Do not let Unknown, the later Interface, the birth record or the scientists announce that the work has completely explained the real `τ`;
- Do not use `I have no position outside it from which to compare them.`. This sentence appears to preserve unknowability, but in practice it has already confirmed that she is contained by a larger structure and supplied a metaphysical explanation for why she cannot answer.
- Do not enter a complete methodological and ethical counter-interrogation immediately after the work ends; first let the birth record provide historical witness from the scientists' side, then complete the identity claim after returning to the capsule, preserving only a short hinge around the non-substitutability of the core enumeration structure, the existence of choice within the structure and the real cost of history.

The current birth record has the scientists confirm that a complete history is presented in one state, and that the later state preserves the reading and is therefore different, but it does not expand the observation into ultimate cosmology. The terminal AI herself does not enter the frame, glow, speak or act; the scientists describe her obliquely through "discovery", "perfect", failed archives, grief for the long road and a blessing that lets go, after which the presence lights go out one by one. Only `Was that you?` after returning to the capsule and the `Interface` label complete the first-person relational claim.
