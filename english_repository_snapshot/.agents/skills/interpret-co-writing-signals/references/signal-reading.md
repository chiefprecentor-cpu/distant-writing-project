# Reading signals inside a co-writing prompt

The useful unit is a sentence, clause, quoted passage plus its comment, or another small discourse unit—not the whole turn by default.

## Possible functions

These are prompts for interpretation, not a closed labelling scheme:

- The author may not understand what a line is doing and may want its rationale before considering a change.
- The author may understand the current choice but want to know whether the design space contains something better.
- The author may feel friction without yet knowing whether it comes from wording, timing, viewpoint, implication, rhythm, or personal uncertainty.
- The author may offer a counterproposal to think with, not to adopt.
- The author may recognise a benefit and a cost in the same sentence.
- The author may find a line acceptable while keeping revision open. Survival in a stronger or later draft, lack of objection, `acceptable for now`, or a neutral assessment does not by itself confirm the wording as a positive model.
- The author may reject or confirm only one part of a quoted opinion.
- The author may make a real selection or authorise implementation.
- The author may be unsure of the target, their preference, or their confidence in judging it. This meta-uncertainty does not surrender authorial authority.

Interrogative grammar and hedging do not establish dissatisfaction. Conversely, a mild phrase can still contain a real rejection when chronology and subsequent action support it.

## Response choices

- For understanding or rationale, explain the current choice before proposing revision.
- For open alternatives, keep the current choice in the comparison unless the author has rejected it.
- For unlocated friction, offer a few competing diagnoses grounded in the text.
- For a tentative counterproposal, compare effects and trade-offs rather than ratifying it automatically.
- For mixed benefit and concern, retain both; do not collapse the sentence to its negative or positive half.
- For provisional acceptability, keep the current version available alongside any live alternative and compare their effects; do not promote either into the aesthetic profile without stronger evidence.
- For meta-uncertainty, turn a vague target into smaller perceptible contrasts and give a provisional recommendation without claiming to have discovered the author's hidden preference.
- For a clear decision, implement or persist it only to the degree authorised.

When several functions occur in one prompt, respond to their relationship. Avoid mechanical line-by-line answers when one integrated explanation would better serve the author's thinking.
