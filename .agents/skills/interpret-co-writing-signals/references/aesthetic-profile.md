# Current aesthetic profile: distant writing

This is an early, partial profile derived mainly from one opening sequence. It is a set of working tendencies and productive tensions, not a finished style constitution. New writing may revise it.

## Repeated current tendencies

- The opening may be quiet without being uniform. Dialogue, narration, action, silence, sound, visual direction, and cuts can trade responsibility for movement.
- Explanations are judged partly by timing and ownership: an elegant sentence may arrive before a character or scene has earned its interpretation.
- Necessary meaning need not be reserved for expert close reading, yet an action or structure that already carries its effect need not be summarised again.
- Character voices may be uneven, technical, discursive, awkward, or nerdy. Naturalisation should not flatten a meaningful way of thinking.
- Ordinary narration, free indirect discourse, and observations from a fallible character concealed behind an apparently third-person, near-omniscient narrative surface may intentionally permeate one another. Local cues need only preserve the degree of orientation the scene actually requires; the reader need not always identify the source exactly.
- The concealed observer may misinterpret phenomena. A near-omniscient surface does not make its explanations infallible or equivalent to an author's verdict.

## Productive tensions

- Subtlety and first-read legibility can pull in different directions; neither is automatically superior.
- A joke or reference may be self-contained, context-supported, restricted to informed readers, deliberately private, or delayed until rereading. Its value depends on frequency, atmosphere, character relation, and what an uninformed reader loses.
- Montage carries a fragmentation cost, and the current preference leans towards sparse use. This project's unusual narration, repetition, and concealed-observer structure may make somewhat more montage productive; the surrounding sequence determines whether the gain is worth the cost.
- Long explanation can reveal thought in motion, crystallise it into a compelling self-account, or sound more settled and authoritative than the character's understanding has earned. Short dialogue can create energy, withhold, or leave expressive pressure unrealised.

## Local allowances currently supported

- Xuyuan may occupy longer, winding turns while a friend answers with shorter compressions.
- Some cultural references may remain opaque when opacity contributes to group texture, belated recognition, or a private layer of comedy.
- Presentation cues may remain in a prose draft if their channel is clear and the player will not mistake them for narration.

## Still under-observed

The available material is not sufficient to generalise confidently about later-worldline prose, action scenes, sustained tragedy, horror, exposition outside Xuyuan's viewpoint, choice writing, or the full range of other characters' voices.
