### Proposition One: The Author Mainly Controls Adoption Direction and Negative Boundaries; the Agent Mainly Controls How Problems Take Shape

**Mechanism.** The author often supplies sources, literary interests, state boundaries, things not to adopt and whether to continue / write, but does not specify every related file or design the conceptual architecture paragraph by paragraph. The Agent therefore gains discretion over semantic routing, problem construction, conceptual connection, sentence-level drafting, cross-file distribution and "when it is complete enough".

**Strongest evidence.** 011 was initially judged by the Agent as unnecessary to import. After the author reopened it with a literary goal, the Agent reconstructed it as an indexical feedback loop. The author supplied seeds such as "social engineering, performative sincerity and reverse profiling", and the Agent formed five kinds of density, recursive population mapping and trust mismatch. Conversely, the author continuously specified boundaries such as "do not test", "no worldline affiliation", "028 only as excluding reference", "do not map roles for Shizuo", and "do not include the provisional dialogue".

**Counterevidence and alternative explanation.** The author is not only responsible for approval. Many of the strongest relational seeds came directly from the author: the triple ambiguity of performative sincerity, the politics of model merging, recommendation-landscape comparison, the distinction between civilisation and species survival, and the hostile-script joke. The Agent is often clearly expressing or extending relations that potentially existed in the material, not provably creating them from nothing. Therefore "the Agent controls how problems take shape" does not mean "the Agent owns the core ideas", much less that the author loses decision-making power.

**Scope.** This distribution is observed in a context where the author intentionally delegated theorising prewriting and allowed the Agent actively to develop a line. It cannot be extended to concrete main-work prose, final scenes or unauthorised canon decisions.

### Proposition Two: The Main Check in This Collaboration Is Not Eliminating Agent Discretion in Advance, but Allowing the Author to Reopen Intermediate Judgements Repeatedly

**Mechanism.** The Agent first gives a highly structured proposal, and the author reopens an apparently completed judgement through felt omission, direction, tone or source concerns. The reversible status of candidate / development and ongoing file review keep rework cost acceptable. The author's control therefore appears more as `reopen / redirect / exclude` than as prior approval of every step.

**Strongest evidence.** The author successively reopened "011 does not need import" and "unnamable profiling should be central"; caught the four-theory reading being cross-wired into the previous task's final; required four disk originals rather than transcript; supplemented the *Laozi* concept group the Agent had omitted; corrected 025's original pseudo-Laplace-demon use; and rejected a provisional sentence that was accurate but had an "AI machine-like feeling". At these points, the Agent mostly withdrew closing judgements or narrowed wording, rather than resisting modification by invoking the reading, research or writing cost already spent.

**Counterevidence and alternative explanation.** Only deviations that the author noticed and raised can be confirmed as repaired. A single active development file can grow by more than a hundred lines in one turn, and the author is likely using semantic checkpoints and later file review rather than synchronous sentence-by-sentence checking. This can also be explained as ordinary iterative writing. It becomes agency negotiation because what gets reopened includes source authority, file state, conceptual centre and whether a task is complete, not just wording.

**Scope.** This check depends on the author still monitoring, remembering key intentions and being willing to correct. It cannot guarantee that unnoticed omissions, appealing but prematurely closed syntheses or low-salience expression drift will automatically surface.
